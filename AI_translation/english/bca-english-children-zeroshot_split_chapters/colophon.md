## Author's Colophon ^a-0

This book, the Way of the Bodhisattva, written by the master Śāntideva, is now complete. ^a-1

## Translators' Colophon ^b-0

This book was first translated into Tibetan by the Indian teacher Sarvajñādeva and the chief Tibetan translator Bande Paltsek, working from a manuscript found in Kashmir. ^b-1

Later, it was checked and improved by the Indian teacher Dharmaśrībhadra, together with the chief Tibetan translators Bande Rinchen Zangpo and Śākya-blo-gros, using a manuscript and commentary from Central India. ^b-2

Still later, it was corrected once more and carefully finalized by the Indian teacher Sumatikīrti, together with the chief Tibetan translator, the monk Blo ldan shes rab. ^b-3
