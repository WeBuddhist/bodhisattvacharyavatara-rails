# Day 15 — Chapter Two begins: offering everything beautiful

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Offer a beautiful thing you pass today
> **Detail:** Chapter 2 begins with offering. Today, when you pass something beautiful that no one owns, offer it in your mind.

## Opening

Chapter Two begins today. After all of Chapter One's praise of the awakening mind, Shantideva turns to a simple question: how do you hold on to it? His first answer is to start giving. These three verses open a long offering, beginning with everything beautiful in the world.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

We usually think we can only give away what belongs to us.

Today's verses go further. Shantideva offers flowers, jewels, whole mountains and forests, none of which are his to give. The commentary on this chapter explains why that still counts: **the offering is made in the mind**. What matters is not who owns the thing, but whether you can let it go freely. Picturing the world's beauty and giving it away trains a mind that enjoys things **without clinging** to them. That open, ungrasping mind is exactly what keeps the precious awakening mind from slipping away.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Sometime today you will pass something beautiful that belongs to no one: a flower, a clear sky, a tree moving in the wind. If you notice it, offer it in your mind to the buddhas. Nothing has to leave your hands.
