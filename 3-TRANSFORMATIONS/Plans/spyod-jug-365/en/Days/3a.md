# Day 3 — Bodhisattvacharyavatara Practice Plan

## Chapter 1: The Benefits of the Mind of Enlightenment — Verses 1-7 to 1-9

---

### 1. Refuge & Bodhicitta (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ་སེམས་བསྐྱེད།)

#### **1. Refuge (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ།)**

> *Until I reach the heart of awakening,*
> *I take refuge in the Buddhas.*
> *Likewise, I take refuge in the Dharma*
> *And in the assembly of Bodhisattvas.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྙིང་པོར་མཆིས་ཀྱི་བར། །
> སངས་རྒྱས་རྣམས་ལ་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །
> ཆོས་དང་བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་དཔའ་ཡི། །
> ཚོགས་ལའང་དེ་བཞིན་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །

---

### 2. Benefit (ཕན་ཡོན།)

Based on the traditional commentaries of Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, Sazang Mati Panchen, and Ngulchu Thokme Zangpo, practicing and reflecting on today's verses yields the following profound benefits:

*   **The Blissful Journey (Easy Path):** The great sages discovered that the mind of enlightenment leads to supreme bliss without severe hardships. This path allows countless beings to easily obtain temporary happiness and ultimate liberation.
*   **Fulfilling Noble Aspirations (Universal Welfare):** Generating this supreme mind is the only certain way to overcome personal suffering and dispel the sorrows of others. It fulfills all wishes for happiness and guides practitioners toward lasting peace.
*   **Instant Spiritual Elevation (Noble Status):** The moment you generate the mind of enlightenment, your name and status completely change. Even if you are bound by negative habits, you instantly become an honored heir of the Buddhas.

### 3. Today's Root Verses & Commentaries (དེ་རིང་གི་སྤྱོད་འཇུག་རྩ་ཚིག་དངོས།)

#### **Verse 1-7**
> བསྐལ་པ་དུ་མར་ཐུབ་ཆེན་རྣམས་ཀྱིས་ནི། །འདི་ཉིད་ཕན་པར་གཟིགས་ཏེ་བསམ་མཛད་པས། །
> འདིས་ནི་ཚད་མེད་སྐྱེ་བོའི་ཚོགས་རྣམས་ཀྱིས། །བདེ་མཆོག་བདེ་བླག་ཉིད་དུ་ཐོབ་པར་བྱེད། ། ^1-7
> 
> *The mighty sages — who contemplated this over many eons — saw that bodhicitta alone is truly beneficial. Through it, the immeasurable multitudes of beings easily and effortlessly attain the highest bliss.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-7**
The great teacher explain that the Buddhas spent countless eons searching for the best way to help others. They discovered that generating the mind of enlightenment is the only tool that truly benefits all wandering beings. Through this joyful path, infinite assemblies of beings can easily reach supreme awakening without undergoing painful hardships.

#### **Verse 1-8**
> སྲིད་པའི་སྡུག་བསྔལ་བརྒྱ་ཕྲག་གཞོམ་འདོད་ཅིང་། །སེམས་ཅན་མི་བདེ་བསལ་བར་འདོད་པ་དང་། །
> བདེ་མང་བརྒྱ་ཕྲག་སྤྱོད་པར་འདོད་པས་ཀྱང་། །བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་ཉིད་རྟག་ཏུ་གཏང་མི་བྱ། ། ^1-8
> 
> *Those who wish to overcome the hundreds of sufferings of existence, who wish to remove the misery of sentient beings, and who wish to experience the many hundreds of joys — let them never abandon bodhicitta.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-8**
This verse outlines three supreme motivations for spiritual practice. These goals are overcoming personal suffering, removing others' misery, and enjoying limitless happiness. Since the mind of enlightenment is the sole method to accomplish these aims, practitioners must never abandon it.

#### **Verse 1-9**
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་སྐྱེས་གྱུར་ན་སྐད་ཅིག་གིས། །འཁོར་བའི་བཙོན་རར་བསྡམས་པའི་ཉམ་ཐག་རྣམས། །
> བདེ་གཤེགས་རྣམས་ཀྱི་སྲས་ཞེས་བརྗོད་བྱ་ཞིང་། །འཇིག་རྟེན་ལྷ་མིར་བཅས་པས་ཕྱག་བྱར་འགྱུར། ། ^1-9
> 
> *The moment bodhicitta is born, even those wretched ones bound in the prison of samsara are called sons and daughters of the Sugatas, and become worthy of veneration by gods and humans alike.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-9**
It highlight the immediate dual transformation of name and status that occurs when compassion arises. Even wretched beings bound in the prison of worldly existence instantly become honored heirs of the Buddhas. This elevation makes them worthy of prostrations and offerings from both humans and worldly gods.

### 4. Daily Life Application (ཉིན་རེའི་འཚོ་བའི་ནང་ཉམས་སུ་ལེན་ཚུལ།)

*   **1. Choosing the Path of Joy:** Today, I will reflect on the Buddhas' discovery that the mind of enlightenment is the easiest path to peace. When faced with difficult choices, I will choose to act with kindness instead of relying on stressful struggles.
*   **2. Committing to Universal Welfare:** I will commit to never abandoning the wish to benefit others, especially when my own problems feel overwhelming. I will actively focus on relieving some other person's stress today to help overcome my own self-centered worries.
*   **3. Recognizing My Inner Potential:** The moment I generate a kind intention today, I will remember that my spiritual status is instantly elevated. I will treat myself and others with dignity, knowing that we all possess the seed of a Buddha.

### 5. Dedication & Aspiration (བསྔོ་བ་དང་སྨོན་ལམ།)

#### **1. Dedication (བསྔོ་བ།)**

> *By the virtue accumulated*
> *Through composing this entry into the Bodhisattva conduct,*
> *May all wandering beings without exception*
> *Engage in the Bodhisattva conduct.*

> བདག་གིས་བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་པ་ལ། །
> འཇུག་པ་རྣམ་པར་བརྩམས་པ་ཡི། །
> དགེ་བ་གང་དེས་འགྲོ་བ་ཀུན། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་ལ་འཇུག་པར་ཤོག །

#### **2. Aspiration (སྨོན་ལམ།)**

> *May the precious and supreme mind of awakening,*
> *Where it has not arisen, arise;*
> *Where it has arisen, may it not decline,*
> *But increase further and further.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་མཆོག་རིན་པོ་ཆེ། །
> མ་སྐྱེས་པ་རྣམས་སྐྱེ་གྱུར་ཅིག །
> སྐྱེས་པ་ཉམས་པ་མེད་པ་དང་། །
> གོང་ནས་གོང་དུ་འཕེལ་བར་ཤོག །

### 6. Key Terms Glossary (གནད་ཚིག་ཁག་གི་འགྲེལ་བཤད།)

*   **Supreme Bliss (བདེ་མཆོག):** This term refers to the ultimate state of perfect awakening and liberation. Commentators explain that this happiness is achieved easily and without hardships through the power of the mind of enlightenment. Unlike worldly pleasure, this bliss is permanent and free from all forms of suffering.
*   **Mind of Enlightenment (བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས):** This term represents the compassionate aspiration to attain full Buddhahood for the benefit of all living beings. The commentaries emphasize that this mind is the single method to fulfill all noble wishes. Practitioners must guard this precious attitude and never abandon it under any circumstances.
*   **Prison of Cyclic Existence (འཁོར་བའི་བཙོན་རྭ།):** This term describes the painful cycle of uncontrolled rebirth driven by negative actions and mental afflictions. Worldly beings are bound in this prison like helpless captives. However, generating a compassionate mind instantly breaks these chains and elevates their spiritual status.
