# Day 25 — Lower your guard three times

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Bring your palms together and ask for help
> **Detail:** Today's verses bow, take refuge, and supplicate. Make one honest request with open hands.

## Opening

Each of today's three verses lowers the guard we keep up around ourselves. Shantideva bows to the teachers and sacred things that helped him get here. He takes refuge, entrusting himself to the buddhas, the dharma, and the sangha until awakening. Then he joins his palms before the buddhas and bodhisattvas of every direction and asks for their help. Three steps, each one a small letting go of self-importance.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

The bow comes first, to the teachers and sacred things that carry the teaching. Then refuge, and here Ngulchu Thokme, a fourteenth-century teacher, draws a clear line. Refuge in the Three Jewels is not a quick rescue grabbed in a moment of fear. It starts from compassion, from wanting to become someone who can help every being, and it lasts until awakening, not just until the fear has passed. Last, Shantideva joins his palms before the buddhas and bodhisattvas of great compassion. Ngulchu reads this as the opening of confession: you turn to those who will not look away, and that makes it safe to admit what you have done.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Bring one teacher who changed something for you to mind, and thank them; it is hard to feel above someone you are thanking.
