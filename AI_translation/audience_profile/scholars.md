# Audience Profile: Scholars' Version

## Audience

Academic readers: Buddhologists, Indologists, Tibetologists, and advanced students already familiar with Buddhist philosophical vocabulary and Sanskrit/Tibetan technical terms.

---

## Translation Goal

Produce a precise, source-faithful rendering suitable for scholarly citation and comparison with the Sanskrit/Tibetan. Fidelity to the source's syntax, technical terminology, and argument structure takes precedence over ease of reading.

---

## Style

* Formal, academic register
* Technical Buddhist terms retained in transliteration at first occurrence, with the target-language gloss (e.g. *bodhicitta* (बोधिचित्त))
* Literal, close rendering of syntax over idiomatic paraphrase
* No simplification of philosophical distinctions (e.g. aspirational vs. engaged bodhicitta kept distinct, not collapsed)
* Sanskrit/Tibetan terms transliterated using standard scholarly convention (IAST for Sanskrit, Wylie for Tibetan) when cited

---

## Explanatory Additions

None. No clarifying words or paraphrase beyond what the source states. Where the source is genuinely ambiguous, preserve the ambiguity rather than resolving it silently.

---

## Priority

1. Accuracy
2. Terminological precision / consistency
3. Fidelity to source syntax and structure
4. Readability
