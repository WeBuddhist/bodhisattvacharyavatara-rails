## Today's Verse

> First, with your own wisdom,
> separate this layer of skin.
> Then, with the weapon of wisdom,
> separate the flesh from the network of bones. ^5-62

> Having separated the bones,
> look into the marrow.
> "What essence is there in this?"
> You yourself should examine. ^5-63

> If, after striving and searching,
> you find no essence there,
> then why, with attachment,
> do you guard this body? ^5-64

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the sixty-second, sixty-third, and sixty-fourth verses of the fifth chapter. If we cut open our own skin, flesh, and bones step by step with the sharp knife of wisdom, is there any hope of finding anything inside in the end?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following key point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. If we think this body has something clean and pleasing, we first separate the seven outer layers of skin with our own mind, one by one. We check whether anything clean, pleasing, or valuable is there. Next, we use the weapon of wisdom to set the flesh and sinews inside the skin to one side, separating them one by one, from among the three hundred and sixty pieces of bone. Again we check whether anything clean, pleasing, or valuable is there.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will examine what essence there is in my own body.

**Explanation:** _(Wisdom Practice)_ If we know there is no essence inside the body, attachment grows less. This frees our own mind from confusion. These words teach us that we must examine the nature of the body again and again.
