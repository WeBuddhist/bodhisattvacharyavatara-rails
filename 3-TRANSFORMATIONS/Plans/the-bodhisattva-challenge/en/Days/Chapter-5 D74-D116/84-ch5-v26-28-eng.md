## Today's Verse

> Many learned, faithful,
> and diligent individuals,
> marred by the fault of lacking awareness,
> fall into downfalls. ^5-26

> Robbers of unawareness,
> following the loss of mindfulness,
> even if merit is accumulated,
> they go to lower realms as if stripped by thieves. ^5-27

> This multitude of troubling emotions, like robbers,
> seeks an opening.
> Once an opening is found, they steal good deeds
> and destroy the life of happy realms. ^5-28

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the twenty-sixth, twenty-seventh, and twenty-eighth verses of the fifth chapter. Just as a thief watches for a chance to break into a storehouse and steal its treasures, the moment our guard of mindfulness and awareness relaxes, the thieves of the afflictions suddenly break in.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us an example that is easy to understand and encouraging.

For example, a thief who plans to steal wealth from a rich storehouse will scout around. They watch where its owner goes and where they stay, looking for the moment to break into that great storehouse. In the same way, the moment our antidote loses even a little of its strength, without our noticing, the afflictions immediately look for their chance.

When afflictions like attachment and hatred find their chance and enter our mind stream, they steal away the wealth of our good deeds. In particular, if even a single instance of anger arises, all the roots of goodness gathered over a long time can be completely destroyed.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, the moment I notice my mindfulness and awareness slipping, or the afflictions watching for a chance, I will restore mindfulness at once and guard my mind.

**Explanation:** _(Ethics Practice)_ Because the thieves of the afflictions are always looking for a chance to harm us, even a small relaxing of the guard of mindfulness and awareness lets them steal away our roots of goodness. So the very moment we notice mindfulness and awareness slipping, we must pay close attention to our own mind. This blocks the afflictions' chance and protects the very life-root of our good deeds. This practice keeps the good deeds in our own mind stream from being lost, and it brings great benefit for eventually reaching happy realms and the path to liberation.
