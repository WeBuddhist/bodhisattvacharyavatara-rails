# Day 2 — Bodhisattvacharyavatara Practice Plan

## Chapter 1: The Benefits of the Mind of Enlightenment — Verses 1-4 to 1-6

---

### 1. Refuge & Bodhicitta (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ་སེམས་བསྐྱེད།)

#### **1. Refuge (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ།)**

> *Until I reach the heart of awakening,*
> *I take refuge in the Buddhas.*
> *Likewise, I take refuge in the Dharma*
> *And in the assembly of Bodhisattvas.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྙིང་པོར་མཆིས་ཀྱི་བར། །
> སངས་རྒྱས་རྣམས་ལ་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །
> ཆོས་དང་བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་དཔའ་ཡི། །
> ཚོགས་ལའང་དེ་བཞིན་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །

---

### 2. Benefit (ཕན་ཡོན།)

Based on the traditional commentaries of Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, Sazang Mati Panchen, and Ngulchu Thokme Zangpo, practicing and reflecting on today's verses yields the following profound benefits:

*   **Extracting the Essence (Precious Life):** This rare human life offers a unique opportunity to accomplish lasting spiritual goals. One must utilize this favorable situation to cultivate goodness before this precious support is lost. Failing to practice virtue now makes finding such an opportunity in the future nearly impossible.
*   **Recognizing the Source of Virtue (Divine Inspiration):** Positive thoughts are extremely brief and rare, like a sudden flash of lightning on a pitch-black night. These wholesome impulses do not arise from personal strength alone. Instead, the profound compassion and blessings of the Buddhas inspire every virtuous aspiration.
*   **Embracing the Ultimate Remedy (Overcoming Negativity):** Ordinary virtues are perpetually weak, whereas negative habits are incredibly powerful and continuous. No ordinary positive action has the strength to conquer such deep negativity. Only the perfect mind of enlightenment possesses the immense power required to completely purify and overcome these obstacles.

### 3. Today's Root Verses & Commentaries (དེ་རིང་གི་སྤྱོད་འཇུག་རྩ་ཚིག་དངོས།)

#### **Verse 1-4**
> དལ་འབྱོར་འདི་ནི་རྙེད་པར་ཤིན་ཏུ་དཀའ། །
> སྐྱེས་བུའི་དོན་སྒྲུབ་ཐོབ་པར་གྱུར་པ་ལ། །
> གལ་ཏེ་འདི་ལ་ཕན་པ་མ་བསྒྲུབས་ན། །
> ཕྱིས་འདི་ཡང་དག་འབྱོར་པར་ག་ལ་འགྱུར། ། ^1-4
> 
> *This precious human life of leisure and endowment is extremely difficult to obtain. Now that one has the chance to accomplish the purpose of a being, if one does not make use of it for benefit, how will such a perfect opportunity arise again?*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-4**
The great teacher Śāntideva urges practitioners to realize the immense value of this precious human life. This precious human life of eighteen freedoms and endowments is extremely rare and difficult to obtain. It serves as the essential support for achieving high rebirth and ultimate liberation. One must make great efforts to practice the sacred teachings now to avoid wasting this perfect opportunity.

#### **Verse 1-5**
> ཇི་ལྟར་མཚན་མོ་མུན་ནག་སྤྲིན་རུམ་ན། །
> གློག་འགྱུ་སྐད་ཅིག་རབ་སྣང་སྟོན་པ་ལྟར། །
> དེ་བཞིན་སངས་རྒྱས་མཐུ་ཡིས་བརྒྱ་ལམ་ན། །
> འཇིག་རྟེན་བསོད་ནམས་བློ་གྲོས་ཐང་འགའ་འབྱུང་། ། ^1-5
> 
> *Just as a flash of lightning on a dark, cloud-covered night illuminates all for one brilliant instant, so too, by the power of the Buddhas, the mind of virtue and wisdom arises but rarely in the world.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-5**
The great teacher Śāntideva uses the metaphor of a sudden lightning flash to illustrate the brevity of virtuous thoughts. A flash of lightning briefly illuminates the dark night, just as virtuous thoughts occasionally arise in a confused mind. This momentary impulse toward goodness does not come from personal strength. It is inspired solely by the blessings and compassion of the Buddhas.

#### **Verse 1-6**
> དེ་ལྟས་དགེ་བ་ཉམ་ཆུང་ཉིད་ལ་རྟག །
> སྡིག་པ་སྟོབས་ཆེན་ཤིན་ཏུ་མི་བཟད་པ། །
> དེ་ནི་རྫོགས་པའི་བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་མིན་པ། །
> དགེ་གཞན་གང་གིས་ཟིལ་གྱིས་གནོན་པར་འགྱུར། ། ^1-6
> 
> *Therefore, virtue is always meager, while the power of negativity is immense and unbearable. Except for perfect bodhicitta, what other virtue can overcome it?*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-6**
The great teacher Śāntideva contrasts weak ordinary virtue with the overwhelming power of negativity. Ordinary virtues are always weak and transient, while negative actions are immensely powerful and continuous. No ordinary positive deed can conquer this overwhelming dark force. Only the precious mind of enlightenment can completely overcome and purify such deep negativity.

### 4. Daily Life Application (ཉིན་རེའི་འཚོ་བའི་ནང་ཉམས་སུ་ལེན་ཚུལ།)

*   **1. Appreciating My Precious Opportunity:** Today, I will reflect on the immense rarity of my human life with all its freedoms. I will consciously choose to use my time for meaningful actions instead of wasting it on trivial distractions.
*   **2. Guarding My Virtuous Thoughts:** When a kind or helpful thought arises in my mind today, I will recognize it as a rare blessing. I will act on it immediately before it is lost in the darkness of my daily worries.
*   **3. Relying on the Mind of Enlightenment:** Today, I will cultivate the wish to help all living beings to overcome my negative habits. I will rely on this compassionate attitude as the ultimate strength to conquer my anger and selfishness.

### 5. Dedication & Aspiration (བསྔོ་བ་དང་སྨོན་ལམ།)

#### **1. Dedication (བསྔོ་བ།)**

> *By the virtue accumulated*
> *Through composing this entry into the Bodhisattva conduct,*
> *May all wandering beings without exception*
> *Engage in the Bodhisattva conduct.*

> བདག་གིས་བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་པ་ལ། །
> འཇུག་པ་རྣམ་པར་བརྩམས་པ་ཡི། །
> དགེ་བ་གང་དེས་འགྲོ་བ་ཀུན། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་ལ་འཇུག་པར་ཤོག །

#### **2. Aspiration (སྨོན་ལམ།)**

> *May the precious and supreme mind of awakening,*
> *Where it has not arisen, arise;*
> *Where it has arisen, may it not decline,*
> *But increase further and further.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་མཆོག་རིན་པོ་ཆེ། །
> མ་སྐྱེས་པ་རྣམས་སྐྱེ་གྱུར་ཅིག །
> སྐྱེས་པ་ཉམས་པ་མེད་པ་དང་། །
> གོང་ནས་གོང་དུ་འཕེལ་བར་ཤོག །

### 6. Key Terms Glossary (གནད་ཚིག་ཁག་གི་འགྲེལ་བཤད།)

*   **Freedoms and Endowments (དལ་འབྱོར།):** This term refers to the eighteen special qualities of a precious human life. It includes eight freedoms from unfavorable states and ten positive conditions that make spiritual practice possible. Having these qualities allows a person to achieve lasting happiness and ultimate peace.
 *   **The Purpose of a Person (སྐྱེས་བུའི་དོན།):** This term describes the meaningful goals that a human being can accomplish. These goals are divided into temporary happiness in higher realms and the ultimate bliss of complete liberation. Cultivating virtue is the only way to fulfill these noble aspirations.
*   **Virtuous Mind (བསོད་ནམས/དགེ་བའི་བློ་གྲོས།):** This term represents a mind inclined toward positive actions and spiritual growth. The commentaries explain that such a mind is exceptionally rare and weak in ordinary beings. It arises only occasionally through the powerful blessings and compassion of the Buddhas.
