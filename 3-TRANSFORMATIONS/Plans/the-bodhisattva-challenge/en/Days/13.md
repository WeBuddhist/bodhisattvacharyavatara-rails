# Day 13 — A moment of admiration counts for more than you think

## Opening

Think of someone you truly look up to. A teacher, maybe. Or someone whose kindness you still remember.

For a moment, you feel it. Real respect.

Today's verses look at two feelings we can hold toward such a person. And what each one does to us.

## From the Tradition

*A note from the teachers who explained this text.*

Today's verses set two thoughts side by side. A hateful thought toward a good person, and a warm one. You might expect them to weigh the same. They do not. The warm one weighs far more.

**Ngulchu Thokme** makes this real with a picture. Imagine someone gives sight back to every blind person in the world. A vast kindness. Now imagine someone else simply looks at a kind, good-hearted person with real warmth. That quiet look, he says, brings even more good than giving back all that sight.

So the good you feel does not need to be grand. **Sazang Mati Panchen** adds that one moment of true respect outweighs a moment of anger.

Hold this today: **a warm thought is never small.**

## Today's Practice Challenge

**Your task today:**

Bring to mind one person whose goodness you respect. A teacher, a friend, anyone kind.

For a few seconds, **let yourself truly admire them.** Feel the warmth, and do not rush past it.
