## Combined Commentary Summaries

Continuing directly from the previous verse, this stanza sharpens the argument: no one — no being at all — has ever granted anyone freedom from death, so there is no way to be certain of escaping it, making present complacency unreasonable.

All three commentators treat this verse as completing the single argument begun in verse 58. Sazang phrases the point sharply: "What person has granted me freedom from the fear of death? No one at all has granted this." Gyaltsab draws the practical conclusion that one should therefore strive at meditating on the path rather than remain under the sway of laziness.
