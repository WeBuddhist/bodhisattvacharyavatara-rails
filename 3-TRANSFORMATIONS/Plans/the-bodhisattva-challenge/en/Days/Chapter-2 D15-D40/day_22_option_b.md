# Day 22 — Be a little shade for someone

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Be shelter for one person having a hard day
> **Detail:** Shantideva offers parasols to the buddhas. Today, be a bit of shade for someone in the heat.

## Opening

Today's offering is a parasol: a beautiful jewelled one with a golden handle, raised high and held steady over the buddhas and bodhisattvas. Of all the offerings, this is the plainest in purpose. A parasol exists for one reason, to put cool shade between someone and the burning sun.

## From the Tradition

We tend to wait until we can fix someone's whole problem before we step in, and so we often do nothing.

The verse offers a parasol, and Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, a fifteenth-century teacher, keeps the picture simple: a fine canopy with a golden handle, raised and held over the one beneath it. A parasol does not solve the day; it just makes the heat bearable for a while. That is the point worth carrying. You may not be able to lift another person's hardship, but you can hold a little shade over them, take one task off their hands, stay calm when they cannot, sit with them so the heat is not faced alone. The relief lands on them, and the habit of being shelter grows in you.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

If you notice someone today having a hard time, offer them a small patch of shade. Take one thing off their plate, keep your own calm when theirs is fraying, or simply stay near so they are not in it alone. You do not have to fix the heat, only soften it a little for them.
