## Today's Verse

> If you harbor the dart of hatred in your mind,
> your mind will never experience peace.
> You will not attain joy or happiness,
> and you will not sleep; your mind will be unstable. ^6-3

> Those who have been helped
> by a master who gave them wealth and respect,
> even they will set upon that master
> if he is filled with hatred. ^6-4

> Because of this, friends become disheartened,
> and even if won over by generosity, they will not stay.
> In short, no one who is angry
> can ever dwell in peace. ^6-5

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the third, fourth, and fifth verses of the sixth chapter. The dart of hatred steals even our sleep and drives our friends far away. So today, we calm the anger in our own mind.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable example, which is easy to understand and encouraging. If a person hears unpleasant words while eating tasty food and becomes angry, the taste of the food is lost at once. The peace and ease of the mind disappear in a moment, and discomfort comes. This example shows that as soon as anger arises, the peace of the mind disappears and discomfort comes.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will not let hatred pierce my mind. I will give up anger.

**Explanation:** _(Patience Practice)_ If we leave hatred stuck in the mind like a dart, our mind does not find peace or joy. We cannot sleep, and hatred burns our body and mind. Therefore, when we give up hatred, our own mind becomes peaceful.
