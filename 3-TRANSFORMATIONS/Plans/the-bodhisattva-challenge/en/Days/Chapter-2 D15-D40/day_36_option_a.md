# Day 36 — Treat it like the illness it is

> **Notification**
> **Title:** When a feeling flares today, name it as the illness, not as you
> **Detail:** Today's verses call attachment and anger a disease. The first cure is seeing it as one.

## Opening

The tone changes today. After the fear of the last days, Shantideva offers a calm, almost medical way of seeing things. We take an ordinary illness seriously and do what the doctor says. He asks why we do not treat the mind the same way, when attachment, anger, and the rest are a chronic illness that has troubled us far longer than any fever. The good news inside that is simple: a disease can be treated.

## From the Tradition

Ngulchu Thokme, a fourteenth-century teacher, holds the comparison up plainly. When a real illness frightens us, we go to the doctor and do what we are told, without arguing. Yet the afflictions of the mind, attachment, anger, the hundred faults that come with them, we tend to wave off as just our personality. He points out that this is the harder disease, not the easier one, partly because it has been with us so long that we no longer notice it as something separate from ourselves. That is exactly the shift the verse asks for. The first step in any treatment is recognising that you are unwell, that the flare of irritation or craving is the illness acting up, not simply who you are. Once you can see it as a condition rather than your identity, a small space opens, and in that space something can actually be done.

## Today's Practice

Once today, when a strong feeling flares up, anger, craving, a spike of anxiety, try naming it quietly to yourself: "this is the affliction acting up," rather than "this is just me." You do not have to fight it or fix it in that moment. Just feel the small gap that opens when you see it as a passing condition, the way you would a symptom, rather than the whole truth of who you are.
