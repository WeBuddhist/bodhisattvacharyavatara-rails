# Day 3 — Bodhisattvacharyavatara Practice Plan

## Chapter 1: The Benefits of the Mind of Enlightenment — Verses 1-7 to 1-9

---

### 1. Refuge & Bodhicitta (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ་སེམས་བསྐྱེད།)

#### **1. Refuge (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ།)**

> *Until I reach the heart of awakening,*
> *I take refuge in the Buddhas.*
> *Likewise, I take refuge in the Dharma*
> *And in the assembly of Bodhisattvas.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྙིང་པོར་མཆིས་ཀྱི་བར། །
> སངས་རྒྱས་རྣམས་ལ་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །
> ཆོས་དང་བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་དཔའ་ཡི། །
> ཚོགས་ལའང་དེ་བཞིན་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །

---

### 2. Benefit (ཕན་ཡོན།)

Based on the traditional commentaries of Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, Sazang Mati Panchen, and Ngulchu Thokme Zangpo, practicing and reflecting on today's verses yields the following profound benefits:

*   **Effortless Supreme Joy (The Sages' Discovery):** By contemplating the mind of Enlightenment, you can enter a joyful path to liberation. The Buddhas spent eons searching for the best way to help others and found this supreme method. It allows you to reach perfect awakening easily without relying on harsh physical penances.
*   **Fulfillment of All Aspirations (The Triple Wish):** Reflecting on these teachings helps you accomplish your deepest spiritual goals. This practice provides the exact tools needed to end your own pain and relieve the suffering of others. It secures both temporary happiness in happy migrations and the ultimate bliss of complete freedom.
*   **Instant Spiritual Elevation (Transformation of Status):** Developing this noble motivation instantly elevates your spiritual status. Though you are currently bound by negative habits, you immediately become a child of the Buddhas. This transformation makes your mind a worthy object of respect for all worldly beings.

### 3. Today's Root Verses & Commentaries (དེ་རིང་གི་སྤྱོད་འཇུག་རྩ་ཚིག་དངོས།)

#### **Verse 1-7**
> བསྐལ་པ་དུ་མར་རབ་དགོངས་མཛད་པ་ཡི། །ཐུབ་དབང་རྣམས་ཀྱིས་འདི་ཉིད་ཕན་པར་གཟིགས། །
> འདིས་ནི་ཚད་མེད་སྐྱེ་བོའི་ཚོགས་རྣམས་ཀྱིས། །བདེ་མཆོག་བདེ་བླག་ཉིད་དུ་ཐོབ་པར་བྱེད། ། ^1-7
> 
> *The mighty sages — who contemplated this over many eons — saw that bodhicitta alone is truly beneficial. Through it, the immeasurable multitudes of beings easily and effortlessly attain the highest bliss.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-7**
The great teacher Śāntideva explains that the Buddhas spent many countless eons analyzing the best ways to help all beings. Through their deep wisdom, they discovered that the mind of Enlightenment is the ultimate source of welfare. This precious mind allows countless wandering beings to easily attain supreme happiness without experiencing severe physical hardships.

#### **Verse 1-8**
> སྲིད་པའི་སྡུག་བསྔལ་བརྒྱ་ཕྲག་གཞོམ་འདོད་ཅིང་། །སེམས་ཅན་མི་བདེ་བསལ་བར་འདོད་པ་དང་། །
> བདེ་མང་བརྒྱ་ཕྲག་སྤྱོད་པར་འདོད་པས་ཀྱང་། །བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་ཉིད་རྟག་ཏུ་གཏང་མི་བྱ། ། ^1-8
> 
> *Those who wish to overcome the hundreds of sufferings of existence, who wish to remove the misery of sentient beings, and who wish to experience the many hundreds of joys — let them never abandon bodhicitta.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-8**
The great teacher Śāntideva outlines three supreme wishes that every practitioner should cultivate in their spiritual practice. You must wish to overcome your own suffering, remove the misery of others, and experience ultimate happiness. Because the mind of Enlightenment is the only way to accomplish these goals, you should never abandon it.

#### **Verse 1-9**
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་སྐྱེས་གྱུར་ན་སྐད་ཅིག་གིས། །འཁོར་བའི་བཙོན་རར་བསྡམས་པའི་ཉམ་ཐག་རྣམས། །
> བདེ་གཤེགས་རྣམས་ཀྱི་སྲས་ཞེས་བརྗོད་བྱ་ཞིང་། །འཇིག་རྟེན་ལྷ་མིར་བཅས་པས་ཕྱག་བྱར་འགྱུར། ། ^1-9
> 
> *The moment bodhicitta is born, even those wretched ones bound in the prison of samsara are called sons and daughters of the Sugatas, and become worthy of veneration by gods and humans alike.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-9**
The great teacher Śāntideva reveals that generating the mind of Enlightenment instantly transforms your name and spiritual status. The very moment you generate this mind, you become a beloved child of the Blissful Ones. Even if you are bound in the prison of cyclic existence, gods and humans will immediately respect you.

### 4. Daily Life Application (ཉིན་རེའི་འཚོ་བའི་ནང་ཉམས་སུ་ལེན་ཚུལ།)

*   **1. Choosing the Joyful Path of Love:** Today, when I feel overwhelmed by spiritual duties, I will remember that the great teacher Śāntideva offers a joyful path. Instead of forcing myself through harsh routines, I will focus on cultivating a warm attitude toward those around me. This simple shift will make my practice feel lighter and more natural.
*   **2. Reaffirming My Triple Commitment:** Whenever I face personal difficulties today, I will intentionally pause to renew my three core spiritual wishes. I will commit to overcoming my own negative habits, helping to remove others' pain, and bringing happiness to everyone. This triple commitment will keep my mind focused on what truly matters.
*   **3. Recognizing My Noble Potential:** When I feel discouraged or trapped by my daily worries today, I will remind myself of my noble potential. I will remember that generating the wish to benefit others instantly elevates my mind. By seeing myself as a child of the Buddhas, I can rise above my current limitations.

### 5. Dedication & Aspiration (བསྔོ་བ་དང་སྨོན་ལམ།)

#### **1. Dedication (བསྔོ་བ།)**

> *By the virtue accumulated*
> *Through composing this entry into the Bodhisattva conduct,*
> *May all wandering beings without exception*
> *Engage in the Bodhisattva conduct.*

> བདག་གིས་བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་པ་ལ། །
> འཇུག་པ་རྣམ་པར་བརྩམས་པ་ཡི། །
> དགེ་བ་གང་དེས་འགྲོ་བ་ཀུན། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་ལ་འཇུག་པར་ཤོག །

#### **2. Aspiration (སྨོན་ལམ།)**

> *May the precious and supreme mind of awakening,*
> *Where it has not arisen, arise;*
> *May it not decline where it has arisen,*
> *But increase further and further.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་མཆོག་རིན་པོ་ཆེ། །
> མ་སྐྱེས་པ་རྣམས་སྐྱེ་གྱུར་ཅིག །
> སྐྱེས་པ་ཉམས་པ་མེད་པ་དང་། །
> གོང་ནས་གོང་དུ་འཕེལ་བར་ཤོག །

### 6. Key Terms Glossary (གནད་ཚིག་ཁག་གི་འགྲེལ་བཤད།)

*   **Mighty Sage (ཐུབ་དབང་།):** This term refers to the Buddha, who has conquered all mental afflictions and holds supreme mastery over his mind. Commentators explain that these enlightened beings spent countless eons investigating how to bring ultimate happiness to all wandering beings. They are the supreme guides who discovered the precious mind of Enlightenment.
*   **Sufferings of Cyclic Existence (སྲིད་པའི་སྡུག་བསྔལ།):** This term describes the hundreds of physical and mental miseries experienced by beings trapped in the cycle of rebirth. Commentators explain that these sufferings include the pain of birth, sickness, aging, and death. Reflecting on these miseries inspires a strong wish to achieve complete liberation and help others do the same.
*   **Child of the Blissful Ones (བདེ་གཤེགས་རྣམས་ཀྱི་སྲས།):** This term designates a practitioner who has generated the genuine mind of Enlightenment. Commentators emphasize that the very instant this noble motivation is born, a person's name and status are completely transformed. Even if they are bound in cyclic existence, they are immediately recognized as spiritual heirs of the Buddhas.
