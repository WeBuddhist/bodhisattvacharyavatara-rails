# Day 5 — The fire that burns even the worst you've done

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Turn an old regret into a wish for others
> **Detail:** This mind protects you, and can burn the worst away. Today, turn one regret into a wish.

## Opening

Today's three verses show what this wish can do. Most kind acts pay off once and then fade. This wish keeps growing instead. It can save even someone who has done great harm, like a brave friend pulling you out of danger. And at its strongest, it can burn away the worst in a moment, like fire.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## Today's Verses

> དགེ་བ་གཞན་ཀུན་ཆུ་ཤིང་བཞིན་དུ་ནི། །
> འབྲས་བུ་བསྐྱེད་ནས་ཟད་པར་འགྱུར་བ་ཉིད། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་ཀྱི་ལྗོན་ཤིང་རྟག་པར་ཡང་། །
> འབྲས་བུ་འབྱིན་པས་མི་ཟད་འཕེལ་བར་འགྱུར། །
>
> All other virtues are like a banana tree that dies after bearing fruit once. In contrast, the tree of the mind of enlightenment continuously bears fruit and always grows.

> སྡིག་པ་ཤིན་ཏུ་མི་བཟད་བྱས་ན་ཡང་། །
> དཔའ་ལ་བརྟེན་ནས་འཇིགས་པ་ཆེན་པོ་ལྟར། །
> གང་ལ་བརྟེན་ནས་ཡུད་ཀྱིས་སྒྲོལ་འགྱུར་བ། །
> དེ་ལ་བག་ཅན་རྣམས་ཀྱིས་ཅིས་མི་བརྟེན། །
>
> Even if someone has committed terrible misdeeds, relying on this mind will swiftly liberate them. This is like relying on a brave companion to escape great danger. Why then do careful people not rely on it?

> དེས་ནི་དུས་མཐའི་མེ་བཞིན་སྡིག་ཆེན་རྣམས། །
> སྐད་ཅིག་གཅིག་གིས་ངེས་པར་སྲེག་པར་བྱེད། །
> དེ་ཡི་ཕན་ཡོན་དཔག་ཏུ་མེད་པ་དག །
> བྱམས་མགོན་བློ་དང་ལྡན་པས་ནོར་བཟང་བཤད། །
>
> In a single instant, this mind consumes even the greatest sins, like the fire at the end of an age. Its immeasurable benefits were explained by Maitreya, the Loving Protector, to Sudhana.

## From the Tradition

**Ngulchu Thokme**, a fourteenth-century teacher, offers a word of comfort here. We often think some things we have done are too bad to fix. The verse says they are not. Even the worst can be cleared away, and quickly.

So if you are carrying something heavy from the past, you are not beyond help. Hold on to this wish, and it will carry you through.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Think of one thing you still feel bad about. Don't dwell on it. Just turn it into a wish: may everyone who carries a regret like this find their way through.
