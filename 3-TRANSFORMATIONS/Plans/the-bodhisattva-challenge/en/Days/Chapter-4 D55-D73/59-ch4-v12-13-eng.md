## Today's Verse

> Therefore, just as I have vowed,
> I will respectfully accomplish it.
> If I do not strive from now on,
> I will go lower and lower. ^4-12

> Countless buddhas, who benefit all beings,
> have passed.
> But I, due to my own faults,
> was not within the sphere of their healing. ^4-13

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the two verses 12 and 13 from the fourth chapter of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. Many buddhas have come in the past, but if we are not ready on our own side it does us no good. So from today onwards we have to make the effort ourselves.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us a comparison that is easy to understand and encouraging.

His Holiness the Dalai Lama has said that the holy Dharma is a means of taming our own mind. It is not something brought about by outer supports: images, texts and stupas and the like.

Jetsun Milarepa had no thangkas and no statues of gold or copper. He went about in the manner of a poor beggar, and yet he reached the level of a buddha in a single life. In the same way, Khedrub Norzang Gyatso had no wealth and no possessions at all, and practised alone in the mountains.

No matter how many outer supports of the Dharma we have, they are of no use if our own mind is not tamed. So real practice does not count on outer supports. From today, from this very moment, we should work year by year. We should make the conduct of our body, speech and mind better.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** From today onwards I will not put off good practice. I will make the effort straight away.

**Explanation:** _(Diligence Practice)_ If from today we do not make effort but put things off, we will fall step by step into the lower realms. But if from today we work hard at the good practices we have promised, we will be freed from the suffering of the lower realms and gain the fruit of doing good.
