### Chapter 9 (167 verses) — The Perfection of Wisdom

No commentary-alignment shift found in this chapter — markers correspond
1:1 with translated verse numbers throughout. This is a philosophically
dense chapter (the two truths, illusion-like phenomena, refutation of a
permanent self, the Cittamatra/Madhyamaka debate on self-awareness,
refutation of Samkhya's three gunas and of an atomist "partless particle,"
refutation of a Creator God, the dream/magician analogies, and the closing
verses on compassion and dedication); a large, distributed sample across
all of its argument sections was checked in full against the Tibetan and
found faithful, with no shift and no doctrinal distortion detected. It is
the strongest chapter for fidelity checked so far.
