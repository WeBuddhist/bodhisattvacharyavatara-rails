## Combined Commentary Summaries

Concluding the argument begun in the previous verse, the commentators agree that failing to follow the instructions of the Omniscient Physician (the Buddha) — who alone can remove every such pain — is a mark of profound delusion, "a state to be reproached."

Gyaltsab Je specifies that only by taking refuge in the Teacher "from the depths of the heart" and practicing the teaching correctly can one apply this cure. Thokme continues to treat this as part of the single combined statement spanning verses 54–56, while Sazang likewise underscores that this antidote-medicine must be relied upon exactly as instructed by the All-Knowing One.
