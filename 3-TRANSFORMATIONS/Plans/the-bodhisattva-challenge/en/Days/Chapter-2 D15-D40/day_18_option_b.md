# Day 18 — Hand yourself over, and mean it

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Pick one ordinary task and offer it as service before you start
> **Detail:** Shantideva stops offering things and offers himself, for good. Today, give one action as service.

## Opening

For days the gifts have grown larger: the world, the heavens, everything beautiful. Today Shantideva offers the one thing left. He offers himself, his whole body, and not for a moment but for good. He gives it to the buddhas and their bodhisattva offspring, and says plainly that he will be their servant and do what they ask.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

We give things away easily enough. Giving ourselves is harder, and we tend to do it for a moment and then take it back.

The verse offers the body and asks the buddhas to accept it. Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, a fifteenth-century teacher, draws out two things the words carry. First, the word "victorious" is not flattery: these are the ones who have defeated the four maras, the forces that defeat the rest of us. You are placing yourself with someone who has actually won. Second, the offering is not a feeling. It ends with **"I will be your servant and do what you ask."** Shantideva is handing over the steering, and for good, not for the length of one prayer. What follows is no longer about pleasing himself.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Pick one ordinary task you have to do today anyway, making a meal, answering a message, a chore. Before you start it, quietly offer it as service to the Buddha, the dharma, and the sangha, and then do it as if it were asked of you.
