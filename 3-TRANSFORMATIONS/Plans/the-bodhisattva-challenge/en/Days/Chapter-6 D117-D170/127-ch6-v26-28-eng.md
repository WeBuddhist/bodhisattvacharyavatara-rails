## Today's Verse

> Those assembled conditions
> also do not intend to generate anything.
> Nor does that which is generated by them
> intend to generate itself. ^6-26

> That which is considered the Primal Substance
> or the self that is conceived
> does not arise with the specific intention
> "I will arise." ^6-27

> If it does not exist before it arises,
> what is that which desires to arise at that time?
> That which is always distracted by objects
> also does not cease. ^6-28

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the twenty-sixth, twenty-seventh, and twenty-eighth verses of the sixth chapter. When someone harms us, we think there is a main planner behind it. But among all the conditions that came together, can we find such a main one anywhere?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable example, which is easy to understand and encouraging.

An independent self, or main doer, has never been born, like the son of a barren woman. Like a rabbit's horn, it cannot do anything. So there is not even one independent doer who wants to create anger and harm. Results arise when causes and conditions simply come together. The causes and conditions do not think of producing the result. And the result does not think, "I was produced by these causes and conditions."

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, when others harm me, I will remember that it came from causes and conditions coming together.

**Explanation:** _(Wisdom Practice)_ Harm comes when causes and conditions come together. Those causes and conditions do not plan to produce anger. If we know this, we are freed from anger toward others.
