# Day 23 — Let the music ease someone's pain

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Hear a song today as comfort offered to others
> **Detail:** Shantideva offers clouds of music to ease beings' suffering. Today, let a sound you hear carry comfort.

## Opening

Today's offering is sound. Shantideva wishes up vast clouds of music and sweet melody, and he asks one particular thing of them: that they ease the suffering of living beings, and stay, each in its place, like clouds that do not drift away.

> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

Beautiful music usually points back at us: it moves me, it suits my mood, it is the sound I like.

The verse offers clouds of music too, but Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, a fifteenth-century teacher, notes the wish attached to them. These are not sounds to be enjoyed and forgotten. The hope is that, merely on being heard, they ease the suffering of whoever hears them and leave that person more at peace. The small turn is this: you can offer a sound not for how it pleases you, but for what it might do for someone else. Held that way, listening becomes a kind of care, and a steadier compassion grows in the one who listens like that.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

The next time you hear music or a song today, take a moment to hear it as something that could comfort a person in pain, and offer it in your mind to the Three Jewels, wishing its ease to reach someone who is suffering.
