# Day 13 — A moment of admiration matters more than you think

## Opening

*Push notification:* One harsh thought and one warm thought do not weigh the same.

Today's verses are about two feelings toward a bodhisattva: anger and admiration. Anger harms only you, not the bodhisattva. Admiration does the opposite: it brings you even greater merit.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.

> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

We usually think it is the big, visible good deeds that count, and that a quiet feeling inside us matters little.

Today's second verse says that admiring a bodhisattva brings even greater merit than anger brings harm. But it does not say how much greater. Ngulchu Thokme, a fourteenth-century commentator on this text, gives a measure. He points to a sutra with a striking example. Imagine everyone in the world had gone blind, and one person made them all see again. That would be an enormous kindness. And yet, the sutra says, a single look at a bodhisattva with real faith brings even more merit than that. The reason is simple. A bodhisattva works to free every being, so even one moment of faith in such a person is worth a great deal.

## Aspiration

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice Challenge

**Your task today:**

Think of one person whose goodness you respect. A teacher, a friend, anyone kind.

For a few seconds, **let yourself truly admire them.**

Feel that respect, and do not hurry away from it.
