# Day 3 — Bodhisattvacharyavatara Practice Plan

## Chapter 1: Benefits of Bodhicitta — Verses 1-7 to 1-9

---

### 1. Refuge & Bodhicitta (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ་སེམས་བསྐྱེད།)

#### **1. Refuge (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ།)**

> *Until I reach the heart of awakening,*
> *I take refuge in the Buddhas.*
> *Likewise, I take refuge in the Dharma*
> *And in the assembly of Bodhisattvas.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྙིང་པོར་མཆིས་ཀྱི་བར། །
> སངས་རྒྱས་རྣམས་ལ་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །
> ཆོས་དང་བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་དཔའི། །
> ཚོགས་ལའང་དེ་བཞིན་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །

---

### 2. Benefit (ཕན་ཡོན།)

Based on the traditional commentaries of Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, Sazang Mati Panchen, and Ngulchu Thokme Zangpo, practicing and reflecting on today's verses yields the following profound benefits:

*   **A Path of Joy, Not Hardship (Ease of Practice):** The wish to awaken is not a burdensome obligation. It is actually a joyful path that becomes easier the further one travels. Understanding this removes the fear of spiritual practice and replaces it with genuine enthusiasm.
*   **A Clear Answer to Suffering (Direction in Life):** When we wish to be free from pain, to help others, and to find real happiness, the wish to awaken is the one method that answers all three. Knowing this gives daily life clear meaning and direction.
*   **Immediate Transformation of Identity (Dignity and Confidence):** The very moment the wish to awaken arises in the mind, a person's inner status changes completely. This understanding brings a quiet sense of dignity that is not dependent on outer circumstances.

### 3. Today's Root Verses & Commentaries (དེ་རིང་གི་སྤྱོད་འཇུག་རྩ་ཚིག་དངོས།)

#### **Verse 1-7**

> བསྐལ་པ་དུ་མར་རབ་དགོངས་མཛད་པ་ཡི། །
> ཐུབ་དབང་རྣམས་ཀྱིས་འདི་ཉིད་ཕན་པར་གཟིགས། །
> འདིས་ནི་ཚད་མེད་སྐྱེ་བོའི་ཚོགས་རྣམས་ཀྱིས། །
> བདེ་མཆོག་བདེ་བླག་ཉིད་དུ་ཐོབ་པར་བྱེད། །

> *The Wise Sages contemplated this for many eons and saw that this mind alone is beneficial. Through it, countless multitudes of beings easily attain the highest happiness.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-7**

The awakened Buddhas spent countless eons searching for the best way to help all beings, and their conclusion was clear: the wish to awaken is the one supreme method. What makes it extraordinary is how it works — not through painful struggle or harsh effort, but through a joyful, gradual path that moves from one happiness to an even greater one.

---

#### **Verse 1-8**

> སྲིད་པའི་སྡུག་བསྔལ་བརྒྱ་ཕྲག་གཞོམ་འདོད་ཅིང་། །
> སེམས་ཅན་མི་བདེ་བསལ་བར་འདོད་པ་དང་། །
> བདེ་མང་བརྒྱ་ཕྲག་སྤྱོད་པར་འདོད་པས་ཀྱང་། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་ཉིད་རྟག་ཏུ་གཏང་མི་བྱ། །

> *Do you wish to overcome the hundreds of sufferings of worldly existence? Do you wish to remove the unhappiness of sentient beings and enjoy hundreds of joys? If so, you must never abandon the mind of enlightenment.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-8**

This verse addresses three deep wishes that most practitioners share: to be free from suffering, to help others be free, and to find lasting joy. All three wishes point to the same answer — the wish to awaken. Since this mind is the only certain way to fulfill all three, anyone who holds these aspirations must protect it and never let it go.

---

#### **Verse 1-9**

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་སྐྱེས་གྱུར་ན་སྐད་ཅིག་གིས། །
> འཁོར་བའི་བཙོན་རར་བསྡམས་པའི་ཉམ་ཐག་རྣམས། །
> བདེ་གཤེགས་རྣམས་ཀྱི་སྲས་ཞེས་བརྗོད་བྱ་ཞིང་། །
> འཇིག་རྟེན་ལྷ་མིར་བཅས་པས་ཕྱག་བྱར་འགྱུར། །

> *The moment this mind is born, wretched beings bound in the prison of cyclic existence become heirs of the Blissful Ones. They also become worthy of respect by gods and humans alike.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-9**

The transformation described here is instantaneous and total. At the very moment the wish to awaken is genuinely born in the mind, even the most ordinary person — still caught in the ordinary round of suffering — becomes a spiritual heir of the Buddha. This is not a distant goal; it is an immediate shift in inner identity that even the gods recognize.

### 4. Daily Life Application (ཉིན་རེའི་འཚོ་བའི་ནང་ཉམས་སུ་ལེན་ཚུལ།)

*   **1. Practice Joyfully, Not Reluctantly:** Today, I will notice any moment when spiritual practice feels like a burden. When that happens, I will remind myself that this path is meant to go from happiness to greater happiness. I will look for the joy in even a small act of kindness or reflection.
*   **2. Use My Wishes as a Compass:** When I feel the wish to be free from stress, to help someone, or to find real peace, I will recognize these as signs pointing toward the wish to awaken. I will let those wishes guide my actions rather than ignore them or feel helpless about them.
*   **3. Remember My Inner Dignity:** At some point today, I will pause and recall that the wish to awaken has been alive in my mind — even faintly — and that this gives me a kind of dignity that no outer circumstance can take away. I will carry that quietly and let it steady me.

### 5. Dedication & Aspiration (བསྔོ་བ་དང་སྨོན་ལམ།)

#### **1. Dedication (བསྔོ་བ།)**

> *By the virtue accumulated*
> *Through composing this entry into the Bodhisattva conduct,*
> *May all wandering beings without exception*
> *Engage in the Bodhisattva conduct.*

> བདག་གིས་བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་པ་ལ། །
> འཇུག་པ་རྣམ་པར་བརྩམས་པ་ཡི། །
> དགེ་བ་གང་དེས་འགྲོ་བ་ཀུན། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་ལ་འཇུག་པར་ཤོག །

#### **2. Aspiration (སྨོན་ལམ།)**

> *May the precious and supreme mind of awakening,*
> *Where it has not arisen, arise;*
> *Where it has arisen, may it not decline,*
> *But increase further and further.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་མཆོག་རིན་པོ་ཆེ། །
> མ་སྐྱེས་པ་རྣམས་སྐྱེ་གྱུར་ཅིག །
> སྐྱེས་པ་ཉམས་པ་མེད་པ་དང་། །
> གོང་ནས་གོང་དུ་འཕེལ་བར་ཤོག །

### 6. Key Terms Glossary (གནད་ཚིག་ཁག་གི་འགྲེལ་བཤད།)

*   **Mind of Awakening (བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་ / byang chub sems):** This is the heartfelt wish to achieve full awakening in order to free all beings from suffering. Today's verses present it as the supreme discovery of all the Buddhas — the single most effective method for bringing happiness to oneself and others.

*   **Wise Sages / Kings of Sages (ཐུབ་དབང་ / thub dbang):** This title refers to the fully awakened Buddhas. The word "sage" points to their great restraint and mastery over the mind, while "king" points to their supreme spiritual authority. These are beings who searched for eons to find the best way to help all living beings.

*   **Cyclic Existence / Prison of Cyclic Existence (འཁོར་བའི་བཙོན་ར་ / 'khor ba'i btson ra):** Cyclic existence refers to the ongoing cycle of birth, suffering, and death that ordinary beings are trapped in. The image of a prison highlights how karma and mental afflictions bind beings against their will, making it impossible to escape without the right method.

*   **Heir of the Blissful Ones (བདེ་གཤེགས་ཀྱི་སྲས་ / bde gshegs kyi sras):** This phrase means a spiritual heir or child of the Buddhas — someone who has taken on the same mission of leading all beings to awakening. Today's verses teach that this noble status is not earned gradually; it arises the very instant the wish to awaken is genuinely born in the mind.

*   **Highest Happiness (བདེ་མཆོག་ / bde mchog):** This refers to the state of full awakening — the most complete and lasting happiness possible. It is not just the absence of pain, but a completely fulfilled state of wisdom and compassion. The great teacher Śāntideva teaches that this is actually easy to attain through the joyful path of the wish to awaken.
