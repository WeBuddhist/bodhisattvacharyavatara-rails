## Today's Verse

> Like the great elements of earth and so forth,
> and like space, may I always
> be the basis of various kinds of sustenance
> for countless beings. ^3-20

> Likewise, for all beings
> reaching to the ends of space,
> until all pass beyond sorrow,
> may I be the cause of their sustenance. ^3-21

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the two verses 20 and 21 from the third chapter of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. Together we reflect on that deep prayer of becoming, ourselves, a support for all beings, like earth, water, fire, wind and space.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us a passage of scripture that is easy to understand and encouraging.

This is a sutra passage quoted in Khenpo Minyak Kunzang Sonam's commentary, by way of the _Shikshasamuchaya_. It shows that we should turn this body of ours into a support for the life of all beings. The four great elements are such a support.

> I shall give this body over to whatever work all beings have. For example, these four great elements, the earth element, the water element, the fire element and the wind element, are used and fully used in many ways, in many kinds, for many purposes, as many articles, and in many activities. In the same way, with this body made of the four great elements, let me think: in many ways, in many kinds, for many purposes, as many articles and in many uses, may I widely sustain the life of all beings.

So says the _Akshayamati Sutra_.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, when I help someone, I will do it like the earth, without expecting anything in return.

**Explanation:** _(Generosity Practice)_ This verse says that we should benefit beings as the earth and space do. The earth is a support for everyone alike. In the same way, if we help others without hoping for a return, happiness comes to us and to them both.
