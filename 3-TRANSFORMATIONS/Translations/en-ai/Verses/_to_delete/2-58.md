## Combined Commentary Summaries

Beginning here, the commentators note a shift toward urging swift effort, grounded in reflection on death. The verse rejects the complacent thought "I surely will not die today," since the moment of death is certain to come, even if its timing is not.

Gyaltsab Je supplies a supporting citation: "'Today I shall do this and that' is not good conduct for a person; the day will surely come when you will cease to exist." Thokme frames the verse as directly rebutting the excuse that "it is enough to confess wrongdoing at some later time" — an excuse he treats together with the following verse as one combined rebuttal. Sazang likewise stresses that the very fact that the time of death is uncertain is itself reason for urgency, not complacency.
