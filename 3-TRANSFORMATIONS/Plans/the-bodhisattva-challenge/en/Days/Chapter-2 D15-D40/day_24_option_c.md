# Day 24 — Say the good you see out loud

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Say one true word of praise today
> **Detail:** Today's verse is praise. Name a good quality you see, out loud or in your heart.

## Opening

After the offerings, Shantideva turns to praise. He calls the buddhas and bodhisattvas oceans of good qualities, and he wants to answer them with an ocean of praise, melody upon melody, rising before them and never running out.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

> I prostrate, bowing as many bodies

> To all the buddhas of the three times,
> The dharma, and the sublime sangha.

## From the Tradition

Praising someone does not always come easily, when part of us would rather be the one praised. Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, a fifteenth-century teacher, reads this verse as training for exactly that. He pictures the praise multiplied past what one mouth could manage: countless heads, and on each head countless tongues, all praising the buddhas' qualities. The point is not the size of the sound. It is what naming another's good does to us. Each time we say plainly that something in someone is good, the pull to compete loosens, our own speech is cleaned of its sharper edges, and gladness at another person's good has room to grow.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Once today, say one true thing in praise of someone, a quality you actually admire in them. Say it to them if you can, or quietly to the buddhas, the dharma, and the sangha. Keep it real and specific. One honest line is plenty.
