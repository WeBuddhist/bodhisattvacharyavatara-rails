# Day 5 — Bodhisattvacharyavatara Practice Plan

## Chapter 1: The Benefits of the Mind of Enlightenment — Verses 1-13 to 1-15

---

### 1. Refuge & Bodhicitta (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ་སེམས་བསྐྱེད།)

#### **1. Refuge (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ།)**

> *Until I reach the heart of awakening,*
> *I take refuge in the Buddhas.*
> *Likewise, I take refuge in the Dharma*
> *And in the assembly of Bodhisattvas.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྙིང་པོར་མཆིས་ཀྱི་བར། །
> སངས་རྒྱས་རྣམས་ལ་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །
> ཆོས་དང་བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་དཔའ་ཡི། །
> ཚོགས་ལའང་དེ་བཞིན་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །

### 2. Benefit (ཕན་ཡོན།)

Based on the traditional commentaries of Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, Sazang Mati Panchen, and Ngulchu Thokme Zangpo, practicing and reflecting on today's verses yields the following profound benefits:

*   **The Heroic Protection (Overcoming Great Fear):** Reflecting on the analogy of relying on a brave companion reveals how the mind of enlightenment acts as an ultimate refuge. Even if a practitioner has committed the most dreadful misdeeds, this compassionate mind swiftly protects them from the terrifying fears of samsara and the lower realms, offering immediate and unbreakable security.
*   **The Cosmic Purification (Complete Destruction of Negativity):** Like the world-consuming fire at the end of an eon, generating the mind of enlightenment has the supreme power to burn and destroy even the heaviest, most deep-seated negative karma in a single instant. This is not just temporary protection, but the complete eradication of suffering's causes from their very root.
*   **The Dual Path of Intention and Action (Understanding Aspiring and Engaging Bodhicitta):** Distinguishing between the aspiring mind (the deep yearning to attain Buddhahood for the sake of others) and the engaging mind (actually entering the Bodhisattva conduct) clarifies the spiritual map. It guides practitioners from initial compassionate inspiration to the active, continuous cultivation of virtue that never decays.

### 3. Today's Root Verses & Commentaries (དེ་རིང་གི་སྤྱོད་འཇུག་རྩ་ཚིག་དངོས།)

#### **Verse 1-13**
> དཔའ་ལ་བརྟེན་ནས་འཇིགས་པ་ཆེན་པོ་ལྟར། །གང་ལ་བརྟེན་ནས་ཡུད་ཀྱིས་སྒྲོལ་འགྱུར་བ། །
> དེ་ལ་བག་ཅན་རྣམས་ཀྱིས་ཅིས་མི་བརྟེན། ། ^1-13
> 
> *Even if someone has committed terrible misdeeds, relying on this mind will swiftly liberate them. This is like relying on a brave companion to escape great danger. Why then do careful people not rely on it?*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-13**
The teachers compare the mind of enlightenment to a brave companion who protects travelers on a dangerous road. Relying on this mind instantly frees even great wrongdoers from the terrifying fears of the lower realms. Therefore, conscientious people should always trust and rely on this supreme protection.

#### **Verse 1-14**
> དེས་ནི་དུས་མཐའི་མེ་བཞིན་སྡིག་ཆེན་རྣམས། །སྐད་ཅིག་གཅིག་གིས་ངེས་པར་སྲེག་པར་བྱེད། །
> དེ་ཡི་ཕན་ཡོན་དཔག་ཏུ་མེད་པ་དག །བྱམས་མགོན་བློ་དང་ལྡན་པས་ནོར་བཟང་བཤད། ། ^1-14
> 
> *In a single instant, this mind consumes even the greatest sins, like the fire at the end of an age. Its immeasurable benefits were explained by Maitreya, the Loving Protector, to Sudhana.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-14**
Generating the mind of enlightenment destroys severe negative actions in a single instant, like a cosmic fire consuming the world. This supreme mind completely eradicates negativity from its very root. The future Buddha Maitreya verified these limitless benefits to the young seeker Sudhana.

#### **Verse 1-15**
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་དེ་མདོར་བསྡུས་ན། །རྣམ་པ་གཉིས་སུ་ཤེས་བྱ་སཏེ། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྨོན་པའི་སེམས་དང་ནི། །བྱང་ཆུབ་འཇུག་པ་ཉིད་ཡིན་ནོ། ། ^1-15
> 
> *Briefly, the mind of enlightenment should be understood to have two aspects: the aspiring mind of enlightenment, and the engaging mind of enlightenment.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-15**
The conventional mind of enlightenment has two aspects: the aspiring mind and the engaging mind. The aspiring mind wishes to achieve enlightenment to help others. The engaging mind actually enters the path and practices the conduct.

### 4. Daily Life Application (ཉིན་རེའི་འཚོ་བའི་ནང་ཉམས་སུ་ལེན་ཚུལ།)

*   **1. Seeking the Hero's Escort:** When I feel overwhelmed by past mistakes, guilt, or negative habits, I will not fall into despair. Instead, I will actively lean on the "heroic companion" of a compassionate intention. I will remind myself that even a single moment of genuine love and dedication to others' welfare has the power to shield me from fear and negative patterns.
*   **2. Igniting the Purifying Fire:** Today, I will visualize my challenges and negative emotions as dry wood, and my mind of enlightenment as a spark of the cosmic, eon-ending fire. I will consciously bring a mind of awakening to my difficult interactions, trusting in its capacity to burn away anger, selfishness, and resentment from the very root.
*   **3. Bridging Wish and Action:** I will observe the movement of my mind today, noting when I merely wish well (the aspiring mind) versus when I actually act to help (the engaging mind). I will take one concrete step to translate a kind wish into an active deed of generosity, patience, or service, bridging the gap between intention and practice.

### 5. Dedication & Aspiration (བསྔོ་བ་དང་སྨོན་ལམ།)

#### **1. Dedication (བསྔོ་བ།)**

> *By the virtue accumulated*
> *Through composing this entry into the Bodhisattva conduct,*
> *May all wandering beings without exception*
> *Engage in the Bodhisattva conduct.*

> བདག་གིས་བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་པ་ལ། །
> འཇུག་པ་རྣམ་པར་བརྩམས་པ་ཡི། །
> དགེ་བ་གང་དེས་འགྲོ་བ་ཀུན། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་ལ་འཇུག་པར་ཤོག །

#### **2. Aspiration (སྨོན་ལམ།)**

> *May the precious and supreme mind of awakening,*
> *Where it has not arisen, arise;*
> *Where it has arisen, may it not decline,*
> *But increase further and further.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་མཆོག་རིན་པོ་ཆེ། །
> མ་སྐྱེས་པ་རྣམས་སྐྱེ་གྱུར་ཅིག །
> སྐྱེས་པ་ཉམས་པ་མེད་པ་དང་། །
> གོང་ནས་གོང་དུ་འཕེལ་བར་ཤོག །

### 6. Key Terms Glossary (གནད་ཚིག་ཁག་གི་འགྲེལ་བཤད།)

*   **Brave Companion (དཔའ་བོ།):** This term serves as a metaphor for the protective capacity of the mind of enlightenment. Just as a traveler relies on a powerful guardian to cross a dangerous road, a practitioner relies on this compassionate mind to cross the perilous cycle of existence. This reliance instantly overcomes the terrifying fears of negative actions.
*   **Aeon-Ending Fire (དུས་མཐའི་མེ།):** This cosmic metaphor represents the ultimate purifying power of the mind of enlightenment. At the end of a cosmic cycle, a great fire completely consumes the physical universe. Similarly, generating the mind of enlightenment burns away even the heaviest negative actions from their very root in a single instant.
*   **Aspiring Mind of Enlightenment (བྱང་ཆུབ་སྨོན་པའི་སེམས།):** This aspect of the conventional mind of enlightenment consists of the deep desire to attain perfect awakening for the ultimate welfare of all beings. Commentators compare this state of mind to wishing to travel to a specific destination.
*   **Engaging Mind of Enlightenment (བྱང་ཆུབ་འཇུག་པའི་སེམས།):** This aspect of the conventional mind of enlightenment actually enters into the practice of the path. It involves committing to the vows and practicing the actions that lead to awakening. Commentators compare this state of mind to actually setting out on the journey.
