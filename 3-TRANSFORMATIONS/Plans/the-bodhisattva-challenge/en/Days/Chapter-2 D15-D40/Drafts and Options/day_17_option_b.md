# Day 17 — Why give what was never yours

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Give the beauty around you, none of it yours to keep
> **Detail:** The offering continues. Today, offer something beautiful you don't own, in your mind.

## Opening

Why offer the world's beauty instead of your own things? Today's verses ask exactly that. Shantideva answers plainly: he has no wealth of his own, and no store of merit behind him. So he offers what belongs to no one, and asks the buddhas to accept it.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

> I am bereft of merit, destitute,
> And have no other wealth that I could offer.
> May the protectors, who think of others' weal,
> Accept these through their power for my sake.

## From the Tradition

We assume a real gift has to come from what we own.

Sazang Mati Panchen, a fourteenth-century teacher, draws out the reason hidden in the verse. Shantideva offers the world's beauty, things no one owns, precisely because he has nothing of his own to give: no wealth, and no merit saved up. That is no obstacle. What makes it a true offering is the open, devoted heart behind it, not the price of the thing. **A gift needs an open heart, not a full purse.**

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Today, offer something beautiful you could never call your own, a stranger's garden, the morning light, a song you hear. Give it, in your mind, to the Buddha, the Dharma, and the Sangha.
