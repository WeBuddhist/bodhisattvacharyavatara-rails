# Audience Profile — AI Translation (en-ai)

This profile defines the "ideal reader" for the AI translation track.

---

## 1. Demographics and region
- **Primary Region:** Global English-speaking Buddhist practitioners and students.
- **Age Range:** General adult audience (18+).
- **Education:** College-level reading or experienced dharma practitioners.
- **Background:** Familiar with basic Mahayana Buddhist terminology and concepts.

## 2. Prior knowledge and reading level
- **Knowledge of Buddhism:** Intermediate to advanced. Familiar with terms like *bodhicitta*, *samsara*, *Sugata*, and *merit*.
- **Reading Level:** Standard to scholarly. Prefers semantic precision and literal accuracy over simplified, modernized paraphrasing.
- **Linguistic background:** Native or fluent English speakers who value seeing the direct mapping of Tibetan terms to consistent English equivalents.

## 3. Use cases and reading settings
- **Primary Use:** Study, practice, and reference. Used alongside classical commentaries or Tibetan root texts.
- **Setting:** Study groups, meditation retreats, or scholarly research.
- **Goal:** To engage with Śāntideva's text as closely as possible to the original Tibetan wording and technical structure.

## 4. Motivations and pain points
- **Motivations:** Deepening spiritual practice, studying the exact philosophical arguments of the *Bodhisattvacaryāvatāra*, and memorization.
- **Pain Points:** Many modern translations are overly loose, interpretative, or strip away key Sanskrit and Tibetan technical terms, making it hard to trace back to the source.
- **Desired Outcome:** A translation that acts as a reliable, literal mirror of the original Tibetan, using consistent terminology across all verses.
