# Registers for the Bodhicaryāvatāra Chinese versions (Traditional characters)

## Academic (學術版)
- Readers: Chinese-reading students, practitioners and scholars of Buddhist texts.
- Modern written Chinese (白話書面語), Traditional characters, full-width punctuation.
- Terminology: use the established Chinese Buddhist term where one exists (善逝, 法身, 佛子, 菩提心, 暇滿, 三寶, 波羅蜜, 安忍, 精進, 靜慮, 正念, 正知, 世俗諦, 勝義諦, 煩惱). Where the Tibetan distinguishes something a single Chinese word would blur (e.g. the senses of ཆོས), choose the established term that keeps the distinction (法 / 諸法, 佛法), following the English glossary's sense split.
- One locked rendering per term (per sense).

## Children's (兒童版)
- Readers: children aged 8–12 reading Chinese.
- Plain everyday modern Chinese, Traditional characters. Short words a child knows; a technical idea becomes a simple phrase that keeps the meaning, never a different idea.
- Names: the common Chinese name (佛陀, 寂天, 文殊); no transliterated technical Sanskrit terms (not 阿耨多羅三藐三菩提 — say it simply).
- One rendering per term (per sense), used every time.
