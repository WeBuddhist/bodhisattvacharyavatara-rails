## Today's Verse

> Whether one is independent or dependent,
> whatever one practices in any situation,
> one should diligently train in that situation
> which is taught in the training. ^5-99

> There is nothing
> that the spiritual children do not train in.
> To a wise person who dwells in that way,
> there is no merit that does not arise. ^5-100

> Whether directly or indirectly,
> one should not act for anything other than the benefit of beings.
> For the sake of beings alone,
> I dedicate everything for awakening. ^5-101

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the ninety-ninth, one hundredth, and one hundred and first verses of the fifth chapter. Today, even every single step we take, we make into something for the sake of all beings.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable example, which is easy to understand and encouraging. The _Good Arm_ gives this example of dedicating merit. Just as rivers that reach the ocean become one taste of salt, so the merit dedicated to unsurpassed awakening becomes one taste with the buddhas. Just as all the rivers that reach the ocean become one taste of salt, the merit dedicated to unsurpassed awakening becomes one taste with the merit of the buddhas.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will dedicate all the good I have done, so that all beings reach awakening.

**Explanation:** _(Doing good)_ This teaches that everything we do must become only for the sake of beings. When we dedicate good to awakening in this way, it brings benefit to all beings. This makes a good cause for awakening, for ourselves and for others.
