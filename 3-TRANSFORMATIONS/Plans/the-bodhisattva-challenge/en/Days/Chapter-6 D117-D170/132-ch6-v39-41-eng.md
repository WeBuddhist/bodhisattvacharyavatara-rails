## Today's Verse

> If harming others
> is the nature of foolish people,
> it is inappropriate to be angry at them,
> just as it is to blame fire for its burning nature. ^6-39

> But if that fault is temporary,
> and sentient beings are naturally pure,
> then it is also inappropriate to be angry,
> like smoke rarely appearing in the sky. ^6-40

> If you are angry at someone
> who wields a stick or other object and strikes,
> that person is also driven by hatred.
> Therefore, it is appropriate to be angry at hatred itself. ^6-41

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the thirty-ninth, fortieth, and forty-first verses of the sixth chapter. Someone throws a stick at us. We do not get angry at the stick, but we get angry at the one who throws it. But that person is also sent by their own anger. So who should we really be angry at?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable example, which is easy to understand and encouraging.

It is the nature of fire to burn. So if fire burns our hand, it is not right to be angry at the fire. In the same way, it is not right to be angry at the harm that childish people do because of their nature. When smoke suddenly appears in a clear sky, we do not blame the sky. In the same way, the nature of beings is peaceful and pure, so we must see harm as a sudden fault. Even if a usually good person drinks alcohol and shows anger, it is not right to consider that person bad because of it. If someone hits us with a stick, we are not angry at the stick. We are angry at the one who throws it. But that person is also brought under the power of hatred. So the object of our anger is hatred. It is not right to be angry at beings.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, if someone harms me, I will not be angry at that person. I will be angry at hatred.

**Explanation:** _(Ethics Practice)_ If someone throws a stick at us, we do not get angry at the stick. We get angry at the one who throws it. But that person is also pushed by hatred and is not free. So we must not be angry at that person. We must be angry at hatred itself.
