## Today's Verse

> Whatever is not forbidden
> should be practiced.
> For the sake of guarding the worldly mind,
> one should truly practice what is seen in the training. ^5-107

> Repeatedly examining the states
> of body and mind—
> that alone, in brief,
> is the definition of guarding awareness. ^5-108

> These things should be practiced with the body.
> What is accomplished by merely speaking words?
> Does merely reading medical texts
> benefit sick people? ^5-109

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the one hundred and seventh, one hundred and eighth, and one hundred and ninth verses of the fifth chapter. Does a sick person get well just by saying the name of a medicine?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable example, which is easy to understand and encouraging. A very sick person gets no benefit if the doctor does not give medicine and only reads a medical text into the person's ear. But if the body takes the medicine and follows the proper way of living, it benefits. In the same way, merely saying or hearing words of Dharma accomplishes no purpose. Therefore, we must make an effort to put it into practice.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will not only read a lesson. I will actually put one Dharma teaching that I already know into practice with my body.

**Explanation:** _(Diligence Practice)_ Merely reading a medical text cannot cure a sick person's illness. In the same way, merely saying words of Dharma brings no great benefit. Therefore, we must actually practice the meaning of the Dharma with our body.
