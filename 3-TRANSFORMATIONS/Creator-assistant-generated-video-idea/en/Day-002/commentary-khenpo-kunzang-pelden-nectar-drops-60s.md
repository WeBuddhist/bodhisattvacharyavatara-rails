# Day 2 — Commentary — Khenpo Kunzang Pelden (Nectar Drops)

Ever feel like you are just waiting for the right moment to finally start something meaningful, like you are stuck in a mental fog you just cannot clear?

Khenpo Kunzang Pelden, a renowned teacher whose commentary Nectar Drops is a guide for the heart, uses a simple image to explain why that matters. He says our world is like a night without stars or moonlight. We are often lost in confusion, not knowing which way to turn. But then, for a split second, a flash of lightning hits. It lights everything up perfectly.

That flash is that sudden, rare thought you get to do something truly good. It is fragile and rarely lasts. When that flicker hits you, do not wait for permission. Do not overthink it. Just act. Because in the rush of our daily lives, that spark is the rarest, most precious thing you own. Catch it before it fades.
