# Day 4 — Bodhisattvacharyavatara Practice Plan

## Chapter 1: The Benefits of the Mind of Enlightenment — Verses 1-10 to 1-12

---

### 1. Refuge & Bodhicitta (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ་སེམས་བསྐྱེད།)

#### **1. Refuge (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ།)**

> *Until I reach the heart of awakening,*
> *I take refuge in the Buddhas.*
> *Likewise, I take refuge in the Dharma*
> *And in the assembly of Bodhisattvas.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྙིང་པོར་མཆིས་ཀྱི་བར། །
> སངས་རྒྱས་རྣམས་ལ་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །
> ཆོས་དང་བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་དཔའ་ཡི། །
> ཚོགས་ལའང་དེ་བཞིན་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །

---

### 2. Benefit (ཕན་ཡོན།)

Based on the traditional commentaries of Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, Sazang Mati Panchen, and Ngulchu Thokme Zangpo, practicing and reflecting on today's verses yields the following profound benefits:

*   **The Alchemical Transformation (Physical and Spiritual Renewal):** Reflecting on these teachings reveals how the mind of enlightenment acts like a supreme elixir. It takes our ordinary, impure physical body and completely transforms it into the priceless, precious body of a Buddha.
*   **Unerring Guidance (Priceless Value):** Following this path connects us to the flawless evaluation of the Buddhas, who act as peerless captains guiding us across the ocean of worldly existence. Their perfect wisdom guarantees that the mind of enlightenment is the most precious treasure we can cultivate.
*   **Inexhaustible Growth of Virtue (Endless Benefits):** Cultivating this compassionate mind ensures that our positive actions do not fade away after producing a single, temporary result. Instead, our virtues become like a wish-fulfilling tree that continuously bears fruit and grows forever.

### 3. Today's Root Verses & Commentaries (དེ་རིང་གི་སྤྱོད་འཇུག་རྩ་ཚིག་དངོས།)

#### **Verse 1-10**
> གསེར་འགྱུར་རྩི་ཡི་རྣམ་པ་མཆོག་ལྟ་བུ། །མི་གཙང་ལུས་འདི་བླངས་ནས་རྒྱལ་བའི་སྐུ། །
> རིན་ཆེན་རིང་ཐང་མེད་པར་བསྒྱུར་བས་ན། །བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་ཞེས་བྱ་བ་རབ་བརྟན་ཟུང་། ། ^1-10
> 
> *Like the supreme alchemical elixir that transforms base matter into gold, this very body — impure as it is — is taken up and transformed into the priceless body of a Victor. Therefore, hold firmly to what is called bodhichitta!*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-10**
The great teacher compare the mind of enlightenment to a supreme gold-making elixir. This spiritual elixir takes our ordinary, impure physical body and transforms it into the priceless, pure body of a Buddha. This compassionate mind acts like alchemy. It completely purifies our negative habits and mental struggles. Furthermore, this supreme attitude can never be corrupted or exhausted by any amount of worldly suffering.

#### **Verse 1-11**
> འགྲོ་བའི་དེད་དཔོན་གཅིག་པུ་ཚད་མེད་བློས། །ལེགས་པར་ཡོངས་སུ་བརྟགས་ན་རིན་ཆེ་བས། །
> འགྲོ་བའི་གནས་དང་བྲལ་བར་འདོད་པ་རྣམས། །རིན་ཆེན་བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་ལེགས་བརྟན་པར་ཟུང་། ། ^1-11
> 
> *The sole guide of wandering beings, examined with a mind of immeasurable wisdom, is seen to be more precious than jewels. Those who wish to be free from the realm of wandering beings — hold firmly to this precious bodhicitta!*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-11**
It describe the Buddha as a skilled maritime captain who guides wandering beings across the ocean of worldly existence. Just as merchants trust a captain to identify genuine treasures, we can trust the Buddha's perfect wisdom. He thoroughly examined all phenomena and found that the mind of enlightenment is the most valuable jewel. Therefore, everyone who wishes to escape ordinary suffering must hold firmly to this precious compassionate attitude.

#### **Verse 1-12**
> དགེ་བ་གཞན་ཀུན་ཆུ་ཤིང་བཞིན་དུ་ནི། །འབྲས་བུ་བསྐྱེད་ནས་ཟད་པར་འགྱུར་བ་ཉིད། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་ཀྱི་ལྗོན་ཤིང་རྟག་པར་ཡང་། །འབྲས་བུ་འབྱིན་པས་མི་ཟད་འཕེལ་བར་འགྱུར། ། ^1-12
> 
> *All other virtues, like the plantain tree, yield their fruit and are exhausted. But the tree of bodhicitta continuously bears fruit and grows without end.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-12**
It compare ordinary good deeds to a banana tree. A banana tree dies immediately after it bears fruit once. In the same way, ordinary virtues are completely exhausted once they produce their temporary results. In contrast, the mind of enlightenment is like a wish-fulfilling tree. Good actions joined with this compassionate mind continuously bear fruit and never decay. Instead, they grow and multiply until we reach complete liberation.

### 4. Daily Life Application (ཉིན་རེའི་འཚོ་བའི་ནང་ཉམས་སུ་ལེན་ཚུལ།)

*   **1. Transforming my daily struggles:** Today, I will view my ordinary physical body and mental struggles as base metals waiting for alchemical transformation. Instead of reacting with frustration, I will apply the elixir of a kind intention to turn my difficult moments into opportunities for cultivating a Buddha's qualities.
*   **2. Trusting the guide's wisdom:** When I feel lost or doubt the value of compassion, I will remember the peerless captain's flawless examination of the mind of enlightenment. I will consciously place my trust in this verified jewel and make a firm decision to hold onto a kind attitude throughout my day.
*   **3. Planting the wish-fulfilling tree:** I will ensure that my positive actions today do not become exhausted like a banana tree. When I perform any act of kindness, I will dedicate it to the ultimate benefit of all beings, turning my simple deeds into an inexhaustible source of growth.

### 5. Dedication & Aspiration (བསྔོ་བ་དང་སྨོན་ལམ།)

#### **1. Dedication (བསྔོ་བ།)**

> *By the virtue accumulated*
> *Through composing this entry into the Bodhisattva conduct,*
> *May all wandering beings without exception*
> *Engage in the Bodhisattva conduct.*

> བདག་གིས་བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་པ་ལ། །
> འཇུག་པ་རྣམ་པར་བརྩམས་པ་ཡི། །
> དགེ་བ་གང་དེས་འགྲོ་བ་ཀུན། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་ལ་འཇུག་པར་ཤོག །

#### **2. Aspiration (སྨོན་ལམ།)**

> *May the precious and supreme mind of awakening,*
> *Where it has not arisen, arise;*
> *Where it has arisen, may it not decline,*
> *But increase further and further.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་མཆོག་རིན་པོ་ཆེ། །
> མ་སྐྱེས་པ་རྣམས་སྐྱེ་གྱུར་ཅིག །
> སྐྱེས་པ་ཉམས་པ་མེད་པ་དང་། །
> གོང་ནས་གོང་དུ་འཕེལ་བར་ཤོག །

### 6. Key Terms Glossary (གནད་ཚིག་ཁག་གི་འགྲེལ་བཤད།)

*   **Alchemical Elixir (གསེར་འགྱུར་རྩི།):** This term refers to a legendary substance that transforms ordinary metals into pure gold. In this context, the commentaries use it to illustrate how the mind of enlightenment takes our impure physical body and purifies it completely. This transformation elevates a regular person into an honored heir of the Buddhas.
*   **Captain of Wandering Beings (འགྲོ་བའི་དེད་དཔོན།):** This term describes the Buddha as a skilled guide who leads all living beings across the dangerous ocean of cyclic existence. Just as merchants rely on an experienced captain to find and verify precious gems, practitioners can rely on the Buddha's perfect wisdom. His flawless examination confirms that compassion is the most valuable treasure in existence.
*   **Banana Tree (ཆུ་ཤིང་།):** This term represents ordinary positive deeds that are not supported by the mind of enlightenment. Just as a banana tree decays immediately after it bears fruit a single time, ordinary virtues are completely spent once they produce their temporary worldly results. This serves as a warning to practitioners to always dedicate their good deeds to the benefit of all.
*   **Wish-Fulfilling Tree (ལྗོན་ཤིང་།):** This term represents the inexhaustible nature of good deeds that are embraced by the mind of enlightenment. Unlike ordinary virtues, actions joined with compassion continuously bear fruit and never decay. Instead, they grow and multiply endlessly until the practitioner reaches ultimate liberation.
