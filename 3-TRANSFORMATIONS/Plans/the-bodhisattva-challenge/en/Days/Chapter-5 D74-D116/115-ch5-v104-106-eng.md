## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the one hundred and fourth, one hundred and fifth, and one hundred and sixth verses of the fifth chapter. All of us open and read, again and again, the _Akashagarbha Sutra_ and the works of Master Nagarjuna.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. The _Avatamsaka Sutra_ and the _Jewel Cloud Sutra_ teach the trainings of bodhisattvas. Those six volumes of the Avatamsaka are very important, so we must study them. In particular, the _Akashagarbha Sutra_ teaches the root downfalls. These include the five downfalls taught to a king, one for ministers, and the eight root downfalls of a beginner bodhisattva. Therefore, a bodhisattva who has taken the engaging vow must first look at that sutra.
