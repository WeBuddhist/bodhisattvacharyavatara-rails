# Day 25 — Bow to those who got you here

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Thank one teacher who shaped you today
> **Detail:** Today's verses bow, take refuge, and ask for help. Start by honoring someone who taught you.

## Opening

Yesterday's bowing carries on, and two new things begin. Shantideva bows once more, now to the teachers and sacred things that carried him toward the path. Then he takes refuge, entrusting himself to the buddhas, the dharma, and the sangha until he reaches awakening. Then he joins his palms before the buddhas and bodhisattvas of every direction and asks for help. Three small acts, each one a way of setting pride down.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

Today moves through three small acts, and each one is a way of setting pride down. The first is the bow. It goes to the images and texts that hold the teaching, the stupas, and, as Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, a fifteenth-century teacher, spells out, the preceptor who gave us our vows, the teacher who showed us the way, and those who keep their discipline honestly. We rarely got anywhere on our own, and bowing simply says so. The second is refuge. To take refuge is to entrust yourself: you rely on the Buddha as your teacher, the dharma as your path, and the sangha as your companions, from now until you reach the heart of awakening. The third is the request. Shantideva joins his palms before the buddhas and bodhisattvas of every direction and asks. Joined palms are the posture of someone who has stopped pretending to need nothing, and that asking is what opens the door to clearing the wrongs we have done.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Bring to mind one teacher who actually changed something for you: a person who taught you, corrected you, or showed you how to live a little better. Hold them in mind for a moment with real thanks, and if you like, make one small bow toward them. It is hard to feel above someone you are thanking.
