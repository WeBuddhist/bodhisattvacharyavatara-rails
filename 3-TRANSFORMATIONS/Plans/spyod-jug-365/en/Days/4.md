# Day 4 — Bodhisattvacharyavatara Practice Plan

## Chapter 1: The Benefits of the Mind of Enlightenment — Verses 1-10 to 1-12

---

### 1. Refuge & Bodhicitta (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ་སེམས་བསྐྱེད།)

#### **1. Refuge (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ།)**

> *Until I reach the heart of awakening,*
> *I take refuge in the Buddhas.*
> *Likewise, I take refuge in the Dharma*
> *And in the assembly of Bodhisattvas.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྙིང་པོར་མཆིས་ཀྱི་བར། །
> སངས་རྒྱས་རྣམས་ལ་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །
> ཆོས་དང་བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་དཔའ་ཡི། །
> ཚོགས་ལའང་དེ་བཞིན་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །

---

### 2. Benefit (ཕན་ཡོན།)

Based on the traditional commentaries of Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, Sazang Mati Panchen, and Ngulchu Thokme Zangpo, practicing and reflecting on today's verses yields the following profound benefits:

*   **Alchemical Transformation (The Gold-Making Elixir):** Contemplating the mind of enlightenment reveals its extraordinary power to transform our ordinary, impure physical body into the priceless, majestic body of a Buddha. This alchemical shift turns the "iron" of our daily karmic struggles and mental afflictions into the "gold" of complete awakening, giving our lives a sublime and ultimate purpose.
*   **Unerring Guidance (The Precious Jewel):** Meditating on these teachings instills absolute trust in the path. The Buddha, as the peerless captain of wandering beings, thoroughly investigated all phenomena with his limitless, omniscient wisdom and identified the mind of enlightenment as the single most precious and beneficial method. Relying on this unerring evaluation gives us the confidence to hold firmly to this path to escape cyclic suffering.
*   **Inexhaustible Virtue (The Wish-Fulfilling Tree):** Understanding the nature of dedicated goodness ensures that our positive efforts are never wasted. While ordinary virtues are like a banana tree that decays immediately after bearing fruit once, virtues conjoined with the mind of enlightenment act like a wish-fulfilling tree. They continuously yield results—from temporary happiness in worldly life to ultimate Buddhahood—and never cease to grow and multiply.

### 3. Today's Root Verses & Commentaries (དེ་རིང་གི་སྤྱོད་འཇུག་རྩ་ཚིག་དངོས།)

#### **Verse 1-10**
> གསེར་འགྱུར་རྩི་ཡི་རྣམ་པ་མཆོག་ལྟ་བུ། །མི་གཙང་ལུས་འདི་བླངས་ནས་རྒྱལ་བའི་སྐུ། །
> རིན་ཆེན་རིང་ཐང་མེད་པར་བསྒྱུར་བས་ན། །བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་ཞེས་བྱ་བ་རབ་བརྟན་ཟུང་། ། ^1-10
> 
> *Like a supreme alchemical elixir, this mind takes our impure body and transforms it into the priceless body of a Buddha. Therefore, hold firmly to this precious mind of enlightenment.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-10**
The commentators (Gyaltsab Je, Sazang Mati Panchen, and Ngulchu Thokme) explain this verse as the analogy of transforming the base into the supreme using a gold-making alchemical elixir (Skt. *rasāyana*; Tib. *gser 'gyur rtsi*).
*   **The Metaphor:** Citing the *Gaṇḍavyūha Sūtra*, they describe a supreme elixir where a single ounce can transform a thousand ounces of iron into gold. Here, the "iron" represents our ordinary, impure physical body, karma, and afflictions, while the "gold" represents the glorious, priceless body of a Buddha.
*   **Doctrinal Application:** Conjoined with the wisdom of dedication, the precious mind of enlightenment (bodhicitta) can never be corrupted or exhausted by negative karma. Instead, it completely consumes all afflictions and turns our base existence into the golden state of Buddhahood.

#### **Verse 1-11**
> འགྲོ་བའི་དེད་དཔོན་གཅིག་པུ་ཚད་མེད་བློས། །ལེགས་པར་ཡོངས་སུ་བརྟགས་ན་རིན་ཆེ་བས། །
> འགྲོ་བའི་གནས་དང་བྲལ་བར་འདོད་པ་རྣམས། །རིན་ཆེན་བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་ལེགས་བརྟན་པར་ཟུང་། ། ^1-11
> 
> *The sole guide of wandering beings thoroughly examined this mind with immeasurable wisdom. He saw its immense value. Therefore, those who wish to escape worldly suffering must hold firmly to this precious jewel of enlightenment.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-11**
This verse emphasizes that the supreme value of the mind of enlightenment is completely trustworthy because it was thoroughly analyzed and verified by the Buddha—the ultimate guide of wandering beings.
*   **The Metaphor:** Just as merchants on a dangerous voyage cherish a jewel that has been verified and praised by an expert maritime captain, we must cherish bodhicitta because the Buddha, the captain of liberation, evaluated and praised its qualities.
*   **Unerring Examination:** Using his immeasurable, omniscient wisdom, the Buddha examined all phenomena and identified bodhicitta as the single most beneficial method. The commentators cite the *Gaṇḍavyūha Sūtra* to show that the virtues of gods, humans, and Arhats can never equal the value of this precious king of jewels.

#### **Verse 1-12**
> དགེ་བ་གཞན་ཀུན་ཆུ་ཤིང་བཞིན་དུ་ནི། །འབྲས་བུ་བསྐྱེད་ནས་ཟད་པར་འགྱུར་བ་ཉིད། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་ཀྱི་ལྗོན་ཤིང་རྟག་པར་ཡང་། །འབྲས་བུ་འབྱིན་པས་མི་ཟད་འཕེལ་བར་འགྱུར། ། ^1-12
> 
> *All other virtues are like a banana tree that dies after bearing fruit once. In contrast, the tree of the mind of enlightenment continuously bears fruit and always grows.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-12**
This verse contrasts ordinary virtues with those embraced by bodhicitta, using the analogies of the banana tree and the wish-fulfilling tree.
*   **Banana Tree (Ordinary Virtues):** Virtues not embraced by bodhicitta decay and are exhausted immediately after bearing fruit once. This includes worldly virtues (which produce temporary samsaric happiness) and the untainted virtues of Hearers and Solitary Realizers (which are exhausted upon entry into remainderless nirvana).
*   **Wish-Fulfilling Tree (Bodhicitta Virtues):** Virtues conjoined with bodhicitta and dedicated to omniscience continuously bear fruit, never exhaust, and constantly increase. Gyaltsab Je cites the *Akṣayamati Sūtra*, comparing dedicated virtue to a drop of water falling into the great ocean, which is never exhausted until the ocean itself dries up. Ngulchu Thokme and Sazang Mati Panchen also cite the *Jewel Casket Sūtra*, comparing bodhicitta to the four elements that support and cause all trees to grow.

### 4. Daily Life Application (ཉིན་རེའི་འཚོ་བའི་ནང་ཉམས་སུ་ལེན་ཚུལ།)

*   **1. Activating Alchemical Transformation:** Today, when I face physical discomfort, tiredness, or feel discouraged by the limitations of my ordinary body, I will recall the alchemical metaphor of [[3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-ai/Verses/1-10|Verse 1-10]]. Instead of viewing my body as just an impure vessel of flesh and bone, I will see it as a precious crucible for transformation. By intentionally generating a kind wish or performing a helpful deed for someone else, I will transform the "iron" of my daily physical struggles into the "gold" of awakened action.
*   **2. Placing Trust in Supreme Guidance:** Whenever I feel doubtful, confused, or lost regarding my spiritual practice or life choices today, I will remember the peerless captain of [[3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-ai/Verses/1-11|Verse 1-11]]. I will remind myself that the Buddha, with his limitless, omniscient wisdom, thoroughly tested all paths and highlighted the mind of enlightenment as the most precious and effective method. Choosing to trust this tested guidance, I will hold firmly to the wish to benefit others as my primary compass.
*   **3. Planting Inexhaustible Seeds of Virtue:** Today, whenever I perform any positive action—no matter how small, such as smiling at a stranger, listening patiently to a colleague, or giving a small donation—I will consciously dedicate this virtue to the ultimate happiness and enlightenment of all beings. I will remind myself that without this dedication, my goodness is like a banana tree that is quickly exhausted. By conjoining my actions with the mind of enlightenment, I am planting a seed in a wish-fulfilling tree that will grow and bear fruit forever.

### 5. Dedication & Aspiration (བསྔོ་བ་དང་སྨོན་ལམ།)

#### **1. Dedication (བསྔོ་བ།)**

> *By the virtue accumulated*
> *Through composing this entry into the Bodhisattva conduct,*
> *May all wandering beings without exception*
> *Engage in the Bodhisattva conduct.*

> བདག་གིས་བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་པ་ལ། །
> འཇུག་པ་རྣམ་པར་བརྩམས་པ་ཡི། །
> དགེ་བ་གང་དེས་འགྲོ་བ་ཀུན། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་ལ་འཇུག་པར་ཤོག །

#### **2. Aspiration (སྨོན་ལམ།)**

> *May the precious and supreme mind of awakening,*
> *Where it has not arisen, arise;*
> *May it not decline where it has arisen,*
> *But increase further and further.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་མཆོག་རིན་པོ་ཆེ། །
> མ་སྐྱེས་པ་རྣམས་སྐྱེ་གྱུར་ཅིག །
> སྐྱེས་པ་ཉམས་པ་མེད་པ་དང་། །
> གོང་ནས་གོང་དུ་འཕེལ་བར་ཤོག །

### 6. Key Terms Glossary (གནད་ཚིག་ཁག་གི་འགྲེལ་བཤད།)

*   **Alchemical Elixir (གསེར་འགྱུར་རྩི།):** This term refers to a legendary gold-making alchemical substance (Skt. *rasāyana*). In classical commentaries, Sazang Mati Panchen defines it as a refined quicksilver where a single ounce can transform a thousand ounces of iron into gold. Doctrinally, it represents the precious mind of enlightenment, which transforms our impure body and our heavy iron-like karma and afflictions into the priceless, golden body of a Buddha.
*   **Guide of Wandering Beings (འགྲོ་བའི་དེད་དཔོན།):** This term refers to the Buddha, who acts as the supreme captain leading sentient beings across the dangerous ocean of cyclic existence to the jewel island of liberation. Commentators emphasize that the Buddha thoroughly and unerringly analyzed all phenomena to find that the mind of enlightenment is of supreme value, making his guidance completely trustworthy.
*   **Banana Tree (ཆུ་ཤིང་།):** This refers to the plantain or banana tree, which traditionally represents impermanence and exhaustion because it dies or decays immediately after bearing fruit once. In these verses, it serves as an analogy for ordinary virtues (unembraced by the mind of enlightenment), which are completely spent once they produce their temporary, worldly results.
