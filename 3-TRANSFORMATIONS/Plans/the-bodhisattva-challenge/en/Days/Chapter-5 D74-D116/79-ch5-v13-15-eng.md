## Today's Verse

> How could there be enough leather
> to cover all the surface of the earth?
> It is like covering the entire earth
> with just a piece of leather for a shoe sole. ^5-13

> Likewise, I cannot turn away
> external things.
> I should turn away this mind of mine;
> what need is there to turn away others? ^5-14

> What fruit arises from generating a single clear mind,
> like that of Brahmā and other gods?
> The fruit of actions of body and speech
> is not like that due to weaker conduct. ^5-15

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the thirteenth, fourteenth, and fifteenth verses of the fifth chapter. Let us look together at a method for taming our own mind. It is like covering a shoe's sole with leather, instead of covering the whole thorny earth.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us an example that is easy to understand and encouraging.

To protect our feet from the thorns and sharp stones covering the earth everywhere, we could never cover the whole earth with leather. But by wearing just a small piece of leather on the sole of our shoe, our feet stay just as comfortable as if the whole earth were covered.

In the same way, we could never defeat or turn back every single harmful person and enemy in the world, one by one. But if we turn back and tame only the anger and hatred that arise in our own mind, every outer harm calms down naturally. So we do not need to tame even a single outer enemy. By taming only our own mind, we become completely free from every outer fear.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, in the course of one single action, I will generate clear loving-kindness from my heart, not just with words.

**Explanation:** _(Diligence Practice)_ Even many weak and unclear actions of body and speech bring far less result than generating one single clear and pure mind of love and compassion. Long ago, a mother's child was swept away by a river. Through the power of the clear, strong love she felt for her child, she was reborn in the world of Brahmā. So today, whatever we do, we will not do it with mere words or empty formality. We will do it with a genuine, clear mind and pure love, from the heart.
