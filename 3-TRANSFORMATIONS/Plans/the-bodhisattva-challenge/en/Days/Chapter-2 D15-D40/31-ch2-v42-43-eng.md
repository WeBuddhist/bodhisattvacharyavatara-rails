## Today's Verse

> I have been so careless, buddhas. I never really understood a danger this serious. And because of that, I have piled up so much wrongdoing, just for the sake of this short life. ^2-42

> Think of someone being led away to lose an arm or a leg. Even before it happens, they are already terrified — mouth dry, eyes wide with fear, completely changed from how they looked before. ^2-43

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on verses 42 and 43 from the second chapter of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. We spend most of our time on this life's living, profit, reputation, and comfort, and along the way we gather all kinds of harmful actions, some deliberately and much of it without noticing. Today's verses remind us how short and unstable this life is, and how much fear the results of bad actions bring at the time of death. They give us the resolve to confess what we have done and to stop trading it away for this life's small returns.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

Notice who Shantideva is speaking to. He calls them protectors. He already knew who they were and what they offered, and he went the careless way regardless. The confession is not that he lacked information. It is that having it changed nothing.

The phrase worth slowing down for is "such a danger," because Khenpo Kunzang Palden does not leave it vague. He separates it into three, in order. First, the terror as life is cut off at death. Second, after death, the terror of the bardo, the state between this life and the next. Third, in the next life, the terror of the lower realms. So this is not one shapeless dread. It is a sequence, and each stage has its own character.

Set that against what the misdeeds were actually for: subduing enemies, looking after our own people, food, money, things to enjoy. A little livelihood for one short life.

Then the verse stops arguing and shows us the man being led out to be maimed — the dry mouth, the fixed eyes, the colour gone from his face. If a human punishment does that, the commentaries ask, what should our own actions do? Therefore, feel regret. Regret is the part that is available today.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today I will not lie or mislead anyone for my own small advantage. I will stay straight with people.

**Explanation:** _(Avoiding wrongdoing)_ Piling up wrongdoing for the sake of this life's profit and comfort is exactly the carelessness these verses confess, and it usually shows up in small forms rather than large ones. A convenient untruth to get out of something. Shading a fact so a deal goes our way. Letting someone believe what suits us. Today I will hold my ground in those moments instead. When the easy false answer is right there, I will give the true one, even if it costs me something. These small deceptions harm the other person and quietly harm us, and declining them once makes the next one easier to decline.
