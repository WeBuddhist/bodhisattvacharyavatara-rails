## Today's Verse

> The Buddha taught that all these fears
> and immeasurable suffering
> arise from the mind.
> This is truly what he taught. ^5-6

> Who intentionally created
> the weapons of the hell beings?
> Who made the burning iron ground?
> From what did those heaps of fire arise? ^5-7

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the sixth and seventh verses of the fifth chapter. Sharp questions like these bring fresh energy to today's practice: who made the weapons of hell, and where did its fires come from?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us a clear and encouraging teaching.

The _Sutra Distinguishing Rebirths_ teaches that the sufferings of hell, its weapons and fires, are not made by any outside maker. They arise from the mind of the person who did wrong. It says:

> The iron ground is vast and hot. Blazing tongues of fire spread everywhere. Sharp iron blades cut a single body into a hundred pieces. All of this arises from the minds of those who did wrong in body, speech, and mind.

So, all the suffering of hell comes from the mind of the person who did the wrong.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, when suffering or fear arises, I will not blame the outside world. Instead, I will examine my own mind.

**Explanation:** _(Taming the mind)_ The Teacher, the Buddha, said that all fear and suffering do not come from outside. They come from our own mind. So when we face difficulty, we should not blame the outside world. Instead, we should look at our own mind and work to tame it. If we tame our own mind, all fear and suffering will calm down naturally.
