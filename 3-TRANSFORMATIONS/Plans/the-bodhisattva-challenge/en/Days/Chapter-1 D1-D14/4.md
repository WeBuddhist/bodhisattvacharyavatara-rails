# Day 4 — The moment this wish is born, everything changes

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Feeling stuck? Make one real wish
> **Detail:** The instant this mind arises, you change. Today, when you feel stuck, wish yourself and others free.

## Opening

Today's verses are about a single moment: when the wish to awaken first becomes real in a person. Shantideva says everything changes in that instant. Someone who was stuck and suffering becomes, right then, an heir of the buddhas. He compares it to a substance people once believed could turn iron into gold: it takes an ordinary body and makes it precious. That is why he tells us to hold on to it.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

Today's verses say that the moment this wish arises, an ordinary person becomes an heir of the buddhas.

**Gyaltsab Je**, a fifteenth-century teacher, points to how fast this happens. There is no waiting period. It does not depend on years of practice, or on becoming a better person first. The first time you form this wish and truly mean it, it counts.

You still have all the same faults you had a moment before, and nothing on the outside has changed. And yet, Gyaltsab says, from that moment even the gods have reason to bow to you.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

When you feel stuck today, caught in the same old pattern, try forming the wish for real, not as words you recite, but as something you mean: may I be free of this, and may everyone caught in something like it be free too.
