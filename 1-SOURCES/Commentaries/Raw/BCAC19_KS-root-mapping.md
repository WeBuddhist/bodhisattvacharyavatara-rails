## Notes

- The Loden Sherab translation also carries a chapter `0. ཀླད་ཀྱི་དོན།` (preliminary topics) and the back-matter sections `མཛད་བྱང།` / `འགྱུར་བྱང།`. In this mchan-'grel the opening homage and preliminaries are not set off as a separate chapter — they sit at the head of chapter 1 (lines 13–280), and the translation/compiler colophon sits in the back matter (lines 4404–4414).
- Chapter boundaries are placed at each closing colophon line, so a span is inclusive of that chapter's colophon. The next chapter's content begins on the immediately following line.
- Per-chapter verse spans (chapters 1–10) are likewise inclusive and contiguous: a verse's commentary runs up to and includes any embedded scriptural citations, ending on the line before the next verse's lead-in. Citation attributions in the Notes column identify the sūtra/śāstra the mchan-'grel quotes within that verse's gloss.
