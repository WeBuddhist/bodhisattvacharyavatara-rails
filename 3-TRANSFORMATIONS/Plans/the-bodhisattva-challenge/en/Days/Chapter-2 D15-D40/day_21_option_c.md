# Day 21 — Make the ground ready

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Tidy one small space today as an offering
> **Detail:** Shantideva cleans and strews a floor with flowers for the buddhas. Today, make a small space ready with care.

## Opening

Among today's offerings is a quiet one that is easy to skip past. Before laying out anything, Shantideva prepares the ground: he cleans the floor, anoints it with scented water, and scatters loose flowers across it. The preparing is not a chore before the offering. It is part of the offering.

> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.

> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

We treat tidying and cleaning as the boring part, the thing to rush so the real event can begin.

The verse prepares a floor: swept clean, anointed with scented water, strewn with loose flowers. Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, a fifteenth-century teacher, counts this preparing among the offerings themselves, not as setup for them. There is a plain link between the outer act and the inner one. As your hands make a small space clean and pleasing, the clutter in your mind tends to settle too, and attention grows clearer in the cleared room. The care you put into readying the space is not lost time. It is the offering, and a steadier mind is what it leaves with you.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Pick one small space today, a desk, a shelf, a corner, and make it clean and pleasing without hurrying, as if you were readying it to welcome someone you respect. As you finish, quietly offer the cared-for space to the buddhas and bodhisattvas, and notice how your mind feels in the cleared room.
