# Chapter 1 — practice, aligned verse, and heading options

| Day | Verses | Today's Practice (in brief) | Aligned verse | Heading options |
|----|--------|------------------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| 1 | 1-1 to 1-3 | Before a sharp word or small lie, ask if you truly trust your actions have effects | **1-3** | Feel the jab rise. Pause. Choose better.<br>Bite the word back. Weigh it. Mean it.<br>Every word lands. Aim it. Own it.<br>Does it really land nowhere?<br>Still think it won't count? |
| 2 | 1-4 to 1-5 | Name one thing that matters that you keep putting off; ask what you're waiting for | **1-4** | This life is rare. Don't squander it. Move.<br>Name the dream. Kill the excuse. | 3 | 1-6 to 1-8 | When something feels hard, recall everyone carries it too; turn it into a wish for them | **1-7** | It hurts. They hurt too. Wish them free.<br>Carry the ache. Look up. Bless someone.<br>Your pain. Their pain. One wish.<br>Who else aches like this right now?<br>Whose load could your wish lighten? |
| 4 | 1-9 to 1-11 | When stuck, form the wish for real: may I and all like me be free | **1-9** | Trapped again? Wish it true. Walk free.<br>Break the loop. Make the wish. Mean it.<br>Caught. Wish hard. Step out.<br>What if you actually meant it?<br>Ready to wish like you mean it? |
| 5 | 1-12 to 1-14 | Turn one lingering regret into a wish for all who carry the like | **1-13** | Drop the shame. Flip it. Free them all.<br>Old regret. New wish. Move on.<br>Stop circling it. Turn it. Release.<br>Think you're beyond fixing?<br>What are you still punishing yourself for? |
| 6 | 1-15 to 1-16 | Pick something long-meant; notice what keeps you wishing instead of starting | **1-16** | Quit wishing. Take one step. Now.<br>Name it. Catch the stall. Start small.<br>Less dreaming. More doing. Today.<br>Wishing it, or doing it?<br>What's really stopping you? |
| 7 | 1-17 to 1-19 | Revisit the intention you began with; say it again and mean it | **1-19** | Why you started — say it. Mean it. Keep it.<br>Look back. Reclaim it. Move forward.<br>Remember the spark. Fan it. Carry on.<br>Has it slipped into autopilot?<br>Do you still mean it? |
| 8 | 1-20 to 1-22 | Wish clearly for one person going through something hard: may you be free | **1-21** | One face. One wish. Make it count.<br>Picture them. Wish hard. Let it land.<br>See the struggle. Send the wish. Release.<br>Whose pain will you hold today?<br>Who needs your wish most right now? |
| 9 | 1-23 to 1-25 | Take one wish all the way: may you be completely free | **1-23** | Don't stop at "feel better." Push on. Free them.<br>Wish wide. Wish deep. Leave no one out.<br>Start the wish. | 10 | 1-26 to 1-28 | When a comfort leaves you worse, name what you actually wanted | **1-28** | Reaching again? Stop. Name the real hunger.<br>Catch the grab. Pause. | 11 | 1-29 to 1-30 | Recall someone who helped you see clearly; feel grateful | **1-30** | One clear voice. Remember it. Say thanks.<br>Recall who woke you. Honor them. | 12 | 1-31 to 1-32 | After a kind act, notice what you were hoping for in return; just see it | **1-31** | Give freely. Then catch the hook.<br>Do the kindness. Watch the wanting. | 13 | 1-33 to 1-34 | Catch irritation or contempt early, before it builds; let it pass | **1-34** | Feel it rise. Starve it. Let it pass.<br>Catch the spark. Don't fan it. Release.<br>Spot the contempt. Hold steady. Let go.<br>Will you feed it or let it fade?<br>Can you catch it before it grows? |
| 14 | 1-35 to 1-36 | Bring to mind someone who meets unkindness with patience; admire them | **1-35** | Struck, yet calm. Look. Learn.<br>Find the patient one. Pause. Admire.<br>Harm met with grace. Notice it. Honor it.<br>Could you stay kind under attack?<br>Who answers harm without anger? |
