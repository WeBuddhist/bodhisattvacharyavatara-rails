## Today's Verse

> Just as Manjushri and the other great bodhisattvas once made offerings to the buddhas, I now make the very same offerings to the buddhas and their heirs. ^2-22

> With endless songs of praise, I praise these beings whose good qualities run as deep as the ocean. May clouds of beautiful praise rise up before them, again and again, forever. ^2-23

> I bow down before every buddha of the past, present, and future, and before the dharma and the whole sangha. I bow with as many bodies as there are specks of dust in the universe. ^2-24

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on verses 22 through 24 from the second chapter of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. It teaches us how to follow the example of great Bodhisattvas like Manjushri and Samantabhadra by offering supreme mental worship, singing heartfelt praises to the ocean of spiritual qualities, and prostrating with bodies as numerous as all the dust particles in the universe. This practice inspires us to cultivate deep devotion, gratitude, and humility.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

These verses explain three key practices: supreme offerings through visualization, songs of praise, and respectful prostrations. Master Shantideva explains that just as great, highly realized Bodhisattvas like Manjushri and Samantabhadra use the power of their deep meditation to create vast clouds of offerings that fill the sky, we too can mentally imagine making these supreme offerings to the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas.

Next, recognizing that the qualities of the Three Jewels (Buddha, Dharma, and Sangha) are as vast as an endless ocean, Shantideva offers melodic songs of praise to honor their wisdom and compassion. Finally, he bows down to all the Buddhas of the past, present, and future, as well as the Holy Dharma and the Sangha, visualizing his body multiplying into as many bodies as there are particles of dust in the entire universe. Spiritual teachers explain that praising and prostrating in this way crushes our inner pride and opens our minds to receive immense positive energy and wisdom.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, recalling the qualities of the Three Jewels, I will bow with deep devotion and show genuine respect and politeness to everyone around me.

**Explanation:** _(Doing good)_ Usually, due to our inner pride or self-centered habits, we forget to show respect to others and fail to appreciate good qualities in those around us. Today, following the teachings of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_ to "bow with bodies as numerous as atoms in the world," I will reflect on the ocean-like virtues of the Three Jewels and bow to them with body, speech, and mind. Furthermore, I will drop my ego and treat the people around me with kindness, polite words, and sincere respect. This simple practice cleanses our pride and gathers a vast store of positive energy.
