## Today's Verse

> When speaking, one should be confident, connected,
> clear, and pleasing.
> One should speak gently and appropriately,
> having abandoned attachment and hatred. ^5-79

> When looking at beings with one's eyes,
> one should look with sincerity and loving-kindness,
> thinking, "Through relying on these very beings,
> I will attain buddhahood." ^5-80

> Always inspired by aspiration,
> or inspired by the antidote,
> virtue and benefit arise in those who suffer,
> and great good deeds are generated. ^5-81

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the seventy-ninth, eightieth, and eighty-first verses of the fifth chapter. We train to look at others with loving eyes and to speak gentle words.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable example, which is easy to understand and encouraging. For example, a person tormented by thirst feels cooled by drinking cold water. In the same way, we think with an honest mind, "Relying on these very beings, I will become a buddha." Like a mother who sees her child, we look with love. We must not look down on others, belittle them, or despise them. We must look in an honest and loving way.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, every being I see, I will look at them with loving and honest eyes.

**Explanation:** _(Doing good)_ Relying on these beings, we ourselves will become buddhas. So we must not belittle anyone. We must look at them with loving eyes, as a mother looks at her child. If we do this, great loving-kindness will arise in our mind.
