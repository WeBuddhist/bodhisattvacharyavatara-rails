## Combined Commentary Summaries

The commentators unanimously read this verse as a second illustrative analogy, parallel to the illness analogy of the previous verses: just as one is careful even near an ordinary, shallow cliff where a fall would cause only minor injury, one should be far more vigilant near the "abyss" of the lower realms, where a fall — specifically into hells such as Avīci — results in suffering that lasts for an immense span of time.

Gyaltsab and Sazang both specify the abyss as tens of thousands of leagues deep, with suffering lasting an intermediate eon or more, while Thokme simply stresses that "a thousand leagues" signifies an especially great abyss. All three agree that this analogy is meant to spur diligence in relying on the antidote to the afflictions.
