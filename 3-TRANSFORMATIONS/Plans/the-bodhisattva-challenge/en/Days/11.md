# Day 11 — Bodhisattvacharyavatara Practice Plan

## Chapter 1: Benefits of Bodhicitta — Verses 1-31 to 1-33

---

### 1. Refuge & Bodhicitta (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ་སེམས་བསྐྱེད།)

#### **1. Refuge (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ།)**

> *Until I reach the heart of awakening,*
> *I take refuge in the Buddhas.*
> *Likewise, I take refuge in the Dharma*
> *And in the assembly of Bodhisattvas.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྙིང་པོར་མཆིས་ཀྱི་བར། །
> སངས་རྒྱས་རྣམས་ལ་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །
> ཆོས་དང་བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་དཔའ་ཡི། །
> ཚོགས་ལའང་དེ་བཞིན་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །

---

### 2. Benefit (ཕན་ཡོན།)

Based on the traditional commentaries of Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, Sazang Mati Panchen, and Ngulchu Thokme Zangpo, practicing and reflecting on today's verses yields the following profound benefits:

*   **Letting Go of Selfishness (Pure Giving):** The Heroes of Enlightenment give freely to all beings. They do not want anything in return. Reflecting on this helps you slowly let go of selfish habits and become more open-hearted.

*   **Finding Deep Motivation (Going Beyond the Surface):** Today's verses show that ordinary kindness is limited. Real kindness reaches all beings without stopping. Seeing this difference gives you stronger motivation to grow on the spiritual path.

*   **Growing Respect for Others (Recognizing True Worth):** These verses show how worthy the Heroes of Enlightenment truly are. Thinking about this deeply helps you build a sincere sense of respect and appreciation for those who work to help all beings.

---

### 3. Today's Root Verses & Commentaries (དེ་རིང་གི་སྤྱོད་འཇུག་རྩ་ཚིག་དངོས།)

#### **Verse 1-31**

> ཕན་བཏགས་ལན་ལྡོན་གང་ཡིན་པ། །
> དེ་ཡང་རེ་ཞིག་བསྔགས་འོས་ན། །
> མ་བཅོལ་ལེགས་པར་བྱེད་པ་ཡི། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་དཔའ་སྨོས་ཅི་དགོས། །
>
> *We praise those who merely repay a favor. What need is there to speak of the Hero of Enlightenment, who does good unsolicited?*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-31**

In ordinary life, a person who repays a kindness is already seen as good and worthy of praise. The Hero of Enlightenment, however, never waits to be asked. Without being prompted, and moved only by great compassion, the Hero of Enlightenment steps forward to help all beings, seeking nothing in return. This shows that the Hero of Enlightenment is far more praiseworthy than any ordinary benefactor could ever be.

---

#### **Verse 1-32**

> འགྲོ་བ་ཉུང་ཟད་ནར་མའི་ཟས་སྦྱོར་བ། །
> སྐད་ཅིག་ཟས་ཙམ་སྦྱིན་པར་བྱེད་པ་དང་། །
> བརྙས་བཅས་ཉིན་ཕྱེད་འགྲངས་པར་བྱེད་པ་ཡང་། །
> དགེ་བ་བྱེད་པ་ཡིན་ཞེས་སྐྱེ་བོས་བཀུར། །
>
> *People honor a person as a virtuous benefactor for giving a single meal to a few beings. They do this even when the food is given with disrespect, satisfying them for only half a day.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-32**

This verse points out how little is needed for the world to call someone generous. A person gives plain food to a small group of people, treats them with disrespect, and the benefit lasts only half a day — yet society still praises that person as a great giver. The Hero of Enlightenment, by contrast, gives the highest happiness to countless beings, with deep care and respect, for as long as space exists. If ordinary, flawed kindness earns praise, the Hero of Enlightenment is beyond all comparison.

#### **Verse 1-33**

> སེམས་ཅན་གྲངས་མཐའ་ཡས་ལ་དུས་རིང་དུ། །
> བདེ་བར་གཤེགས་ཀྱི་བདེ་བ་བླ་ན་མེད། །
> ཡིད་ལ་བསམ་པ་མཐའ་དག་རྫོགས་བྱེད་པ། །
> རྟག་ཏུ་སྦྱིན་པ་ལྟ་ཞིག་སྨོས་ཅི་དགོས། །
>
> *What need is there to speak of the Heroes of Enlightenment? They give the supreme bliss of the Blissful Ones to countless beings, fulfilling every wish in their minds.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-33**

The Heroes of Enlightenment give the highest spiritual happiness — not ordinary food or comfort — to a limitless number of beings. They do this with complete respect, fulfilling every wish that beings have in their hearts. This giving never stops; it continues for as long as space remains and living beings exist. No worldly benefactor can come close to this kind of infinite, pure, and unending generosity.

### 4. Daily Life Application (ཉིན་རེའི་འཚོ་བའི་ནང་ཉམས་སུ་ལེན་ཚུལ།)

*   **1. Help Without Being Asked:** Today, I will look for one chance to help someone before they ask me. I will do it quietly, without wanting praise or thanks in return.

*   **2. Notice My Limits and Expand Them:** When I feel reluctant to help someone, I will remember the Hero of Enlightenment who helps all beings without limit. I will use this as a reminder to widen my own care, even just a little.

*   **3. Reflect on True Generosity:** In the evening, I will spend a few minutes thinking about what I gave today. I will ask myself: did I give with a warm heart, or was there some selfishness mixed in? I will use this honest look to improve tomorrow.

### 5. Dedication & Aspiration (བསྔོ་བ་དང་སྨོན་ལམ།)

#### **1. Dedication (བསྔོ་བ།)**

> *By the virtue accumulated*
> *Through composing this entry into the Bodhisattva conduct,*
> *May all wandering beings without exception*
> *Engage in the Bodhisattva conduct.*

> བདག་གིས་བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་པ་ལ། །
> འཇུག་པ་རྣམར་བར་བརྩམས་པ་ཡི། །
> དགེ་བ་གང་དེས་འགྲོ་བ་ཀུན། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་ལ་འཇུག་པར་ཤོག །

#### **2. Aspiration (སྨོན་ལམ།)**

> *May the precious and supreme mind of awakening,*
> *Where it has not arisen, arise;*
> *Where it has arisen, may it not decline,*
> *But increase further and further.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་མཆོག་རིན་པོ་ཆེ། །
> མ་སྐྱེས་པ་རྣམས་སྐྱེ་གྱུར་ཅིག །
> སྐྱེས་པ་ཉམས་པ་མེད་པ་དང་། །
> གོང་ནས་གོང་དུ་འཕེལ་བར་ཤོག །

### 6. Key Terms Glossary (གནད་ཚིག་ཁག་གི་འགྲེལ་བཤད།)

*   **Hero of Enlightenment (བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་དཔའ།):** This term refers to someone who has made a deep commitment to reach full awakening for the sake of all living beings. In today's verses, the Hero of Enlightenment is described as one who acts without being asked, moved only by compassion and the wish to help every being without exception.

*   **Unsolicited Action (མ་བཅོལ་བ།):** This means helping others without waiting to be asked. The great teacher Śāntideva uses this quality to show why the Hero of Enlightenment is far greater than any ordinary benefactor. Acting without being prompted is a sign of truly pure and selfless care.

*   **Supreme Bliss of the Blissful Ones (བདེ་བར་གཤེགས་ཀྱི་བདེ་བ་བླ་ན་མེད།):** This phrase points to the highest form of happiness — the lasting, stainless happiness of full awakening. Unlike the temporary satisfaction of food or comfort, this bliss is complete and never fades. The Heroes of Enlightenment work to give this ultimate happiness to all beings.

*   **Fulfilling Every Wish (ཡིད་ལ་བསམ་པ་མཐའ་དག་རྫོགས་བྱེད།):** This means completely satisfying everything that a being hopes for or needs at the deepest level. The Hero of Enlightenment does not just meet surface needs — the wish is to bring every being to full happiness and freedom from suffering.

*   **Always / Constantly (རྟག་ཏུ།):** In today's verses, this word means that the giving of the Heroes of Enlightenment never stops. Traditional explanations say it means "as long as space remains and living beings exist." This is not a temporary gift — it is an eternal and inexhaustible commitment to all beings.
