## Combined Commentary Summaries

All three commentators highlight the stark image of dying alone: not only wealth but life itself, along with all relatives and friends, must eventually be left behind, and one must go on alone to an uncertain destination.

Given this, they agree there is no real point in the attachment to loved ones or the aversion to the unloved that drives so much wrongdoing. Sazang and Thokme both state directly that clinging to such distinctions is "pointless" or accomplishes "nothing," while Gyaltsab emphasizes there is "not the slightest benefit" in it — all three treating this as the conclusion of the reflections on impermanence begun with the two preceding verses.
