# Day 20 — A scent that travels further than you can see

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Do one quiet good thing today that no one will notice
> **Detail:** Shantideva anoints the buddhas with a scent that fills the world. Today, trust that a small good act carries.

## Opening

Today's offering is a fragrance. In his mind, Shantideva anoints the bodies of the buddhas, already glowing like polished gold, with the finest scent, and that scent drifts out until it fills countless worlds. One small anointing, and the whole of space turns sweet.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

We tend to think a kind act only matters if someone sees it and thanks us for it.

The verse anoints the buddhas with a scent, and Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, a fifteenth-century teacher, dwells on where the scent goes: out from one body until it fills a whole universe of worlds. A scent does not need to be watched to do its work. It simply travels, and people far off catch it without knowing the source. Your good conduct moves the same way. A patient word, an honest choice, a small kindness done where no one is looking, these reach further than you will ever see, and the people they reach are mostly strangers to you.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Do one good thing today that no one will notice: clean up something that isn't yours, be patient when you could snap, tell a small truth. Then let it go without checking who saw. Trust that, like a scent, it carries further than you can see.
