## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the thirty-ninth, fortieth, and forty-first verses of the fifth chapter. This teaching clearly shows that we must tie the wild elephant of the mind to the great pillar of the sacred teachings with the rope of mindfulness.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on this verse give us an example that is easy to understand and encouraging.

Our own mind, when it falls under the power of attachment and hatred, is like a wild elephant. Just as a wild elephant, if it slips free from its bindings, causes great harm, so a distracted mind causes the harm of the lower realms, such as hell and the hungry-ghost realm.

The sacred teachings are like a great pillar. Mindfulness is like the rope that binds the elephant of the mind to that great pillar of the teachings. Awareness is like the watchman who continually checks whether the rope has come loose or the mind has slipped away. So we must tie the mind with mindfulness, so it does not wander outward, and guard it with awareness.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will tie my wild mind with mindfulness to a good focus, and I will continually check it with awareness.

**Explanation:** _(Meditation Practice)_ A mind maddened by the afflictions, like a wild elephant, wanders aimlessly toward the objects of desire, without any real direction. So we must tie it firmly with the rope of mindfulness to the pillar of reflecting on the sacred teachings, and watch over it again and again with the eye of awareness. By practicing this way, the restless mind becomes calm and does not slip away from its good focus, and we gain firm concentration.
