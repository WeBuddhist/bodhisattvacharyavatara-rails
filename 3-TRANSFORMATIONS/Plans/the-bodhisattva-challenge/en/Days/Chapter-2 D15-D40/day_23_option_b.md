# Day 23 — A kindness that doesn't stop

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Set one small good thing to keep going today
> **Detail:** Shantideva wishes for a rain of offerings that never stops. Today, start one kindness that continues.

## Opening

Today Shantideva does not offer a single thing and finish. He wishes for a rain, of jewels, of flowers, of every good thing, to fall on the dharma, the stupas, and the sacred images, and to keep falling without a single break.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

Most of our good intentions run on mood. We are generous when we feel like it, and the rest of the time the giving quietly stops.

The verse asks for something steadier: a rain that falls without interruption. Ngulchu Thokme, a fourteenth-century teacher, pictures it pouring on the scriptures, the stupas, and the images of the buddhas, and the key word in his reading is unceasing. Shantideva is not offering once; he is setting a wish in motion that keeps going on its own. There is a plain lesson in it. A small kindness you keep up, day after day, does more good than a grand one you do once and drop. And the steadiness feeds back: a giving that does not stop leaves a quiet, settled gladness in you, and a faith that no longer wavers with your mood.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Choose one small good thing you can keep doing, not just today but on the ordinary days too: a daily word of thanks, a tidy you always leave behind, a moment of patience you return to. Start it today, and let it be something that continues whether or not you feel like it.
