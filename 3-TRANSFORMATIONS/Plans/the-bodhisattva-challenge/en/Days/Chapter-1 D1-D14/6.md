# Day 6 — Wanting to go is not the same as going

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Notice what you keep meaning to start
> **Detail:** There are two kinds of this mind: wishing, and setting out. Today, notice the gap.

## Opening

Today's two verses sort the awakening mind into two kinds. The first is the wish to awaken. The second is actually setting out to do it. Shantideva gives a simple comparison: wanting to take a trip is not the same as getting up and going. Both matter, but they are not the same, and it helps to know which one you are in.

## From the Tradition

**Gyaltsab Je**, a fifteenth-century teacher, points out something easy to miss. The two minds want the very same thing. Both want to awaken for the sake of others. They are not divided by what they hope for.

What divides them is whether the wish has joined with action. One person means it and stays where they are. The other means it and starts to walk. The thought can be identical; only the moving is different.
