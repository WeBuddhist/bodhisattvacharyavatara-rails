# Day 28 — The friend, the enemy, and the self all go

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Let one grudge sit a little lighter
> **Detail:** At death the friend, the enemy, and the self all cease. Today, soften one grudge.

## Opening

We spend a lot of effort sorting people into those we protect and those we resist. Today's three verses cut under that. Death comes for everyone without warning, and when it does we go on alone, leaving all of them behind. The friends, the enemies, and the self that divided them all cease to be.

> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.

> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>

> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

Most of us carry two lists without thinking about it: people we look after, and people we hold something against. A lot of what we do, good and bad, is done on behalf of one list or the other. **Ngulchu Thokme**, a fourteenth-century teacher, points to what the verse does to both lists. At death we leave every person on them and go on alone. The friend, the enemy, and the self that drew the line between them all cease to be. So the wrongs we commit to help one side and harm the other are done for a sorting that does not last. Seen clearly, he says, clinging to either list is effort spent on nothing.

## Today's Practice

If a grudge comes to mind today, someone you have quietly filed under "against me", hold it beside one plain fact: one day both of you will be gone. You do not have to forgive on the spot. Just let the grudge sit a little lighter for a moment.
