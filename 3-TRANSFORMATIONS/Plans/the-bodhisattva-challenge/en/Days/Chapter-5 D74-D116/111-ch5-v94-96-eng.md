## Today's Verse

> One should not indicate with a finger;
> with respect, one should use the right hand.
> Everyone should likewise
> show the way in that manner. ^5-94

> One should not move the hand excessively,
> but move it slightly and make a sound.
> By signaling with snapping fingers and so forth,
> otherwise, one will be unrestrained. ^5-95

> One should lie down facing the desired direction,
> like the Protector resting in nirvana.
> One should immediately apply awareness
> to the resolve to rise quickly. ^5-96

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the ninety-fourth, ninety-fifth, and ninety-sixth verses of the fifth chapter. Today, we learn the conduct of respectfully stretching out the right hand when we show the way to others.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. We must lie down on our right side, like the Protector resting in nirvana. We must cover our own body with the Dharma robe and lie facing the direction we want. Then, with awareness, we must give rise to the thought, "I will rise quickly." We must definitely apply this thought at the very beginning of lying down.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, at night, I will lie on my right side and definitely hold the thought of rising quickly, with awareness.

**Explanation:** _(Diligence Practice)_ If we lie on our right side, like the Buddha's posture when he passed into nirvana, our awareness does not wander. If we hold the thought of rising quickly at the very beginning of lying down, we will rise quickly on time.
