# Day 10 — Destroying their own happiness like an enemy

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Reaching for relief that leaves you worse?
> **Detail:** Today's reading: how beings chase happiness but, out of confusion, destroy it (the moth flying into the flame). Practice: next time you reach for something that soothes then stings, scrolling, eating past full, a cutting word, pause and name what you actually wanted.

## Opening

Chapter 1 of the Bodhisattvacharyavatara has been building one case for bodhicitta — the aspiration to awaken for all beings. These two verses complete it. The first makes the highest claim yet about bodhicitta's value; the second reveals what makes this mind so urgently needed.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

*MIND TRAINING (recite 3 times)*

> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in immeasurable equanimity, free from attachment and aversion.

*REFUGE (recite 3 times)*

> Until I reach the heart of awakening, I take refuge in the Buddha, the Dharma, and the Sangha.

*TAKING THE BODHISATTVA VOW (recite 3 times)*

> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And trained step by step in the bodhisattva conduct,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And train step by step in that same conduct.

## Today's Verses

> ཕན་པར་བསམས་པ་ཙམ་གྱིས་ཀྱང་། །
> སངས་རྒྱས་མཆོད་ལས་ཁྱད་འཕགས་ན། །
> སེམས་ཅན་མ་ལུས་ཐམས་ཅད་ཀྱི། །
> བདེ་དོན་བརྩོན་པ་སྨོས་ཅི་དགོས། །
>
> Merely wishing to benefit others is far superior to making offerings to the Buddhas. What need is there to speak of striving for the happiness of all sentient beings without exception?

> སྡུག་བསྔལ་འདོར་འདོད་སེམས་ཡོད་ཀྱང་། །
> སྡུག་བསྔལ་ཉིད་ལ་མངོན་པར་རྒྱུག །
> བདེ་བ་འདོད་ཀྱང་གཏི་མུག་པས། །
> རང་གི་བདེ་བ་དགྲ་ལྟར་འཇོམས། །
>
> Although beings wish to abandon suffering, they run directly into suffering itself. Although they desire happiness, out of ignorance they destroy their own happiness like an enemy.

## From the Tradition

Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen identifies three functions of bodhicitta that follow directly from these verses: satisfying beings with happiness, cutting suffering, and — most deeply — dispelling the confusion about what causes what. The third is the least obvious and, in his reading, the most important. Beings are not confused about what they want. What they lack is knowledge of causes — specifically, that non-virtuous action causes suffering, not happiness. Sazang Mati Panchen gives this a single image: a moth flying into fire. Not self-destructive by intention, simply confused about what it is doing. This is the condition bodhicitta addresses at its root: not only delivering happiness or removing suffering, but showing the path clearly enough that beings can stop reaching for the wrong things.

## Aspiration

> May the precious and supreme bodhicitta —
> Where it has not yet arisen, may it arise;
> Where it has arisen, may it not decline,
> But grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of this practice,
> May all wandering beings, without exception,
> Enter the path of the bodhisattva.

## Today's Practice Challenge

The next time you do something that offers momentary relief but leaves you worse — eating past hunger, scrolling past the point of rest, saying something cutting when you wanted connection — pause and name what you were actually seeking. Ngulchu Thokme reads this verse as a teaching about confused means, not bad intention: the wanting was real, but the reach was wrong. The moth doesn't want to burn.
