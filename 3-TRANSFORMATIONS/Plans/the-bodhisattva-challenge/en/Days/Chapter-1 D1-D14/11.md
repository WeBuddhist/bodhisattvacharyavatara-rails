# Day 11 — The friend who helps you see clearly

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Thank someone who helped you see clearly
> **Detail:** The deepest help isn't comfort, but clarity. Today, remember someone who gave you that.

## Opening

Today's two verses name three things this mind does for people who are struggling. It brings them happiness. It takes away their pain. And it clears away their confusion. Then the verses ask: where is there anything, or anyone, equal to that?

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

>
> It also dispels their ignorance. Where is there a virtue equal to this? Where is there such a spiritual friend, and where is there such merit?

## From the Tradition

These two verses show, in a way that is easy for ordinary people to understand, three wonderful qualities of the bodhisattva who removes the troubles of beings.

Beings want happiness but do not have it; with great love, the bodhisattva fills them with every kind of happiness, both for now and for the long run. Beings do not want suffering but have so much of it; with great compassion, the bodhisattva cuts away all the suffering of birth, ageing, sickness, and death, right at the root. And for people who do not know what to take up and what to give up when it comes to the causes of happiness and suffering, the bodhisattva, with great wisdom, clears away the darkness of their confusion.

What other good deed could equal the awakening mind, which helps beings through these three: love, compassion, and wisdom? There is no better friend, and no greater merit, anywhere.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Bring to mind someone who once helped you see something clearly, not just someone who comforted you, but someone who helped you understand what was really going on. Take a moment to feel grateful for them.
