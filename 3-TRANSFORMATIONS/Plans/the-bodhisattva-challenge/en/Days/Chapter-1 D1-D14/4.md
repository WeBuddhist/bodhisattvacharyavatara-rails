# Day 4 — The moment bodhicitta is born, everything changes

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Feeling stuck in the same pattern again?
> **Detail:** Today's reading: the instant bodhicitta arises, an ordinary being becomes an heir of the buddhas. Practice: when you feel stuck today, quietly wish: may I be free of this, and everyone caught in something like it too.

## Introduction

Today's verses move from argument to image. Shantideva stops making the case for bodhicitta and starts showing what it actually does: the moment it arises, your spiritual status changes instantly; this impure body becomes the raw material for a Buddha's body; and the mind has been examined and declared priceless by the only authority capable of knowing its worth.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

*MIND TRAINING (recite 3 times)*

> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in immeasurable equanimity, free from attachment and aversion.

*REFUGE (recite 3 times)*

> Until I reach the heart of awakening, I take refuge in the Buddha, the Dharma, and the Sangha.

*TAKING THE BODHISATTVA VOW (recite 3 times)*

> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And trained step by step in the bodhisattva conduct,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And train step by step in that same conduct.

## From the Tradition

Gyaltsab Je pauses on the word "instant" in verse 1.9. This transformation, he explains, is not gradual and not reserved for advanced practitioners. Ordinary beings — still fully caught in samsara — who generate the conventional mind of enlightenment for the very first time receive the name and status of heir of the Blissful Ones in that same moment. The paradox he highlights is striking: such a person remains physically bound by karma and afflictions in the prison of cyclic existence, and yet their spiritual status immediately exceeds that of the gods and humans who now bow to them.

## Aspiration

> May the precious and supreme bodhicitta —
> Where it has not yet arisen, may it arise;
> Where it has arisen, may it not decline,
> But grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of this practice,
> May all wandering beings, without exception,
> Enter the path of the bodhisattva.

## Today's Practice Challenge

Sazang describes the person in verse 1.9 as "bound by the fetters of karma and afflictions" — and in the very same instant, elevated above gods and humans. The transformation hinges entirely on the generation of the wish. Today, when you feel stuck or caught in the same pattern again, try actually forming it — not as a recitation but as a real intention: may I be free from this, and may everyone else caught in something similar be free too.
