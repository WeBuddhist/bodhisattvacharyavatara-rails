# Day 1 — Bodhisattvacharyavatara Practice Plan

## Chapter 1: The Benefits of Bodhicitta — Verses 1-1 to 1-3

---

### 1. Refuge 

#### **1. Refuge (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ།)**

> *Until I reach the heart of awakening,*
> *I take refuge in the Buddhas.*
> *Likewise, I take refuge in the Dharma*
> *And in the assembly of Bodhisattvas.*

---

### 2. Benefit (ཕན་ཡོན།)

Based on the traditional commentaries of Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, Sazang Mati Panchen, and Ngulchu Thokme Zangpo, practicing and reflecting on today's verses yields the following profound benefits:

*   **Obstacle Pacification (Spiritual Progress):** Paying homage and taking refuge in the Three Jewels aligns our mind with the lineage of awakening. This devotion clears away mental obstacles in our daily practice. It also generates vast positive energy for our spiritual journey. This practice establishes a firm foundation for our ultimate awakening.
*   **Humility and Pride Reduction (Mind Cultivation):** Reflecting on the author's deep humility helps us overcome intellectual pride and arrogance. By acknowledging our own limitations, we develop a gentle and receptive mind. This open attitude makes us ready to learn from others. We cultivate a receptive heart that is ready to absorb the instructions.
*   **Self-Transformation (Mind Habituation):** Studying the teachings for self-transformation rather than worldly fame keeps our path sincere. Writing down and reflecting on these truths helps habituate our minds with virtue. This practice prevents our spiritual growth from declining over time. It also inspires other practitioners of equal fortune to join us.

### 3. Today's Root Verses & Commentaries (དེ་རིང་གི་སྤྱོད་འཇུག་རྩ་ཚིག་དངོས།)

#### **Verse 1-1**
> བདེ་གཤེགས་ཆོས་ཀྱི་སྐུ་མངའ་སྲས་བཅས་དང་། །
> ཕྱག་འོས་ཀུན་ལའང་གུས་པར་ཕྱག་འཚལ་ཏེ། །
> བདེ་གཤེགས་སྲས་ཀྱི་སྡོམ་ལ་འཇུག་པ་ནི། །
> ལུང་བཞིན་མདོར་བསྡུས་ནས་ནི་བརྗོད་པར་བྱ། ། ^1-1
> 
> *Paying homage with reverence to the Sugatas — the Dharmakāya and their heirs — and to all who are worthy of veneration, I shall now briefly describe, in accordance with scripture, the practice of entering the discipline of the Sugatas' heirs.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-1**
The author pays homage to the Buddhas, the Truth Body, and their spiritual community. This act of devotion purifies mental obstacles and gathers positive power for writing the text. It also introduces his main purpose of summarizing the scriptures on the ethical vows of a child of the Buddhas.

#### **Verse 1-2**
> སྔོན་ཆད་མ་བྱུང་བ་ཡང་འདིར་བརྗོད་མེད། །
> སྡེབ་སྦྱོར་མཁས་པའང་བདག་ལ་ཡོད་མིན་ཏེ། །
> དེ་ཕྱིར་གཞན་དོན་བསམ་པ་བདག་ལ་མེད། །
> རང་གི་ཡིད་ལ་བསྒོམ་ཕྱིར་ངས་འདི་བརྩམས། ། ^1-2
> 
> *What is written here has not been said before, nor do I possess any skill in composition. Therefore I have no thought of benefiting others — I composed this solely to cultivate it in my own mind.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-2**
The author expresses deep humility by denying any claim to original ideas or beautiful writing style. This modest attitude helps him overcome pride and intellectual arrogance before his peers. He writes this text primarily to train his own mind and prevent his spiritual growth from declining.

#### **Verse 1-3**
> དགེ་བ་བསྒོམ་ཕྱིར་བདག་གི་དད་པའི་ཤུགས། །
> འདི་དག་གིས་ཀྱང་རེ་ཞིག་འཕེལ་འགྱུར་ལ། །
> བདག་དང་སྐལ་བ་མཉམ་པ་གཞན་གྱིས་ཀྱང་། །
> ཅི་སྟེ་འདི་དག་མཐོང་ན་དོན་ཡོད་འགྱུར། ། ^1-3
> 
> *Through these writings, my faith in virtue may grow stronger for a while, and should another of equal fortune encounter this, perhaps it may also be of benefit to them.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-3**
Writing down these reflections acts as a powerful support that increases the author's own faith and virtue. He hopes that other practitioners with similar aspirations might also find these words useful. This potential to help others represents a secondary, long-term goal that inspires students to engage with the teachings.

### 4. Daily Life Application (ཉིན་རེའི་འཚོ་བའི་ནང་ཉམས་སུ་ལེན་ཚུལ།)

*   **1. Commencing Tasks with Devotion:** Before starting your work, study, or meditation today, pause to pay silent respect to your teachers. This simple act establishes a highly respectful mindset and aligns your daily intentions with positive outcomes.
*   **2. Practicing Intellectual Humility:** When sharing your knowledge or working with others today, actively set aside any urge to show off. Remind yourself of your own human limitations and state your ideas to others with genuine modesty.
*   **3. Studying for Inner Growth:** Focus your reading or practice today entirely on your own inner growth rather than seeking praise. Let your main goal be the active and continuous familiarization of your own mind with virtue.

---

### 5. Dedication & Aspiration (བསྔོ་བ་དང་སྨོན་ལམ།)

#### **1. Dedication (བསྔོ་བ།)**

> *By the virtue accumulated*
> *Through composing this entry into the Bodhisattva conduct,*
> *May all wandering beings without exception*
> *Engage in the Bodhisattva conduct.*

#### **2. Aspiration (སྨོན་ལམ།)**

> *May the precious and supreme mind of awakening,*
> *Where it has not arisen, arise;*
> *Where it has arisen, may it not decline,*
> *But increase further and further.*

### 6. Key Terms Glossary (གནད་ཚིག་ཁག་གི་འགྲེལ་བཤད།)

*   **The Blissful Ones (བདེ་གཤེགས།):** This term refers directly to the Buddhas who have crossed the ocean of cyclic existence. Commentators explain this state as having perfectly abandoned all mental defilements and negative habits. They have also directly and completely realized the ultimate nature of all conventional reality.
*   **Truth Body (ཆོས་ཀྱི་སྐུ།):** This term represents the ultimate and naturally pure nature of a Buddha's mind. It remains completely pure and free from all temporary stains of ignorance. This body serves as the ultimate source of all wisdom and teachings.
*   **Commitments (སྡོམ་པ།):** This term refers directly to the ethical promises and trainings of a Hero of Enlightenment. It encompasses practicing the six perfections and avoiding any actions that harm other beings. These vows guide the practitioner toward the achievement of complete and ultimate spiritual perfection.
*   **Goodness (དགེ་བ།):** This term refers to helpful deeds and positive mental states like faith and compassion. Cultivating goodness purifies our mind and creates the necessary conditions for spiritual progress. It acts as the primary cause for both temporary happiness and ultimate awakening.
