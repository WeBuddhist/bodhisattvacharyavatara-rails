## Today's Verse

> Just as the buddhas of the past
> generated the Mind of Awakening,
> and remained in the bodhisattva training
> in that sequence, ^3-22

> likewise, for the benefit of beings,
> I will generate the Mind of Awakening.
> And likewise, I will train
> in the training in that sequence. ^3-23

> In this way, the wise one,
> having taken up the excellent Mind of Awakening,
> strengthens the mind as follows
> to also cause it to flourish: ^3-24

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the three verses 22 to 24 from the third chapter of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. Following the buddhas of the past, today the time has come to actually generate bodhicitta and to take the bodhisattva vow.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses tell us a story that is easy to understand and encouraging.

As Khenpo Kunzang Palden says, when we take up bodhicitta we should take the buddhas of the past as our example.

Long ago, when our teacher Shakyamuni was the potter's son called Nangje, he generated bodhicitta before the Tathagata Great Shakyamuni. In the same way the monk Akshobhya generated bodhicitta before Buddha Great Eye. The Blessed One Amitabha, the Medicine Buddha and others also did the same, before the buddhas of the past.

At first they too were beginners, just as we are. But they trained in the bodhisattva trainings step by step, from beginner all the way up to the level of a buddha. That is how they became buddhas. That is why we too can take them as our example, generate bodhicitta, and practise.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today I will decide on one act that helps someone else, and do it.

**Explanation:** _(Doing good)_ This verse says that we should follow the buddhas and generate the mind that benefits others. Then we train in stages in what is to be learned. If today we do one small act that helps someone, our own mind grows better. We can move one step forward on the bodhisattva path.
