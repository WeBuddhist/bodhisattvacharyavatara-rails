# Registers for the Bodhicaryāvatāra Hindi versions (Devanagari)

## Academic (शैक्षणिक संस्करण)
- Readers: Hindi-reading students, practitioners and scholars of Buddhist texts.
- Modern standard Hindi in Devanagari; precise and faithful.
- Terminology: the established Sanskrit (tatsama) Buddhist terms used in Hindi Buddhist scholarship (सुगत, धर्मकाय, बोधिचित्त, बोधिसत्त्व, क्षण-सम्पदा, पारमिता, क्षान्ति, वीर्य, ध्यान, प्रज्ञा, स्मृति, सम्प्रजन्य, क्लेश, संवृति-सत्य, परमार्थ-सत्य); proper names in their Sanskrit form (शान्तिदेव, मञ्जुश्री, समन्तभद्र, अवलोकितेश्वर). Avoid Hindu-devotional or Urdu-Persian substitutes for technical terms.
- One locked rendering per term (per sense).

## Children's (बच्चों का संस्करण)
- Readers: children aged 8–12 reading Hindi.
- Simple everyday Hindi; short common words. A technical idea becomes a simple phrase that keeps the meaning, never a different idea.
- Names kept (बुद्ध, शान्तिदेव, मञ्जुश्री); technical Sanskrit compounds replaced by plain phrases.
- One rendering per term (per sense), used every time.
