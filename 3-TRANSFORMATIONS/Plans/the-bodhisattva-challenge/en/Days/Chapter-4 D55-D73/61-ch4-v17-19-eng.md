## Today's Verse

> With such conduct,
> I will not obtain a human body.
> If I do not obtain a human body,
> there will be only misdeeds and no good deeds. ^4-17

> If, when I have the fortune to practice virtue,
> I do not perform good deeds,
> and am bewildered by all the suffering of the lower realms,
> what will I be able to do then? ^4-18

> If I have not performed good deeds,
> and have accumulated misdeeds,
> even for a hundred million eons,
> I will not even hear the word "happy realms." ^4-19

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the three verses 17 to 19 from the fourth chapter of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. If we fall into the lower realms, we will not even hear the word "happy realms" for a hundred million eons. Let us think together about this frightening thought.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us a story that is easy to understand and encouraging.

Long ago, the naga king Sagara invited the Blessed Buddha into the ocean. He asked the Buddha this: "When I was born as a naga in the past, there were only a few nagas. Now there are so many that the ocean cannot hold them all. What is the reason for this?" The Buddha answered: "Whether bound by vows or not, it happens because people commit small wrongdoings and downfalls without care."

What does this story teach us? If we leave even small wrongdoing unwatched, it grows step by step, and we must take countless births in the lower realms. So we should do even small good actions, and be careful even with small wrongdoing.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, while I have the chance to do good, I will actually carry out one good action.

**Explanation:** _(Doing good)_ Right now we have a good chance to do good. If we do not do good now, then later, if we are born in the lower realms, regret will come but it will not help. So let us work at doing good today. This brings happiness in this life and in all our future lives.
