# Day 13 — A moment of admiration counts for more than you think

## Opening

Think of someone you truly look up to. A teacher, maybe. Or someone whose kindness you still remember.

For a moment, you feel it. Real respect.

Today's verses look at two feelings we can hold toward such a person. And what each one does to us.

## From the Tradition

*A short look from the old teachers.*

Yesterday the verse warned about anger. Now look at the other side.

You might think a bad thought and a good thought just cancel out. The old teachers say no. The good one counts for far more.

**Ngulchu Thokme** tells an old story to show how much more. Picture someone who gives sight back to every blind being in the world. A huge kindness. Now picture someone else who simply looks at a good-hearted person with real warmth. That quiet look, Thokme says, brings even greater good than giving back all that sight.

So you do not need to do something grand. **Sazang Mati Panchen** adds that one moment of true respect for a kind person outweighs the harm of an angry one.

Hold this today: **a warm thought counts for more than you think.**

## Today's Practice Challenge

**Your task today:**

Bring to mind one person whose goodness you respect. A teacher, a friend, anyone kind.

For a few seconds, **let yourself truly admire them.** Feel the warmth, and do not rush past it.
