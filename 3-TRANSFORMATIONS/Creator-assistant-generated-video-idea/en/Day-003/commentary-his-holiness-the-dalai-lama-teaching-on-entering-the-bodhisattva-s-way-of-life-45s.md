# Day 3 — Commentary — His Holiness the Dalai Lama (Teaching on Entering the Bodhisattva's Way of Life)

You ever feel like you are doing everything right, but it just is not adding up to real peace? We try to fix our lives one problem at a time, but the problems just keep coming back.

His Holiness the Dalai Lama, the world’s most famous Buddhist teacher, says the ancients looked at this differently. They spent eons searching for the one thing that actually works. And they landed on this: a mind that cares for others as much as itself. It sounds simple, but it is a superpower. When you stop obsessing over your own needs and genuinely wish others well, your whole world shifts. It is the shortcut to happiness. Send this to someone who is carrying way too much today.
