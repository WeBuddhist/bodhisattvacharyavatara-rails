## Today's Verse

> To all well-spoken words,
> one should say, "Well said!"
> If one sees someone performing good deeds,
> one should generate gladness with praise. ^5-75

> One should praise good qualities in secret,
> and if good qualities are praised, one should echo them.
> If one's own good qualities are praised,
> one should recognize them as good qualities. ^5-76

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the seventy-fifth and seventy-sixth verses of the fifth chapter. When you see the good actions of others, your words of praise make a great light shine in their minds.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable story, which is easy to understand and encouraging. Long ago, a lama said at a Dharma place, "Gwa, gya, and lba, these three are the ornament of a monk." At that time, an old monk had all three. He lifted his head a little and became happy. The lama knew this and said, "If all three are present, it is a bad omen." Because of this, the monk bowed his head, feeling deflated. This shows what is meant by "if one's own good qualities are praised."

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, when I see someone doing a good action, I will praise them face to face.

**Explanation:** _(Doing good)_ When we see others do good work and praise them without jealousy, joy arises in their minds. This also increases our own great merit. This is an important practice taught in the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_.
