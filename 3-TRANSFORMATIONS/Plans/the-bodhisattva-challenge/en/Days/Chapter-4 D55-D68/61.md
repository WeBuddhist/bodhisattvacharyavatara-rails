# Day 61 — When virtue has no name

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Notice that you can still choose — that is today's practice
> **Detail:** Today's verses describe a state where the option to do good simply disappears. You are not in that state right now.

## Opening

Picture a hawk circling overhead. It is not thinking about right or wrong. It is hungry, and it will kill something before evening. It is not an evil creature. It simply cannot do otherwise. Today's verses describe a state like that, not punishment but incapacity. And they ask: what are you doing while the capacity to choose is still yours?

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

In the lower realms, virtue is not even named. Khenchen Thrangu Rinpoche gives this example: look at a hawk or a wolf. You never see them doing anything good. Not because they choose wrong, but because **choosing is gone.** Every day, in countless ways, they accumulate misdeeds just by being alive.

Then he gives a second image: a boulder rolling off a steep cliff. It does not reverse. It goes down by its own nature, faster as it goes. Once in a lower realm, even hearing the words "higher realms" becomes impossible.

What you have right now, the ability to notice and to stop, is the condition those states have lost.

**The capacity to practice is here today. That is what these verses are pointing to.**

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Once today, when you are about to act from pure habit, stop for one breath first. It could be before saying something sharp, before scrolling past someone who needs a reply, before giving in to something you know is not useful. Notice that stopping is possible. That ability, to pause and choose, is what today's verses say is rare and present right now.
