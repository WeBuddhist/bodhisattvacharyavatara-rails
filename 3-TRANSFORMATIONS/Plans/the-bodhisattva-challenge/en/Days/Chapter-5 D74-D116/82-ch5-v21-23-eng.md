## Today's Verse

> If one lives with such conduct,
> whether living among bad people
> or among women,
> the steadfast discipline will not be harmed. ^5-21

> My gains, respect,
> body, and livelihood may be lost,
> and other good deeds may be lost,
> but the mind must never be lost. ^5-22

> To those who wish to guard their minds,
> I implore you with joined palms:
> "Guard mindfulness and awareness
> with all your effort!" ^5-23

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the twenty-first, twenty-second, and twenty-third verses of the fifth chapter. We feel deep inspiration seeing how Master Shantideva himself joins his palms and pleads with us from the depths of his heart.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us a point that is easy to understand and encouraging.

When Master Shantideva joins his palms and pleads with his followers to guard their minds, this is not just a devotional gesture. It is because guarding both mindfulness and awareness is so important that we must protect them even at the cost of our life.

It is not enough for those who wish to guard their minds simply to sit alone with the body, or to withdraw from crowds and stay by themselves. They must rely on mindfulness and awareness at all times. This is just as Jowo Atisha said:

> With mindfulness, awareness, and carefulness, always guard the doors of the senses. Again and again, day and night, three times each, examine the flow of your own mind.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will examine my own body and mind again and again, and I will not forget mindfulness and awareness.

**Explanation:** _(Meditation Practice)_ Those who wish to guard their minds must first work hard to guard both mindfulness and awareness. Mindfulness keeps us from forgetting what to accept and what to reject. Awareness lets us examine the state of our own body and mind again and again. By doing this, we do not let our own mind fall under the power of what is harmful. Instead, we can guide it onto the good path.
