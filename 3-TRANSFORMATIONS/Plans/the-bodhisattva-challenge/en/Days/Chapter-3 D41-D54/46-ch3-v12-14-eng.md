## Today's Verse

> I have given this body to all embodied beings,
> to do with it as they please.
> Therefore, they are free to always kill, insult,
> beat, or do whatever else they wish. ^3-12

> Even if they make sport of my body,
> or make it the object of their mockery and derision,
> I have already given this body away,
> so what is the use of fussing over it? ^3-13

> Let them do whatever actions
> will not harm them.
> May no one ever act pointlessly
> by focusing on me. ^3-14

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the three verses 12 to 14 from the third chapter of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. We have already given our body away to others completely. So even if others beat us or mock us, we drop the grip of protecting ourselves. Together we turn our minds to this practice of the helpful attitude. In it, not one being is left without benefit.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us an explanation and a passage of scripture that are easy to understand and encouraging.

We have already given our body to all beings. So even if others beat us, ridicule us, or abuse us, there is no need to protect this body. The reason is that a thing already given to someone else does not need protecting.

From the _Sutra Requested by Ugra_:

> What is given is not to be protected.
> What is kept at home is to be protected.
> What is given brings existence to an end.
> What is kept at home makes existence grow.

As that says, once our body has been given away completely, there is no worry about protecting it. We bring samsara to an end.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, when others make fun of me, I will remember that this body has been given away and there is nothing to protect. I will not get angry.

**Explanation:** _(Patience Practice)_ Think that we have already given this body to all beings. Then when others look down on us or make fun of us, it is not possible for us to feel hurt or angry. Since it is not held as ours, there is nothing that needs protecting either. If we can cultivate patience from a place of ease, our own mind grows peaceful and the strength of a good mind increases.
