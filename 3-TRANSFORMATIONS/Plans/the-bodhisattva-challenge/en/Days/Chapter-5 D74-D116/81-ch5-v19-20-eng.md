## Today's Verse

> When living amidst a jostling crowd,
> one carefully guards a wound.
> Likewise, though living among bad people,
> one must always guard this wound of the mind. ^5-19

> If one is afraid of a minor wound's suffering
> and guards the wound with care,
> why would one not guard the mind's wound
> from the fears of crushing and dismemberment? ^5-20

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the nineteenth and twentieth verses of the fifth chapter. Just as we carefully protect a wound on our body from being touched by others in a crowd, this teaching shows that we should protect our own mind out of fear of far greater suffering.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us an example that is easy to understand and encouraging.

For example, if we have a wound on our body while walking through a big crowd, we protect it carefully. We are afraid that someone will touch it or bump into it. In the same way, when we live among bad company, we must always guard the wound of our mind with mindfulness and awareness.

Someone who kills a louse caught between their nails will be reborn in the Crushing Hell, where the body must endure being pressed like sesame oil between two iron mountains. So if people carefully guard a small wound on the body out of fear of its small pain, then out of fear of the terrible suffering of hells like the Crushing Hell, we must guard the wound of the mind, the seed of the afflictions, all the more.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, when I am among difficult people, I will guard the wound of my mind with mindfulness and awareness.

**Explanation:** _(Taming the mind)_ When we live among difficult people who stir up the afflictions, we must guard the wound of our mind, the seeds of the afflictions, with mindfulness and awareness, just as we guard a wound on the body. If we protect our mind this way and keep its wound from being touched, we will be freed from the great suffering of the lower realms. Our own mind will become calm and tamed.
