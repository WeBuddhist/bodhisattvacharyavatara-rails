## Combined Commentary Summaries

Here the commentators turn from renunciation to its positive counterpart: sustained reflection on karma. Because non-virtuous actions such as killing produce the suffering of the lower realms as their fruit, one should ask continually, day and night, how to be freed from that suffering together with its cause.

Gyaltsab Je adds a notable philosophical aside, found nowhere else in this cluster of verses: firm conviction in karmic cause and effect is indispensable, and no view — however profound — can substitute for it. A claim to have "realized emptiness" while disregarding the workings of karma reveals, in his words, that "one's understanding has gone astray," since genuine realization of emptiness is inseparable from confidence in dependent origination. Sazang supports the same point by citing a sūtra promising that whoever holds this right view of karma will not fall into the lower realms even for a thousand eons.
