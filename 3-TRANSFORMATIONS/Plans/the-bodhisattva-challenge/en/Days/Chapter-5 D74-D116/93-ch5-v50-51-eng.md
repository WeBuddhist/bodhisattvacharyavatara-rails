## Today's Verse

> Whenever one is eager to praise oneself,
> or to criticize others,
> or is prone to scolding or regret,
> at that time, one should remain like a piece of wood. ^5-50

> If one desires gain, respect, or fame,
> or wishes to seek attendants,
> or if one's mind desires service,
> at that time, one should remain like a piece of wood. ^5-51

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the fiftieth and fifty-first verses of the fifth chapter. When we hear praise, we feel happy. Even if pride arises, a piece of wood stays still.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following key point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. When others praise us, we may become too happy. The wish to hear still more may arise, and pride may arise. Then we must not keep listening. We must not act with body or speech. We must remain like wood.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, if the thought of praising myself or criticizing others arises in me, I will stay still like a piece of wood and say nothing.

**Explanation:** _(Taming the mind)_ When praising ourselves or criticizing others comes up, we must not act with body or speech. We must remain like wood. When we are praised, the wish to listen and pride may arise. Not listening and not speaking at that moment is a way to tame the mind.
