# Day 60 — This good day will not hold

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Use this day before it slips away
> **Detail:** Today's verses look at the ease of a good day and name what it really is: rare, borrowed, and passing.

## Opening

Today feels fine. You ate something. Nothing hurts. The day seems like it will hold. This is the exact moment Shantideva is describing, not a crisis but a comfortable day. Today's three verses look at that comfort clearly. They are not saying today is bad. They are saying today is rare and passing, which is not the same thing.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

The body is borrowed from the four elements. Khenchen Thrangu Rinpoche reads verse 16 that way: what you are carrying is on loan and will be returned to water, earth, fire, and wind. Today life looks stable. But, he says, it deceives in an instant, even when nothing hurts and everything seems fine.

On verse 15, he points to what has come together right now: a teacher, faith to receive the teaching, a human body, and the ability to practice. He calls these ten endowments, five that come from outside and five that are your own. This combination took enormous conditions to assemble. He notes plainly that it is being used up on small things. It will not come together easily again.

**This good day is rare. What you do with it is the only question.**

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

When you think of something good to do and then decide to do it later, do a small part of it now instead. One kind word, one moment of real attention, one act of help you have been putting off. The loan is shorter than it looks.
