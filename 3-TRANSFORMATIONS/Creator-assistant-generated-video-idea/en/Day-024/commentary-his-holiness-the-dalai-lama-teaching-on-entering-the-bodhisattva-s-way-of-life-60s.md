# Day 24 — Commentary — His Holiness the Dalai Lama (Teaching on Entering the Bodhisattva's Way of Life)

Have you ever felt like your gratitude is just too small? Like you want to thank someone, or acknowledge something beautiful, but your words and your gestures just don't cover the scale of what you feel?

The Dalai Lama, a world-renowned Buddhist teacher, explains that we don't have to be stuck with that feeling of being limited. He shares a teaching from the guide to the bodhisattva life written by Shantideva, an eighth-century Indian monk. He says that even if we cannot offer actual riches or grand displays, we can use our minds. We can emanate measureless offerings with a vast motivation and a clear, focused intention.

It is like choosing to imagine a sky filled with light, just because the moment deserves it. When you feel small, try widening your internal horizon. Send out your appreciation with a vast, clear mind. It is a way to make your gratitude as big as you need it to be.
