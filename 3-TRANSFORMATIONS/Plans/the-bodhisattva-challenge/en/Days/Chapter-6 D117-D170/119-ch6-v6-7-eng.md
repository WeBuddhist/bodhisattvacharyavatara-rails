## Today's Verse

> The enemy of anger
> will cause such sufferings.
> Whoever strives to conquer anger
> will be happy in this life and the next. ^6-6

> When you find the food of unhappiness
> because something undesirable happened
> or something desirable was hindered,
> hatred grows strong and destroys you. ^6-7

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the sixth and seventh verses of the sixth chapter. Together, we think about how the unhappiness in our mind feeds hatred and destroys us.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable example, which is easy to understand and encouraging. Just as food makes the body grow, unhappiness is the food that creates and increases hatred. When hatred finds the food of unhappiness, its strength grows, and it destroys us in this life and the next. If we do not turn back the cause of anger, there is no way to turn back anger. So we must also give up the causes of unhappiness.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, when anger arises, I will stop it with great effort and rely on patience.

**Explanation:** _(Patience Practice)_ Anger is the worst enemy. It creates endless suffering in this life and the next. When we defeat anger with the antidote that stops it, we find happiness in this life and the next. By relying on patience, we quickly gain a happy mind, a body at ease, and a long life, and we give anger no chance to arise.
