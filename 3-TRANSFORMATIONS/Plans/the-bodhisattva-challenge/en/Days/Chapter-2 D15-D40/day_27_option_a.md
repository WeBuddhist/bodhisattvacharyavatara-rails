# Day 27 — Confess it before death comes

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Set right one thing today, before the day runs out
> **Detail:** Today's verses name the harm we have done, confess the whole of it, and ask to be rescued before death arrives.

## Opening

Most of us plan to make amends later, when there is more time. Today Shantideva names the harm he has done to the Three Jewels, his parents and his teachers, gathers every misdeed into one confession before the buddhas and bodhisattvas, and asks to be rescued before death arrives.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

We tell ourselves we will put things right later, when life is calmer. The last verse asks to be rescued before death comes, but it does not say why the hurry. Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, a fifteenth-century teacher, fills that in. Death does not wait until your wrongs are cleared. It does not wait for your work to be finished or left unfinished. The healthy die as suddenly as the sick, so you cannot count on even one more day. That is why the three verses move so fast: name the harm done to the Three Jewels, parents and teachers, confess the whole unbearable mass of it, then ask to be rescued now. If you wait, the cost falls on you, in the life that comes next.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

If there is someone you have been meaning to set right with, a parent, a teacher, or anyone you have wronged, try not to leave it for a better day. Name the wrong quietly to the buddhas and bodhisattvas now, while you still can.
