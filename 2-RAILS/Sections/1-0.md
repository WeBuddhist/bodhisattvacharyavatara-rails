## བྱང་ཆུབ་ཀྱི་སེམས་ཀྱི་ཕན་ཡོན་བཤད་པ།

### Synthesis (original language)

མཚན་མོའི་མུན་ནག་གི་སྤྲིན་གསེབ་ཏུ་གློག་འགྱུ་བ་ལྟར་དགེ་བའི་བློ་གྲོས་ནི་བརྒྱ་ལམ་ན་ཐང་འགའ་ཙམ་འབྱུང་བས་ཤིན་ཏུ་ཉམས་ཆུང་ལ། སྡིག་པ་མི་དགེ་བ་ནི་སྟོབས་ཤིན་ཏུ་ཆེ་བས་དགེ་བ་ཕལ་པ་གཞན་གྱིས་ཟིལ་གྱིས་གནོན་མི་ནུས་པ་རྣམས་བྱང་ཆུབ་ཀྱི་སེམས་ཀྱིས་འགོག་ཅིང་ཟིལ་གྱིས་གནོན་པར་བྱེད་དོ།།

### English translation

Just as lightning flashes momentarily through the dense dark clouds of a pitch-black night, the *virtuous mind* (དགེ་བའི་བློ་གྲོས་, *dge ba'i blo gros*) arises only occasionally and is extremely weak, whereas negativity and non-virtue are exceedingly powerful; yet *Bodhicitta* (བྱང་ཆུབ་ཀྱི་སེམས་, *byang chub kyi sems*—the mind of enlightenment) prevents and overcomes what no ordinary virtues can overcome.

The moment Bodhicitta is generated, even wretched beings bound in the prison of *saṃsāra* (འཁོར་བ་, *'khor ba*) undergo a transformation of name and status, becoming known as "Children of the Sugatas" (བདེ་གཤེགས་ཀྱི་སྲས་, *bde gshegs kyi sras*) and becoming worthy of homage and offerings by gods and humans alike.

Regarding praise through analogies: Bodhicitta is like an alchemical elixir (གསེར་འགྱུར་རྩི་, *gser 'gyur rtsi*) that transforms the naturally impure body into the precious body of a Buddha; like a wish-fulfilling jewel (ཡིད་བཞིན་གྱི་ནོར་བུ་, *yid bzhin gyi nor bu*) that dispels the destitution and suffering of beings; while other virtues are like a plantain tree (ཆུ་ཤིང་, *chu shing*) exhausted after bearing fruit once, the tree of Bodhicitta is like a wish-fulfilling tree (དཔག་བསམ་གྱི་ཤིང་, *dpag bsam gyi shing*) whose fruit is inexhaustible and continuously increases.

Bodhicitta divides into *aspiring bodhicitta* (སྨོན་པའི་སེམས་, *smon pa'i sems*) and *engaging bodhicitta* (འཇུག་པའི་སེམས་, *'jug pa'i sems*), analogous to the difference between wishing to travel to a destination and actually traveling there. Although great fruits arise even from the aspiring mind, merit does not flow continuously; but from the moment one formally adopts the engaging mind with the irreversible resolve to free the infinite realms of sentient beings, a continuous flow of merit equal to space arises uninterruptedly even during sleep or heedlessness, as taught in the *Subāhuparipṛcchā Sūtra* (ལག་བཟངས་ཀྱིས་ཞུས་པའི་མདོ་).

Such an altruistic attitude, which desires to dispel the immeasurable suffering of every sentient being and establish them in the immeasurable qualities of Buddhahood, is an unprecedented and wondrous marvel that has never been dreamed of — even for their own benefit — by parents, gods, sages, or Brahmā.

Although sentient beings desire happiness, out of *ignorance* (གཏི་མུག་, *gti mug*) they destroy their own happiness as if it were an enemy and run into suffering; Bodhicitta is the peerless spiritual friend and mass of merit that satisfies beings with all happiness and cuts off all suffering.

If one generates a negative mind toward a bodhisattva benefactor in whom precious Bodhicitta has arisen, one will dwell in hell for aeons; if one generates a clear and faithful mind, the fruit increases even further. Therefore, I pay homage and take refuge in the bodies of those in whom precious Bodhicitta has arisen.
