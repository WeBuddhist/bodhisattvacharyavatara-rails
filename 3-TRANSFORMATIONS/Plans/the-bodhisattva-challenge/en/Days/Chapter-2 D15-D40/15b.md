# Day 15 — Chapter 2 opens: the whole world as an offering

## Opening

*Push notification:* Chapter 2 begins today. It opens with an offering, not a confession.

Today a new chapter starts. Before anything else, Shantideva gives. He pictures flowers, clean water, jeweled mountains, quiet forests, trees full of ripe fruit, and offers them all to the Three Jewels. It is a generous and open way to begin.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

Shantideva had no altar. He had no offerings.

But **Ngulchu Thokme**, a fourteenth-century teacher, shows where he looked. The *King of Meditative Stabilization*, one of the great sutras, gives a simple instruction: picture every flower, every clear stream, every mountain and forest in all the worlds in all ten directions. None of them belong to anyone. Offer them all in your mind.

This is not pretend. Ngulchu says that when you do this without grasping, without holding on, you gather merit without limit. Your wish for buddhahood deepens. The good in you keeps growing.

**You have no altar. But you have a mind. That is enough.**

## Aspiration

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

When you go outside today, find one beautiful thing that belongs to no one. A tree, the open sky, a flower, running water. Hold it in your mind and offer it to the Buddha, the Dharma, and the Sangha.
