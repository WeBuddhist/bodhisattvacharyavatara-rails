## Today's Verse

> For the great guests—beings traveling on the road of existence
> who wish to enjoy the delights of happiness—
> this is the supreme source of happiness,
> and it satisfies them. ^3-32

> Today, before all the protectors, I have
> invited all beings to buddhahood
> and to happiness in the interim.
> Therefore, gods, demigods, and others, rejoice! ^3-33

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the two verses 32 and 33 from the third chapter of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. This is the close of the third chapter. Please look at this great promise, made with all the buddhas as witness: to take on all beings as guests for the happiness that comes at the end.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us something valuable that is easy to understand and encouraging.

This is a great promise. It invites all beings as guests to the state of a buddha in the end, and to the happiness of gods and humans for now. It takes that on as one's own responsibility. It is not empty talk. There is a real reason behind it.

To give oneself up from the bottom of the heart and work for the good of others takes courage that is astonishing. So no being in the three worlds is able to find fault with it.

And there is more. The bodhisattva who makes this promise is an ordinary person standing on the first stages of the path. He is still bound by every chain of the afflictions. For now he has no understanding that sees the truth directly. Even so, he gives rise to courage like that for the sake of all beings. That makes it more wonderful still.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, in the Buddha's presence, I will say my promise aloud once more for the happiness of all beings.

**Explanation:** _(Diligence Practice)_ That verse shows that we should set down our promise to give happiness to all beings once again, and then make effort. If we hold the promise firmly in this way, saying it with our own voice, bodhicitta will not decline. It will grow higher and higher.
