There is no root verse `^7-76` or `^7-77`. Verses `^7-76` and `^7-77` in the beginner Vietnamese file are near-verbatim restatements of `^7-74` and `^7-75` respectively (the same "recall the teaching on carefulness, become light and ready to act" instruction, and the same "wind moves a wisp of cotton" diligence simile, each reworded). This is a content-duplication defect in the translation file itself — two spurious extra verse blocks appended after the chapter's legitimate close — not a mistranslation of any specific commentary-backed verse, and not present in the root text or commentary. It should be corrected by removing verses 76–77 (or clearly marking them as non-canonical) rather than treated as part of the 75-verse chapter.

No bucket-shift artifact for verses 7-1 through 7-75; all 75 commentary markers align with their own verses.

### Chapter 8 — Thiền Định (bSam gtan bstan pa)

**Commentary-bucket shift (not a defect — already found and verified independently in the general-grade report for this vault).** Root verses 8-185 and 8-186 have no separate transclusion markers in `BCAC19_KS_bo.md`; Zhenga's prose for root verses 184, 185, and 186 is combined under the single marker `^8-184` (the same three-verse merge pattern as Chapter 7's `^7-61`). The chapter's true final commentary bucket, `^8-185`, holds the colophon-adjacent commentary for root verse 8-187 (this translation's actual final verse — 187 root verses, but only 185 commentary markers). Independently re-read in full for this pass: bucket `^8-184` visibly contains three distinct thoughts matching VI 8-184 ("give up childish conduct, follow the wise, recall the heedfulness teaching of Chapter 4, dispel sleep/lethargy"), VI 8-185 ("like the compassionate bodhisattva-sons, apply the antidote's effort; if I don't strive day and night equally, will my suffering ever end") and VI 8-186 (same content restated — see note on 8-185/8-186 below); bucket `^8-185` matches VI 8-187 ("dispel the afflictive obscuration of stinginess etc. and the cognitive obscuration of conceptualizing the three spheres, settle in equipoise on equalizing/exchanging self and other — closing colophon"). Verses 1–183 are direct, unshifted matches; the shift applies only to 184–187.

**Note on 8-185/8-186 similarity:** unlike Chapter 7's spurious duplicate verses (which have no commentary or root-text basis at all), VI 8-184–8-186 are three distinct Vietnamese verses translating three distinct thoughts that genuinely exist within the single combined commentary passage `^8-184` — this is a legitimate three-way split of one dense passage, not a duplication defect, and matches the general-grade translation's identical handling of the same passage.
