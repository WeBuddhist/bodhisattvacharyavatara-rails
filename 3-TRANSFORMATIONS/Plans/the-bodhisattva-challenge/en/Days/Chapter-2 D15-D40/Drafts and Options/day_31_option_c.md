# Day 31 — Don't look away from the picture

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Take one small step today on a thing you keep avoiding
> **Detail:** Today's verse holds up a hard picture so it can move us, gently, to act now.

## Opening

Shantideva paints an uncomfortable scene on purpose: a person marched off today to lose their limbs, dry-mouthed, wild-eyed, unrecognisable with fear. It is the kind of image we instinctively look away from. He is asking us not to look away, because if we let it land for a moment, it can move us, while we still have time and choices.

## From the Tradition

Sazang Mati Panchen, a fourteenth-century teacher, adds one detail that sharpens the scene. The person dragging the prisoner along is only an ordinary human, someone of the same kind as the rest of us. Even that is enough to leave the prisoner thirsty with terror, eyes wide, looking in every direction for a way out. Sazang lets the comparison sit unfinished, because the next thought is obvious: if a human escort produces that, what about the end none of us escapes. He does not raise the picture to make us despair. The whole verse turns on the word heedless from just before it. We avoid such thoughts and stay distracted, and that is exactly what keeps us from preparing. Held steadily for even a moment, the fear stops being something to flee and becomes a push to do, today, the things we keep postponing.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Bring to mind one good thing you keep putting off because there is always more time: a call you owe, a habit you mean to start, an amends you have been avoiding. Let the plain fact that time is not promised be a gentle push rather than a worry, and take just the first small step on it today.
