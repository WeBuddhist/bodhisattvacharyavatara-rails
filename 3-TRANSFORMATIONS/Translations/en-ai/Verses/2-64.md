## Combined Commentary Summaries

Completing the confession begun in the previous verse, this stanza describes the manner in which it is made: directly before the compassionate Protectors, with palms joined, prostrating again and again, moved by a mind afraid of the suffering that wrongdoing produces.

Sazang uses the phrase "openly acknowledge" (mthol zhing bshags pa) to capture the double gesture of admission and confession, while Gyaltsab and Thokme both stress the repeated, physically embodied nature of the act — prostrating "again and again" — as itself an expression of the sincerity of the regret.
