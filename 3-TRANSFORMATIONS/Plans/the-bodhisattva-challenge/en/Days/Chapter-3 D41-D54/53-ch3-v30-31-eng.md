## Today's Verse

> It is a bridge that delivers
> all beings from the lower realms.
> It is the moon that has risen
> to clear away the torment of the disturbing emotions of beings. ^3-30

> It is the great sun that entirely removes
> the confusion and blurred vision of beings.
> It is the essence of butter that emerged
> from churning the milk of the sacred teachings. ^3-31

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the two verses 30 and 31 from the third chapter of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. Just as a cool moon rises in the mind and clears away its fever, let us look closely today at these words.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us a comparison that is easy to understand and encouraging.

When a lot of milk is churned again and again, butter comes from it. When that butter is melted, the essence of butter is drawn out, the purest part of what was already pure.

It is the same with the great ocean of the Buddha's holy teaching. If we churn it again and again with the churning stick of the wisdom of hearing and reflecting, then bodhicitta comes out of it. Bodhicitta is the finest refined essence of the heart of the Dharma.

This mind holds the power to produce all benefit and happiness, and it holds the finest taste. So we should listen to the profound Dharma and reflect on it again and again. Once we have found bodhicitta itself, we should hold it always with great joy.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, when anger arises in me, I will calm it with cool loving-kindness.

**Explanation:** _(Taming the mind)_ Bodhicitta is like the moon that clears away the fever of the afflictions in our own mind. When anger or desire arises, if we meditate on loving-kindness, the burning of the mind is calmed. In that way we make our own mind peaceful and tame.
