# Day 11 — The friend who helps you see clearly

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Thank someone who helped you see clearly
> **Detail:** The deepest help isn't comfort, but clarity. Today, remember someone who gave you that.

## Opening

Today's two verses name three things this mind does for people who are struggling. It brings them happiness. It takes away their pain. And it clears away their confusion. Then the verses ask: where is there anything, or anyone, equal to that?

>
> It also dispels their ignorance. Where is there a virtue equal to this? Where is there such a spiritual friend, and where is there such merit?

## From the Tradition

**Gyaltsab Je**, a fifteenth-century teacher, draws out which of the three matters most. Giving someone comfort is good. Easing their pain is good. But the deepest gift, he says, is the third one: clearing away their confusion.

His reason is simple. People are not short on the wish to be happy. What they lack is seeing what actually leads there. So the rarest friend is not the one who only comforts you, but the one who helps you see clearly. That, the verse says, is what nothing else can match.

## Today's Practice

Bring to mind someone who once helped you see something clearly, not just someone who comforted you, but someone who helped you understand what was really going on. Take a moment to feel grateful for them.
