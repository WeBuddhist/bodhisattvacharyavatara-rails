## Combined Commentary Summaries

This verse begins the culminating act of the whole passage: the actual, explicit confession. It specifies the scope of what is confessed — every wrong committed out of ignorance and delusion, whether "naturally unwholesome" (wrong for anyone who commits it, such as killing) or "wrong by convention" (wrong only for one bound by a precept, such as drinking alcohol for a vow-holder, or, as Thokme specifies, eating in the afternoon).

All three commentators treat this verse as inseparable from the one that follows, forming together one act of open, repeated acknowledgment of every fault and downfall.
