# Day 18 — The bathhouse you don't own

> **Notification**
> **Title:** When you wash today, offer it as a bath for the awakened ones
> **Detail:** Shantideva offers a jewelled bathhouse he builds in his mind. Today, turn an ordinary wash into an offering.

## Opening

Shantideva has no palace and no jewels. So he builds one in his mind: a bathhouse so fragrant the air is sweet, a floor of clear crystal, pillars lit with jewels, a canopy of pearls overhead. Then he invites the buddhas and bodhisattvas in and bathes them there. None of it sits in his house. All of it is made by attention.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

It is easy to think you have nothing fine enough to give, so you give nothing.

The verse describes a bathhouse no ordinary person owns. Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, a fifteenth-century teacher, sets it among the offerings the mind makes rather than the hand. The crystal floor and the pearl canopy are built by imagining them clearly, and then the buddhas are bathed there. The point is simple and freeing: the offering is real because the mind makes it real, not because you paid for it. Anyone can build this, and the merit lands on the one who offers. You are not short of materials.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

The next time you wash today, at the sink or in the shower, take a few seconds to turn it into an offering. In your mind, let the water be clean and fragrant and the buddhas and bodhisattvas be the ones you are bathing. The whole offering is built right there, with nothing you had to buy.
