## Today's Verse

> I bow with palms joined to the buddhas who wish to pass beyond sorrow
> and pray:
> “Please do not abandon these blind beings,
> but remain for countless eons.” ^3-5

> Having done all these things,
> whatever good deeds I have accumulated,
> may all the suffering of all beings
> be removed by them. ^3-6

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the two verses 5 and 6 from the third chapter of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. We join our palms and pray that beings are not left sightless and blind. And we dedicate all our own good to removing the suffering of beings.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses tell us a story that is easy to understand and encouraging.

Long ago, when our teacher Shakyamuni had reached the age of eighty, he formed the intention to pass into nirvana. At that time a lay practitioner named Chunda, the blacksmith's son, asked him again and again not to pass away. By this the Teacher's life was extended by three months.

In the same way, this verse joins the palms before the buddhas of the ten directions. They have won the battle against the four maras. It prays them not to pass into nirvana.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today I will dedicate all the good I do to removing the suffering of all beings.

**Explanation:** _(Generosity Practice)_ This line shows that we should not dedicate only for ourselves, for a long life, freedom from illness, wealth and the like. All the roots of good we have gathered are to be dedicated completely. They are dedicated as a cause for removing the suffering of body and mind of all beings, who are as many as space is wide.
