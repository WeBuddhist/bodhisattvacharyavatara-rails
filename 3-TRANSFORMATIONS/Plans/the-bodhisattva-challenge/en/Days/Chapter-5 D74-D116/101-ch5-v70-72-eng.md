## Today's Verse

> Regarding the body as merely a boat
> for coming and going,
> transform it into a wish-fulfilling body
> to accomplish the benefit of beings. ^5-70

> Since you are independent,
> always keep a smiling face.
> Completely abandon frowning and scowling,
> and be gentle with beings, like a friend. ^5-71

> Do not carelessly throw down
> chairs and other things with noise.
> Do not forcefully open doors;
> always be quiet and joyful. ^5-72

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the seventieth, seventy-first, and seventy-second verses of the fifth chapter. We regard our own body as a boat. We train in closing doors gently and looking at others with a smile.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable example, which is easy to understand and encouraging. To cross the ocean of samsara, we must see this body of ours as a fine boat. We give the body only the wages of food and clothes. We do not use it for wrongdoing. We use it for the good actions of generating bodhicitta and the six perfections. In the end, we accomplish the form body of a buddha, take many different forms, and accomplish the vast benefit of beings.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will give up frowning and speak honestly to everyone, with a smile.

**Explanation:** _(Doing good)_ If we always speak honestly with a smiling face, people become happy. If we give up frowning and fake shyness and make friends with a good heart, both we and others benefit.
