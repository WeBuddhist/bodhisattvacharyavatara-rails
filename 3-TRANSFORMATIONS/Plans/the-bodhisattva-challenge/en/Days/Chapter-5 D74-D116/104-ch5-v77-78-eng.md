## Today's Verse

> All endeavors are for joy,
> which is hard to obtain even for a price.
> Therefore, one should experience the joy
> of others' good qualities. ^5-77

> Here, no harm comes to me,
> and great happiness comes to others.
> Displeasure brings unhappiness and suffering,
> and great suffering comes to others. ^5-78

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the seventy-seventh and seventy-eighth verses of the fifth chapter. The flower of joy that gold cannot buy blooms in our own mind.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable example, which is easy to understand and encouraging. If we go to the big cities of India with money, we can buy whatever things we need. But if we go to buy joy, there is nothing to sell. Each of us must accomplish it by directing our own thoughts. In this way, we must meditate on joy in the good qualities that others have created, and enjoy the happiness of joy.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will not be jealous of the good qualities of others. I will rejoice in them.

**Explanation:** _(Taming the mind)_ If we meditate on joy in the good qualities of others, we suffer no harm, and we find happiness in this life and the next. If we are jealous, our mind is unhappy, and we will also feel suffering in the next life.
