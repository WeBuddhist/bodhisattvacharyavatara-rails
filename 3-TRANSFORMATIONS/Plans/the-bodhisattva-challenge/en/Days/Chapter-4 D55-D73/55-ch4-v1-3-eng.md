## Today's Verse

> Having firmly taken hold of the Mind of Awakening,
> the spiritual children of the buddhas
> should always strive without straying
> from the training. ^4-1

> Whatever has been undertaken rashly,
> or whatever has not been properly examined,
> even if a vow has been made,
> it is right to examine whether to do it or abandon it. ^4-2

> What the buddhas and their spiritual children
> have examined with great wisdom,
> and what I myself have examined and re-examined—
> what reason is there to turn away from that? ^4-3

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the three verses 1 to 3 from the fourth chapter of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. This promise has already been well examined. So now let us go forward with courage, with no doubt and no drawing back.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us a passage of scripture that is easy to understand and encouraging.

> Carefulness is the place of the nectar,
> and carelessness the place of death: so the Sage taught.
> Therefore, in order to increase what is good,
> always act with respect and with care.

As today's first verse says, it is very important to be careful: to hold bodhicitta firmly and not let the training decline. Carefulness is the root and the support of everything good. It keeps the promise of bodhicitta and the good we do alive, and makes them grow.

But if we become careless, every kind of wrongdoing arises, and it turns into a cause of the suffering of samsara. So we must not fall into carelessness even for a moment. We should be careful always.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, before I begin anything, I will examine carefully whether it is right to do or not.

**Explanation:** _(Wisdom Practice)_ Acting in a rush, without examining, brings harm to ourselves and to others alike. So whatever the task, we should examine it well before we start. Then there is no regret, and the aim is accomplished by itself.
