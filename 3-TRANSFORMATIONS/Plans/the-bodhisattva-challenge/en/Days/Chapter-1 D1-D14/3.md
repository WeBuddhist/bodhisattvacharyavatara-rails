# Day 3 — What the buddhas searched ages to find

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Turn a hard moment into a wish for others
> **Detail:** Only this mind can outweigh our worst. Today, when something hurts, let it become a wish.

## Opening

Yesterday's verses were about urgency. Today's three give the answer. Ordinary good deeds are too weak against the weight of our harm. The buddhas searched for ages and found that this one mind works. So if you long to end suffering, your own and everyone's, hold on to it.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

> Do you wish to overcome the hundreds of sufferings of worldly existence? Do you wish to remove the unhappiness of sentient beings and enjoy hundreds of joys? If so, you must never abandon the mind of enlightenment.

## From the Tradition

These three verses show three wonderful benefits of the awakening mind.

**It overpowers wrongdoing.** Our good deeds are weak, and our wrongdoing is very strong. But as the sutra says, "just as mice are overpowered when a cat looks at them," the moment perfect bodhicitta arises, it overpowers all of that strong wrongdoing.

**It brings happiness quickly.** The Buddhas thought deeply and carefully for countless ages. In the end they saw that the one and only path that easily brings help and happiness to countless beings is this awakening mind.

**It fulfils every wish.** So whether a humble person wants to end their own suffering, or a great person wants to end the suffering of others, or someone simply wants to enjoy much happiness — this good mind is the best way to make all those wishes come true. The advice is this: never let this good mind slip away from your heart.

## Today's Practice

Next time something in your day feels hard, a wave of tiredness, a real ache, the weight of a bad mood, pause and remember that everyone else carries some version of it too. Let that plain recognition become a quiet wish for them.
