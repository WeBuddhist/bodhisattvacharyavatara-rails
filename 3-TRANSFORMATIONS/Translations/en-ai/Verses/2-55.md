## Combined Commentary Summaries

This verse and the next are treated by all three commentators as forming one continuous argument. Verse 55 states that even a single one of the afflictions, left unchecked, is powerful enough to destroy every person living in this world.

Ngulchu Thokme's commentary presents verses 55 and 56 as a single combined statement. Sazang stresses that no antidote for the afflictions can be found anywhere except in the Buddha's teaching, and Gyaltsab Je adds that this remedy cannot be found even in other exalted realms such as Brahmā's world, underscoring its rarity and value.
