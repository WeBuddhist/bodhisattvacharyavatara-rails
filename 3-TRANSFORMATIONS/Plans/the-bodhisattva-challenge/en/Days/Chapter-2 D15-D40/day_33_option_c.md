# Day 33 — From this very day

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Pick one burden and, from today, stop carrying it alone
> **Detail:** Today's verse makes refuge a real decision: from this very day, and meant.

## Opening

The verse does not say "someday I will take refuge." It says from this very day. After all the fear, Shantideva makes refuge a decision with a date on it, and he takes it in the buddhas, the dharma, and the sangha. There is a quiet courage in that, choosing now, before the next crisis, to stop facing everything alone.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

Sazang Mati Panchen, a fourteenth-century teacher, leans on the small word that the translation gives as "truly," and the Tibetan calls authentic. Refuge, he says, is taken just as one takes refuge in the Buddha, and the authentic part means without pretence and without doubt, with full faith. That is the difference between refuge as a vague background belief and refuge as something real. A half-hearted, hedged refuge does not hold any weight when weight is put on it. Paired with the verse's other phrase, from this very day, the message is clear and bracing. This is not a feeling that drifts in and out. It is a choice you make, sincerely and whole, and then keep making. Sazang's point is not that you must manufacture certainty, but that refuge becomes a real shelter only when you actually lean on it, rather than keeping one foot out the door.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Bring to mind one worry you have been carrying alone for a while. Today, make a small, real decision about it: from this day, you hand it to the Buddha, the dharma, and the sangha, and you stop shouldering it by yourself. Say it plainly, and mean it. You can renew the same decision tomorrow; the point is to actually start, today.
