## Today's Verse

> One should share with those who have fallen,
> the helpless, and those upholding discipline.
> One should eat a moderate amount,
> and give away everything except the three robes. ^5-85

> One should not harm this body,
> which is the practice of the sacred teachings, for trivial reasons.
> If one acts in this way, the aspirations of beings
> will quickly be fulfilled. ^5-86

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the eighty-fifth and eighty-sixth verses of the fifth chapter. Today, we think about the extra food and clothes we have, and to whom we can or cannot give them.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. The food we get from alms and so on must be divided into four parts. One part must be given to those who have fallen into wrong ways, one to the helpless, and one to those who keep strict discipline. We ourselves must eat the last part, knowing the right amount of food, not too much and not too little. Also, we must not be attached to extra clothes beyond the three Dharma robes. We must give them to others.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will eat just enough, and give away my extra food and clothes to the helpless.

**Explanation:** _(Generosity Practice)_ The root verse says that after eating just enough, we must give our extra food and clothes to the helpless and to those who hold vows. This gives up our own desire and helps others. By being moderate with food and clothes, the mind finds peace.
