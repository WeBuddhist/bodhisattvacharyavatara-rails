## Today's Verse

> If anyone becomes angry or faithful
> by focusing on me,
> may that itself always
> be the cause for accomplishing all their aims. ^3-15

> Whoever speaks ill of me,
> or harms others,
> or slanders them,
> may all of them attain awakening. ^3-16

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's daily practice is based on the two verses 15 and 16 from the third chapter of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. These give a special instruction that turns even the person who harms us into a cause of awakening.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us a passage of scripture that is easy to understand and encouraging. It runs: "May those who give me food obtain the happiness of complete peace," and so on.

Khenpo Kunzang Palden took this example and applied it to the prayer in verse sixteen. Its meaning is this. Whoever sees me, or hears me, or remembers me, or touches me, and whether the connection is a good one or a bad one: may all those beings accomplish everything they wish for. And may it become a cause for bringing about every benefit and happiness, both for now and in the end. That is the prayer.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today I will pray that those who harm me attain awakening.

**Explanation:** _(Patience Practice)_ This practice works with all three kinds of harm: speaking harshly with the voice, harming with the body, and slandering behind one's back. Whichever of them is done, we do not get angry ourselves. Instead we pray that all of those people come to have the fortune of attaining awakening. This makes a cause of awakening for the beings who harm us. It also has the benefit of taming our own mind.
