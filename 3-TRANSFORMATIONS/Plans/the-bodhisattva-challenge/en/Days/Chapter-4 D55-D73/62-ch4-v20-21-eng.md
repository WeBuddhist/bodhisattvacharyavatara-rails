## Today's Verse

> Therefore, the Blessed One taught
> that a human body is extremely hard to obtain,
> like a turtle putting its neck
> through the hole of a yoke in a vast ocean. ^4-20

> If even a single moment of misdeed
> can cause one to dwell in the lowest hell for an eon,
> needless to say, if misdeeds accumulated in beginningless cyclic existence
> will prevent one from going to happy realms! ^4-21

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the two verses 20 and 21 from the fourth chapter of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. Like a blind turtle's neck slipping through the hole of a yoke drifting on a great ocean, let us think closely about the good fortune of gaining this rare human body.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us a comparison that is easy to understand and encouraging.

The Buddha spoke to the monks like this: "Imagine the whole world turns into a great ocean. In it, a yoke with a single hole drifts about, moved by the wind in every direction. Deep in the ocean there is a blind turtle that comes up to the surface only once every hundred years. For that blind turtle's neck to slip through the hole in the drifting yoke is extremely hard." The Buddha said that gaining a human body is even harder than that. So, when we gain this precious human body, so hard to find, we should not fall under the power of carelessness and laziness. We should take the essence from the holy Dharma.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, when anger toward others arises in me, I will catch my own mind right away and hold back from acting on hatred.

**Explanation:** _(Taming the mind)_ Even a single moment of anger can bring great suffering in the lower realms. So we must always watch our own mind with care. If we give up hatred, we are freed from great wrongdoing. This is the best way to protect ourselves from the lower realms.
