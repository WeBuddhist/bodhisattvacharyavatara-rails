## Today's Verse

> Defilements do not dwell in objects, nor in the sense faculties, nor in between.
> Nor are they elsewhere. So where do these things dwell that harm all beings?
> This is like an illusion. Therefore, abandon fear in your heart and strive to know.
> Why do they needlessly inflict harm on me in hell and so forth? ^4-47

> Having reflected in this way,
> I must strive to accomplish the training as explained.
> If a patient does not listen to the doctor's words,
> how can that patient, needing treatment, be cured by medicines? ^4-48

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the last two verses of the fourth chapter, verses 47 and 48. From this example of the afflictions being like an illusion, and a patient needing to take medicine, let us all join together today in actual practice.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us a story that is easy to understand and encouraging. For example, a sick person may find a skilled and kind doctor and receive good medicine. But if that person does not drink the medicine and does not follow the right diet, the illness will not be cured. This is not the fault of the doctor or the medicine. It is the patient's own fault. In the same way, the Buddha is like the doctor, the holy Dharma is like the medicine, the afflictions such as craving and hatred are like the illness, and we are like the patient. The _Chandrapradipa Sutra_ also says that even if a skilled doctor gives medicine, if the patient does not drink it, they cannot be freed from the illness. So merely hearing the Dharma does not help us. We must put it into actual practice.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will actually carry out in practice one piece of advice I have learned.

**Explanation:** _(Diligence Practice)_ If a patient does not listen to the doctor's advice, medicine cannot cure the illness. In the same way, merely hearing the Dharma does not help. We must put it into actual practice. If we take this advice to heart today, it will greatly help clear away the afflictions in our own mind.
