## Today's Verse

> If I continue to act in the same way,
> it will be the same again and again.
> In the lower realms, I will experience
> illness, bondage, cutting, piercing, and so forth. ^4-14

> When will I obtain
> such a rare human body,
> the appearance of a buddha,
> faith, and the ability to practice virtue? ^4-15

> This life, free from illness,
> with food and without harm,
> is fleeting and deceptive;
> the body is like a temporary loan. ^4-16

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the three verses 14 to 16 from the fourth chapter of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. Carelessness makes us fall into the lower realms. Even when we get a human body, we are worn down by illness, imprisonment and the like. And four things are hard to bring together at once. They are a buddha appearing, faith, a human body, and the chance to grow used to doing good. So let us know that this body does not last, that it is like something borrowed, and hurry to do good.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us a comparison that is easy to understand and encouraging.

As Master Nagarjuna says in the _Ratnavali_ and Master Aryadeva in the _Chatuhshataka_, this body of ours is a gathering of the four elements: earth, water, fire and air, and they sit together like four poisonous snakes put into one basket, each against the others, so the body is never steady for long.

Today's medical scans and tests show the same thing. There is no solid core in the body that stays firm. There are seventy-two thousand fine channels in our body, and if even one of them is cut it can become a cause of death.

So we should not put things off until tomorrow, resting on a body that may die at any time. From today itself we should work at the Dharma with great heart.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, knowing that this body does not last and is like something borrowed, let us work at the holy Dharma without putting it off.

**Explanation:** _(Practice of remembering that nothing lasts)_ Today we may be free of illness and free of harm, yet life runs down moment by moment and deceives us. This body we hold so dear is also something borrowed for a short while. So while we have this rare human life, we should not put things off. We should work at the practice.
