## Today's Verse

> To check for dangers on the road and so forth,
> I should repeatedly look in the four directions.
> After resting, I should turn my head and
> look behind me. ^5-37

> Having checked ahead and behind,
> I should go or come.
> In all situations,
> I should act with appropriate knowledge. ^5-38

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the thirty-seventh and thirty-eighth verses of the fifth chapter. This teaching clearly shows that the training in mindfulness and awareness is not a rigid set of rules. It must adapt to the needs of each situation.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us a point that is easy to understand and encouraging.

When we walk somewhere, we must carefully check behind us for enemies, thieves, or dangerous animals. This shows that the training in mindfulness and awareness is not a rigid set of rules. It must be applied according to the need and reason of each situation.

We should give up pointless distraction and act only for reasons that benefit both ourselves and others. So we too should conduct ourselves properly, always knowing the actual need behind each action.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, whatever I do, I will first check whether it is truly needed for myself or others, and act with mindfulness and awareness.

**Explanation:** _(Wisdom Practice)_ This practice is not simply a rigid set of rules. It is the best method for benefiting ourselves and others, according to the need and reason of each situation. If we act only once we clearly know the purpose of any action, no mistakes will arise, and our time will not be wasted on pointless activity.
