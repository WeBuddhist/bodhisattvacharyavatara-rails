## Today's Verse

> With full certainty and deep faith,
> steadfastness, respect, and humility,
> shame, and fear,
> calmly and striving to please others. ^5-55

> Not wearied by the childish
> desires that conflict with each other,
> and with compassion, thinking, "These troubling emotions
> have arisen in this mind." ^5-56

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the fifty-fifth and fifty-sixth verses of the fifth chapter. If even the Buddha could not fulfill everyone's wishes, what should we do when people's wishes do not agree?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following story, which is easy to understand and encouraging. Even when we do good for others, some people become pleased and some do not. Even our teacher Shakyamuni could not please Sunakshatra, Devadatta, and others. So we ordinary people cannot please everyone. For that reason, we do not let our own mind lose heart. People's thoughts differ, and some have faith and some do not. All this comes from the afflictions, such as desire, anger, and jealousy. So we must take care of these people with love and compassion, as well as we can.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, even when other people's wishes do not agree, I will not get angry. I will practice developing a loving heart.

**Explanation:** _(Patience Practice)_ People's wishes do not agree because of the afflictions. If we do not get angry about this, our own mind finds peace. If we meditate on love for others, people become harmonious with one another. This makes the good quality of patience grow in our mind.
