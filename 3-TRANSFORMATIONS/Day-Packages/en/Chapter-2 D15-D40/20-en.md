## 1. Today's Challenge (from the practice-plan track)

<!-- challenge:opening -->
### Opening

Today's practice is based on verses 2-14 and 2-15 from the second chapter of the *Bodhicaryāvatāra*. It invites us to use the power of our imagination to offer supreme sweet-scented ointments that fill the entire universe, along with heavenly blossoms woven into beautiful garlands, to the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas. This practice helps us cultivate a generous and open heart through mental offerings.

<!-- challenge:tradition -->
### From the Tradition

These verses explain two special types of mental offering: fragrant ointments and flowers. Master Shantideva imagines gathering the finest sweet scents — perfumes like pure sandalwood whose fragrance spreads across a billion-fold universe. He respectfully anoints the radiant bodies of the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas, which glow brightly like refined, polished gold. He then presents lovely blossoms from heavenly and earthly realms, including mandarava flowers, lotuses, and blue utpalas. He offers these sweet-scented flowers both scattered individually through the air and woven into artistic, delightful garlands.

Spiritual teachers explain that the awakened Buddhas do not need our perfumes or flowers. We make these mental offerings to cleanse the negative karma and mental pollution of ourselves and all living beings. By offering fragrant ointments with pure devotion, we plant the positive seeds for all beings to one day attain the radiant, golden complexion of a Buddha. By offering beautiful flowers and garlands, we plant the seeds for the seven spiritual qualities of enlightenment to bloom like lovely flowers within the hearts of all living beings.

<!-- challenge:practice -->
### Today's Practice

**Practice:** Today, whenever I see a beautiful flower or smell a sweet scent, I will mentally offer it to the Three Jewels to help clear away negative karma.

**Explanation:** *(Generosity)* Our usual instinct is to grasp at beautiful things and keep them for ourselves. Today, instead of clinging to that beauty, I will imagine it multiplying to fill the universe and mentally offer it to the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas. This simple mental shift breaks our habit of selfish attachment, redirecting that positive joy to help spiritual qualities bloom in all living beings.

<!-- cm:tenzin-gyatso -->
##### His Holiness the Dalai Lama (Teaching on Entering the Bodhisattva's Way of Life)

Then, the offering of anointing the body: with the finest fragrance, whose scent wafts through — or pervades — all the billionfold world-systems, upon the bodies of the Lords of Sages, which are like refined, polished gold and by nature glow and blaze with light, radiant with splendor, I anoint with those fine anointing-perfumes. Upon those bodies, glowing and ablaze with light, I anoint in particular with that finest fragrance whose scent can pervade the whole billionfold universe.

Sources: [[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/Transcluded/BCAC20_TG_bo.toc.md#^2-14]]

<!-- cm:kunpal -->
##### Khenpo Kunzang Pelden (Nectar Drops)

The second [of the mind-emanated offerings]. As the *Abhidharmakośa* says: "A thousand of the four continents, suns and moons, Mount Merus, desire-gods, and Brahma-worlds is held to be a first-order thousand; a thousand of those is the second-order, the middling world-system; a thousand of those is the third-order [thousand]." So: with the finest, cool, sweet fragrances — white sandalwood and the like — whose sweet scent rises and spreads through all the boundless world-systems of the great billionfold universe, one fills precious vessels, conch-cups, mother-of-pearl [ladles] and the like, and the offering-deities anoint the bodies of all the Lords of Sages — the buddhas and bodhisattvas — bodies like fine gold well smelted and refined up to sixteen times, brought to their luster with various medicinal substances and burnished with silk cloths, so that they glow to oneself and blaze forth to others: these one anoints, so that no dust may settle. And one should think: "may all beings, oneself and others, thereby gain the excellent mark of skin the color of gold."

##### Sabzang Mati Panchen (Clear Illumination)

The fourth, the offering of sweet fragrance: a billionfold universe is a thousandfold-of-thousandfold, having a hundred million of the four-continent [worlds] — this is one buddha-field; and throughout all such boundless buddha-fields of the ten directions, with the finest fragrances — sandalwood and the like — whose sweet scent rises and spreads, I anoint the bodies of the Lords of Sages, the buddhas and bodhisattvas, which, like fine gold well refined and smelted and then polished, take on the luster of light-rays and blaze; anointing and rubbing them, I make offering.

<!-- cm:minyak-kunzang-sonam -->
##### Minyak Kunzang Sonam (Inexhaustible Treasury Vase)

The second, the offering of enjoyment-items, presented in the order from scented water through to the parasol. The anointing with sweet fragrance: "throughout the billionfold universe," it says. Its meaning, following the *Abhidharmakośa*: a thousand [worlds] of the four continents, Mount Meru, desire-gods and Brahma-worlds is the first-order thousand, the first world-system; a thousandfold enlargement of that is the second-order, the middling world-system; a thousand of those is the third-order, the great billionfold world-system — in which there are a hundred million each of the four continents, Mount Merus, suns and moons, desire-gods and the rest. One such billionfold universe is the buddha-field of a single buddha; and, pervading without exception all the fields abiding in the boundless ten directions, with anointing-perfumes made of the finest sweet fragrances — sandalwood and the like — whose sweet scent rises and spreads, I anoint the bodies of all the Lords of Sages, the buddhas and bodhisattvas. Those bodies are like refined gold — its outer stains cleansed with waters of myrobalan, salt, natron and the like; its inner stains cleansed by placing it in a sealed clay crucible and firing it long — and then repeatedly burnished with gems such as *rocana*, so that it is well polished, taking on a most brilliant luster and blazing and radiating light outwardly: these bodies I anoint and rub, and so make offering.

Sources: [[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/Transcluded/BCAC19_MKS_bo_segmented.md#^2-14]]

<!-- cm:khenpo-kunga -->
##### Khenpo Kunga Wangchuk (Commentary on the Bodhicaryāvatāra)

Again, the offering of anointing-perfume. "Billionfold" — the four continents of east, south, west and north together with the eight subcontinents; at the center Mount Meru, king of mountains; the sun and moon that light them; the desire-gods; and the Brahma-worlds. As the *Abhidharmakośa* teaches, "a thousand Brahma-worlds is held to be a first-order thousand": in brief, when the count of a thousand of the four-continent world-systems is complete, that is the first-order thousandfold world; when a thousand of those is complete, that is the second-order, the middling thousandfold world-system; and when a thousand of those is complete, that is the great billionfold world-system, as taught in the Abhidharma. In that great billionfold world-system there are said to be a hundred million Mount Merus, a hundred million four-continent worlds, a hundred million suns, a hundred million moons, and a hundred million Brahma-worlds. Throughout all such a world-system, with anointing-perfumes of the sweet-scented waters of sandalwood, aloeswood and the like, whose fine fragrance rises and pervades everywhere — upon the bodies of all the Sages who are "able" (thub) in the face of the misdeeds of the three doors, the perfectly complete buddhas, the Blessed Ones — bodies like gold well refined, smelted some sixteen times and then polished, glowing to oneself and blazing with light to others: all of these too I duly anoint. That is, with an anointing-perfume of sweet-scented water applied to the bodies of all the buddhas and bodhisattvas to the depth of an ox-hide's thickness, I anoint them. And, on the strength of the merit of thus offering the anointing-perfume, one also prays: "may the skin of the beings of myself and others become, in the future, the color of gold."

Sources: [[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/Transcluded/BCAC20_NKW_bo_segmented.md#^2-14]]

<!-- sub:teaching-points -->
#### Main Teaching Points

###### 1. The Offering of Anointing-Perfume — Anointing the Bodies of the Sages With the Finest Fragrance That Pervades the Whole Billionfold Universe

As all the commentaries explain on a shared basis, this verse presents the anointing with sweet fragrance, within the mind-emanated offering: one anoints the bodies of all the Lords of Sages with the finest fragrance — sandalwood and the like — that pervades the whole great billionfold universe.

###### 2. The Chief Image — Like Refined Gold, Well Polished, Glowing to Oneself and Blazing With Light to Others

The body of the Sage is explained as being like refined gold well smelted and refined up to sixteen times and then burnished, so that by nature it glows to oneself and blazes with light to others.

###### 3. The Way the Billionfold Universe Is Counted, Set Out Clearly From the Abhidharmakośa

The extent that the field of offering pervades is set out from the *Abhidharmakośa*: a thousand of the four-continent worlds with Mount Meru, sun and moon, desire-gods and Brahma-worlds is a first-order thousand; a thousand of those is the middling world-system; and a thousand of those is the great billionfold world-system.

###### 4. Identifying "Refined Gold" — Gold Whose Outer and Inner Stains Have Been Purified

Minyak Kunzang Sonam identifies "refined" in detail: gold whose outer stains are cleansed with waters of myrobalan, salt, natron and the like, and whose inner stains are cleansed by placing it in a sealed clay crucible and firing it long.

###### 5. The Aspiration and Dedication — That All Beings May in Future Gain the Excellent Mark of Gold-Colored Skin

Kunzang Pelden and Khenpo Kunga Wangchuk both teach making the aspiration-prayer that, on the strength of the merit of offering the anointing-perfume, the skin of the beings of oneself and others may become the color of gold.

###### 6. The Enumeration of the Offering's Position Differs

Gyaltsab and Sabzang treat it as the fourth by count; Ngulchu Thogme as the fifth; and Minyak Kunzang Sonam explains that from this verse a new section, "the offering of enjoyment-items," begins.

#### Verse Synthesis (overview)

**Brief introduction.** This verse presents the offering of anointing with sweet fragrance, within the mind-emanated offering: with the finest fragrance that pervades the whole great billionfold universe, one anoints the bodies of all the Lords of Sages — bodies that, like refined gold well polished, glow to oneself and blaze with light to others.

**Key points.**

- The extent that the anointing-offering pervades is the finest fragrance — sandalwood and the like — that pervades the whole great billionfold universe.
- The way the billionfold universe is counted follows the *Abhidharmakośa*: from a thousand of the four-continent worlds with Mount Meru, sun and moon, desire-gods and Brahma-worlds, through the second-order middling world-system, up to the great billionfold world-system.
- The body anointed is explained, on a shared basis, as being like refined gold whose outer and inner stains have been well purified and then polished, glowing to oneself and blazing with light to others.
- On the fruit of offering the anointing-perfume, one prays that all beings, oneself and others, may in future gain the excellent mark of gold-colored skin.
- ⚑ The position of this offering within the sequence differs: Gyaltsab and Sabzang count it fourth, Ngulchu Thogme fifth, and Minyak Kunzang Sonam explains that from here a new section, "the offering of enjoyment-items," begins.

##### His Holiness the Dalai Lama (Teaching on Entering the Bodhisattva's Way of Life)

To the Lords of Sages, the supreme fields of offering, together with their heirs, I offer all the pleasing, sweet-scented flowers — mandārava, lotus, utpala and the rest — and, further, beautifully woven, pleasing garlands, flower strung to flower. Thus, to those [buddhas] appearing as Emanation Bodies free of attachment, [these flowers] take the form of fine mansions, canopies, pennants, parasols, victory-banners, mandalas and the like; and, stirred by the wind, the falling rain of flowers becomes an offering to the victors of the ten directions as well. To the Lords of Sages, who have become the supreme places or recipients of offering, [I offer] flowers pleasing in every way — by scent, shape, color and the rest: the divine flower mandārava, that is, the "mind-holder," and the great mandāra born of the wish-fulfilling tree; the lotus, that is, the mallikā or pārśika; the blue utpala; the jasmine flower; the campaka and the rest — all the sweet-scented flowers scattered as loose blossoms; and also with hanging-garlands beautifully woven from many kinds of flower — that is, beautifully arrayed in shape and color, pleasing and joy-producing. With these too I make offering.

##### Khenpo Kunga Wangchuk (Commentary on the Bodhicaryāvatāra)

To the Teacher, the Lord of Sages, supreme among all places of offering — the buddhas together with their host of bodhisattva heirs — with all those utterly beautiful, pleasing divine flowers, sweet in scent: mandārava, lotus, utpala, saffron-flower, kumuda and the rest; and, further, with garlands strung flower to flower on a thread, beautifully woven and pleasing by nature — with all of these too I make offering, chiefly upon the heads of those recipients of offering. Offering such flowers, one prays: "on the strength of this merit, may there arise in the beings of myself and others, in the future, the blossoming flower of the thirty-two excellent marks and the eighty fine signs."

Sources: [[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/Transcluded/BCAC20_NKW_bo_segmented.md#^2-15]]

<!-- div:divergences -->
##### ⚑ Divergences (where the commentaries differ)

- **gyaltsab / sabzang:** they number the incense-offering that follows the flower-offering (verse 2-16) as "the sixth," thereby counting this flower-offering as the fifth.
- **ngulchu-thogmed:** he numbers the incense-offering as "the seventh (incense)," counting each branch of the mind-emanated offering differently from the others.

The meaning does not conflict, but the commentaries divide and count the position of this flower-offering within the branches of the mind-emanated offering differently.

#### Metaphors and Examples

- **The flower-offering** (མེ་ཏོག་གི་མཆོད་པ) → the flowers of the seven branches of enlightenment blossoming in the mind-stream. — Kunzang Pelden makes the aspiration-prayer that, by the merit of the outer flower-offering, the flower-cluster of the seven branches of enlightenment may blossom in the mind-streams of oneself and all beings — setting the outer flower as an emblem for the inner branches of enlightenment.
- **The flower-offering** (མེ་ཏོག་གི་མཆོད་པ) → gaining the fully blossomed flower of the marks and signs. — Khenpo Kunga Wangchuk makes the aspiration-prayer that, by the merit of offering flowers, the fully blossomed flower of the thirty-two excellent marks and the eighty fine signs may arise in the future in the mind-streams of oneself and all beings.

<!-- sub:teaching-points -->
#### Main Teaching Points

###### 1. The Offering-Substance — All the Sweet-Scented Flowers Such as the Divine Mandārava, Lotus, and Utpala

As the root text and all the commentaries explain on a shared basis, all the kinds of sweet-scented flower pleasing to the supreme field of offering, the Lords of Sages — mandārava, lotus, utpala and the rest — are set forth as offering-substances.

###### 2. The Field of Offering — the Lords of Sages Who Have Become the Supreme Place of Offering, Together With Their Heirs

Khenpo Kunga Wangchuk sets out clearly that the field is the buddhas, supreme among all places of offering, together with their host of bodhisattva heirs.

###### 3. The Two Ways of Offering — Scattering as Loose Blossoms and Stringing Into Garlands

Many commentaries explain clearly the two ways of offering: scattering all the flowers as loose blossoms, and arranging them into beautifully woven garlands or hanging-garlands.

###### 4. The Detailed Account of the Kinds of Flower

Minyak Kunzang Sonam glosses the mandārava as the "mind-holder" and the great mandāra born of the wish-fulfilling tree, and the utpala as blue; and Khenpo Kunga Wangchuk adds saffron-flower and kumuda among the kinds of flower.

###### 5. Making an Aspiration-Prayer on the Fruit of the Offering

Kunzang Pelden prays that, by the merit of the flower-offering, the flowers of the seven branches of enlightenment may blossom in the mind-stream; and Khenpo Kunga Wangchuk prays that the fully blossomed flower of the marks and signs may be attained.

###### 6. The Explanation Linked to the Flourishing of the Faculties

Gyalwa Tenzin Gyatso explains that this flower-offering is offered chiefly for the flourishing of the faculty of the eye, and that after it the incense-offering is linked to the flourishing of the faculty of the nose.

<!-- sub:synthesis -->
#### Verse Synthesis (overview)

**Brief introduction.** This verse presents the flower-offering, within the mind-emanated offering: to the Lords of Sages, the supreme field of offering, together with their heirs, one offers all the sweet-scented flowers — the divine mandārava, lotus, utpala and the rest — scattered as loose blossoms and strung into beautifully woven garlands. All the commentaries explain it in agreement on the offering-substance and the manner of offering.

**Key points.**

- The offering-substance is all the kinds of sweet-scented flower — the divine mandārava, lotus, utpala and the rest.
- The field of offering is the Lords of Sages, supreme among all places of offering, together with their host of bodhisattva heirs.
- There are two ways of offering: scattering as loose blossoms, and stringing into beautifully woven garlands.
- Kunzang Pelden and Khenpo Kunga Wangchuk make aspiration-prayers on the fruit of the flower-offering: that the flowers of the seven branches of enlightenment, and the flower of the marks and signs, may blossom.
- ⚑ The commentaries divide and count the position of this flower-offering within the branches of the mind-emanated offering differently.
