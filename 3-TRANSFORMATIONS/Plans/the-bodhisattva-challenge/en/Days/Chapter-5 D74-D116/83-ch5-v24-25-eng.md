## Today's Verse

> Just as people afflicted by illness
> are powerless in all tasks,
> likewise, those whose minds are disturbed by delusion
> are powerless in all tasks. ^5-24

> Even hearing, contemplating, and cultivating
> with a mind that lacks awareness,
> like water in a leaky pot,
> it does not remain in memory. ^5-25

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the twenty-fourth and twenty-fifth verses of the fifth chapter. Here we can see a clear example: however much water we pour into a pot with a hole in its base, it does not stay, and simply leaks back out.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us an example that is easy to understand and encouraging.

If we pour water into a pot that has a hole in its base or side, in a short time the water leaks back out and does not remain. In the same way, however many good qualities a person without awareness gains from hearing, contemplating, and meditating, these will gradually be lost and forgotten.

Just as a sick person cannot do their work, without mindfulness and awareness, we have no power to do good deeds. So without awareness, the wisdom gained from hearing, contemplating, and meditating will not last. It will simply go to waste.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will look at the state of my own body and mind again and again. I will hold on to awareness and protect what I have learned from hearing and reflecting.

**Explanation:** _(Wisdom Practice)_ If we do not have the awareness to continually check what our own body and mind are doing, the good qualities and wisdom that come from hearing, contemplating, and meditating will go to waste, like water leaking from a cracked pot. So if we look at our own mind again and again with awareness and guard mindfulness without distraction, all our training and good qualities can remain firm and stable in the mind. This brings great benefit.
