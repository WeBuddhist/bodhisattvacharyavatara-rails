| 9 | 1-23 to 1-25 | Take one wish all the way: may you be completely free | **1-23** | Don't stop at "feel better." Push on. Free them. | Wish wide. Wish deep. Leave no one out. | Start the wish. Go further. Finish it. | Why does your kindness stop halfway? | Can you wish them all the way free? |

| 11 | 1-29 to 1-30 | Recall someone who helped you see clearly; feel grateful | **1-30** | One clear voice. Remember it. Say thanks. | Recall who woke you. Honor them. Be grateful. | Find the face. | 13 | 1-33 to 1-34 | Catch irritation or contempt early, before it builds; let it pass | **1-34** | Feel it rise. Starve it. Let it pass. | Catch the spark. Don't fan it. Release. | Spot the contempt. Hold steady. Let go. | Will you feed it or let it fade? | Can you catch it before it grows? |
| 14 | 1-35 to 1-36 | Bring to mind someone who meets unkindness with patience; admire them | **1-35** | Struck, yet calm. Look. Learn. | Find the patient one. Pause. Admire. | Harm met with grace. Notice it. Honor it. | Could you stay kind under attack? | Who answers harm without anger? |
