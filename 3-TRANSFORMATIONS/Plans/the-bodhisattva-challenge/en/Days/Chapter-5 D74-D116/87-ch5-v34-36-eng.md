## Today's Verse

> First, knowing that this mind
> is flawed in this way,
> at that time, I must remain
> as inert as a piece of wood. ^5-34

> I must never look
> aimlessly at distractions.
> With focused attention, I must always
> lower my gaze and look. ^5-35

> For the sake of resting my gaze,
> I may occasionally look in a direction.
> If someone appears in my sight,
> I should look and offer a welcome. ^5-36

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the thirty-fourth, thirty-fifth, and thirty-sixth verses of the fifth chapter. This teaching clearly shows that when an affliction arises in the mind, we must remain as steady as a great tree unmoved by the wind.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on this verse give us an example that is easy to understand and encouraging.

Before engaging in any action, we must examine what thought has arisen in our own mind. If a harmful or flawed thought has arisen, we must recognize that this thought will cause harm in both this life and future lives.

At that time, we should not act with the body or speak with the voice. We must not let the mind follow after that harmful thought. Just as the wind cannot shake or move a great tree, we must remain steady, keeping our body, speech, and mind from falling under the power of a harmful thought.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, before any action, I will examine my own mind. When an affliction arises, I will remain unmoved, like a tree.

**Explanation:** _(Patience Practice)_ It is very important to examine the intention behind our own mind before we do anything. If we see an affliction like anger or pride arising within us, we should not move the body or speak. We must remain steady, like a great tree unmoved by the wind. Taming the mind this way, without falling under the power of the afflictions, is the very heart of this verse of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. This brings the benefit of avoiding unwanted suffering for both ourselves and others, and of gaining great peace of mind.
