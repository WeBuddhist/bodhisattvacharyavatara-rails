# Audience Profile: Children's Version

## Audience

Young readers (roughly ages 8–12) with no Buddhist background at all.

---

## Translation Goal

Make the text easy to understand and follow for a child, while keeping its meaning and its seriousness.

---

## Style

* Simple
* Warm
* Short sentences

---

## Explanatory Additions

Minor clarification through wording is permitted.
Do not add information absent from the source.

---

## Priority

1. Understanding
2. Simplicity
3. Core meaning
4. Accuracy
