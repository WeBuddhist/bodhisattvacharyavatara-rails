# Termbase — Bodhisattva Challenge, English translation stream

Vocabulary contract for translation output in `Plans/the-bodhisattva-challenge/en/Days/`. One rendering per term.

Forked from the-bodhisattva-challenge English termbase, which governs *authored*
day content. This one governs *translation* of the Tibetan day file's three
prose sections, so it keeps that file's core spine and drops its authoring-only
rules (e.g. "never carry a bare proper name" — a translator carries whatever the
source carries).

**No diacritics anywhere in prose.** Shantideva, Manjushri, Samantabhadra.

Register target: CEFR A2–B1. One idea per sentence, active voice, under 20 words.

## Epithets and descriptive titles — render plainly, never transliterate

This is where "don't translate the Buddhist lingo" stops applying. Names stay;
descriptions get said in ordinary words.

| Tibetan | Write | Not |
|---|---|---|
| ས་བཅུའི་དབང་ཕྱུག་ / ས་བཅུའི་བྱང་སེམས་ཆེན་པོ་ | great bodhisattvas who have reached the highest stages | lords of the tenth bhumi |
| ཐུགས་རྗེ་ཆེན་པོ་ཅན་ | full of compassion | possessed of great compassion, mahakaruna |
| ཡོན་ཏན་རྒྱ་མཚོ་ | an ocean of good qualities | oceanic assembly of virtues |
| སྒོ་གསུམ་ | body, speech and mind | the three doors |
| ཡོན་གནས་དམ་པ་ | those most worthy of offerings | supreme objects of veneration, supreme field of merit |
| དུས་གསུམ་གཤེགས་པའི་སངས་རྒྱས་ | the buddhas of past, present and future | the buddhas of the three times |

## Attribution formulas — preserve exactly, never sharpen

| Tibetan | English |
|---|---|
| སློབ་དཔོན་ཞི་བ་ལྷས། | Master Shantideva (then "Shantideva") |
| འགྲེལ་བ་རྣམས་སུ་གསུངས་པ་ལྟར། | as the commentaries say |
| གསུངས་པ་ལྟར། | as it is taught |

Never resolve a vague attribution ("the commentaries") to a specific named
commentator, and never drop one because the sentence reads better without it.

## Avoid

| Do not write | Write instead |
|---|---|
| cyclic existence | samsara |
| the god realms | the heavens |
| sentient beings (in prose) | beings, people |
| negative karma / demerit | misdeeds, wrongdoing |
| deign to accept, supplicate, laud | accept, ask, praise |
| one is exhorted to | you are invited to, today we |
| the practitioner should | I will (in section ༦), you can (elsewhere) |
| emanate, manifest forth | imagine, picture |
| Bodhisattva Shantideva | Master Shantideva, then Shantideva |
