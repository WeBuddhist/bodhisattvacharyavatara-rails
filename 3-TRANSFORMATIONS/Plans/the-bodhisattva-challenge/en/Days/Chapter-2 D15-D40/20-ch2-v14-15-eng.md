## Today's Verse

> With the finest perfumes, whose scent spreads through the whole universe, I anoint the bodies of every buddha — bodies that shine like polished gold. ^2-14

> I offer the buddhas, who deserve the highest respect, beautiful flowers of every kind — soft blossoms, lotuses, and sweet-smelling garlands woven with care. ^2-15

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on verses 2-14 and 2-15 from the second chapter of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. It invites us to use the power of our imagination to offer supreme sweet-scented ointments that fill the entire universe, along with heavenly blossoms woven into beautiful garlands, to the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas. This practice helps us cultivate a generous and open heart through mental offerings.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

These verses explain two special types of mental offering: fragrant ointments and flowers. Master Shantideva imagines gathering the finest sweet scents—perfumes like pure sandalwood whose fragrance spreads across a billion-fold universe. He respectfully anoints the radiant bodies of the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas, which glow brightly like refined, polished gold. He then presents lovely blossoms from heavenly and earthly realms, including mandarava flowers, lotuses, and blue utpalas. He offers these sweet-scented flowers both scattered individually through the air and woven into artistic, delightful garlands.

Spiritual teachers explain that the awakened Buddhas do not need our perfumes or flowers. We make these mental offerings to cleanse the negative karma and mental pollution of ourselves and all living beings. By offering fragrant ointments with pure devotion, we plant the positive seeds for all beings to one day attain the radiant, golden complexion of a Buddha. By offering beautiful flowers and garlands, we plant the seeds for the seven spiritual qualities of enlightenment to bloom like lovely flowers within the hearts of all living beings.

## 3) Today's Practice

The Practice:
Today, whenever I see a beautiful flower or smell a sweet scent, I will mentally offer it to the Three Jewels to help clear away negative karma.

The Meaning (Generosity):
Our usual instinct is to grasp at beautiful things and keep them for ourselves. Today, instead of clinging to that beauty, I will imagine it multiplying to fill the universe and mentally offer it to the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas. This simple mental shift breaks our habit of selfish attachment, redirecting that positive joy to help spiritual qualities bloom in all living beings.
