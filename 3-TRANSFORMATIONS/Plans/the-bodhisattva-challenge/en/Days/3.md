# Day 3 — Bodhisattvacharyavatara Practice Plan

## Chapter 1: The Benefits of the Mind of Enlightenment — Verses 1-7 to 1-9

---

### 1. Refuge & Bodhicitta (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ་སེམས་བསྐྱེད།)

#### **1. Refuge (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ།)**

> *Until I reach the heart of awakening,*
> *I take refuge in the Buddhas.*
> *Likewise, I take refuge in the Dharma*
> *And in the assembly of Bodhisattvas.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྙིང་པོར་མཆིས་ཀྱི་བར། །
> སངས་རྒྱས་རྣམས་ལ་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །
> ཆོས་དང་བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་དཔའི། །
> ཚོགས་ལའང་དེ་བཞིན་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །

---

### 2. Benefit (ཕན་ཡོན།)

Based on the traditional commentaries of Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, Sazang Mati Panchen, and Ngulchu Thokme Zangpo, practicing and reflecting on today's verses yields the following profound benefits:

*   **The Wisest Discovery (Tested by the Buddhas):** The wish for enlightenment is not just a nice idea. The Buddhas spent many eons looking for the best way to help all beings. They all agreed that this wish is the most powerful tool of all.

*   **A Joyful Path to Happiness (No Need for Harsh Struggle):** With the wish for enlightenment, a person does not need to follow a painful or very difficult path. This wish brings countless beings to the highest happiness in a way that is smooth and even joyful. It moves from one kind of happiness to an even greater one.

*   **Instant Spiritual Transformation (Changed in a Moment):** The moment a person sincerely generates the wish for enlightenment, something real changes right away. Even someone who has made many mistakes becomes an heir of the Buddhas in that very instant. Gods and humans recognize this change and respect that person.

---

### 3. Today's Root Verses & Commentaries (དེ་རིང་གི་སྤྱོད་འཇུག་རྩ་ཚིག་དངོས།)

#### **Verse 1-7**

> བསྐལ་པ་དུ་མར་རབ་དགོངས་མཛད་པ་ཡི། །
> ཐུབ་དབང་རྣམས་ཀྱིས་འདི་ཉིད་ཕན་པར་གཟིགས། །
> འདིས་ནི་ཚད་མེད་སྐྱེ་བོའི་ཚོགས་རྣམས་ཀྱིས། །
> བདེ་མཆོག་བདེ་བླག་ཉིད་དུ་ཐོབ་པར་བྱེད། །
>
> *The Wise Sages contemplated this for many eons and saw that this mind alone is beneficial. Through it, countless multitudes of beings easily attain the highest happiness.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-7**

The great teacher Śāntideva tells us that the wish for enlightenment is not just a simple idea — it is the most important discovery of all the Buddhas, found only after searching for many, many eons. These great sages looked carefully at every method to help all living beings, and they all came to the same conclusion: the wish to awaken for the sake of others is the one true key. Through this wish, countless beings can reach the highest happiness easily and joyfully, without needing to go through extremely painful or harsh practices.

---

#### **Verse 1-8**

> སྲིད་པའི་སྡུག་བསྔལ་བརྒྱ་ཕྲག་གཞོམ་འདོད་ཅིང་། །
> སེམས་ཅན་མི་བདེ་བསལ་བར་འདོད་པ་དང་། །
> བདེ་མང་བརྒྱ་ཕྲག་སྤྱོད་པར་འདོད་པས་ཀྱང་། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་ཉིད་རྟག་ཏུ་གཏང་མི་བྱ། །
>
> *Do you wish to overcome the hundreds of sufferings of worldly existence? Do you wish to remove the unhappiness of sentient beings and enjoy hundreds of joys? If so, you must never abandon the mind of enlightenment.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-8**

This verse speaks directly to anyone who wants real happiness and who also cares about the suffering of others. The great teacher Śāntideva points out three deep wishes that many people feel: the wish to be free from their own suffering, the wish to help others stop hurting, and the wish to enjoy lasting happiness. The teaching is simple and clear — the only reliable way to fulfill all three of these wishes is to hold firmly to the wish for enlightenment and never let it go, because it is the one method that is certain to accomplish all of them.

---

#### **Verse 1-9**

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་སྐྱེས་གྱུར་ན་སྐད་ཅིག་གིས། །
> འཁོར་བའི་བཙོན་རར་བསྡམས་པའི་ཉམ་ཐག་རྣམས། །
> བདེ་གཤེགས་རྣམས་ཀྱི་སྲས་ཞེས་བརྗོད་བྱ་ཞིང་། །
> འཇིག་རྟེན་ལྷ་མིར་བཅས་པས་ཕྱག་བྱར་འགྱུར། །
>
> *The moment this mind is born, wretched beings bound in the prison of cyclic existence become heirs of the Blissful Ones. They also become worthy of respect by gods and humans alike.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-9**

The great teacher Śāntideva shows here that something remarkable happens the very instant a person sincerely generates the wish for enlightenment. Even someone who has been trapped for a long time in cycles of suffering, held tight by past mistakes and negative habits, is immediately given a new name and a new status — they become an heir of the Buddhas. This is not just a title; it is a real change in who that person is spiritually. Gods and humans recognize this transformation and offer respect, because a being who truly holds this wish is already on the highest path.

---

### 4. Daily Life Application (ཉིན་རེའི་འཚོ་བའི་ནང་ཉམས་སུ་ལེན་ཚུལ།)

*   **1. Trust the Wish for Enlightenment:** Today, when I feel worried or unsure, I will remember that the wish to help all beings is the most powerful thing I can hold in my mind. I will trust that this wish — not my own cleverness — is the best guide I have.

*   **2. Check My Three Wishes:** At some point today, I will quietly ask myself: Do I want to be free from my own suffering? Do I want others to stop hurting? Do I want real happiness? When I see that the answer is yes to all three, I will remind myself that the wish for enlightenment is my best response to each one.

*   **3. Remember That Change Happens Now:** When I feel like my mistakes are too big or that I am too far behind on the path, I will recall verse 1-9. I will remind myself that the moment I sincerely hold the wish to awaken for the sake of all beings, something real and good begins right away — not in the future, but now.

---

### 5. Dedication & Aspiration (བསྔོ་བ་དང་སྨོན་ལམ།)

#### **1. Dedication (བསྔོ་བ།)**

> *By the virtue accumulated*
> *Through composing this entry into the Bodhisattva conduct,*
> *May all wandering beings without exception*
> *Engage in the Bodhisattva conduct.*

> བདག་གིས་བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་པ་ལ། །
> འཇུག་པ་རྣམ་པར་བརྩམས་པ་ཡི། །
> དགེ་བ་གང་དེས་འགྲོ་བ་ཀུན། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་ལ་འཇུག་པར་ཤོག །

#### **2. Aspiration (སྨོན་ལམ།)**

> *May the precious and supreme mind of awakening,*
> *Where it has not arisen, arise;*
> *Where it has arisen, may it not decline,*
> *But increase further and further.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་མཆོག་རིན་པོ་ཆེ། །
> མ་སྐྱེས་པ་རྣམས་སྐྱེ་གྱུར་ཅིག །
> སྐྱེས་པ་ཉམས་པ་མེད་པ་དང་། །
> གོང་ནས་གོང་དུ་འཕེལ་བར་ཤོག །

### 6. Key Terms Glossary (གནད་ཚིག་ཁག་གི་འགྲེལ་བཤད།)

*   **Mind of Enlightenment (བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་):** This is the sincere wish to reach full awakening in order to help all living beings without exception. In verse 1-7, it is described as the supreme discovery of all the Buddhas, proven over many eons to be the most beneficial thing a person can develop. It is also called the most reliable path to real and lasting happiness.

*   **Cyclic Existence (འཁོར་བ་):** This refers to the endless cycle of birth, suffering, death, and rebirth that all beings experience when they are driven by past actions and mental habits. In verse 1-9, it is compared to a prison that holds beings tightly. The wish for enlightenment has the power to begin freeing a person from this cycle immediately.

*   **Heir of the Blissful Ones (བདེ་གཤེགས་སྲས་):** This is the name given to a person who has genuinely generated the wish for enlightenment. In verse 1-9, the great teacher Śāntideva explains that this title carries real meaning — it describes an actual change in the person's spiritual status, not just a kind word. Even gods offer respect to someone who holds this wish sincerely.

*   **Supreme Happiness (བདེ་མཆོག་):** In verse 1-7, this refers to the highest state of freedom and well-being — full enlightenment for the benefit of all beings. The commentaries explain that this happiness is reached easily and joyfully through the wish for enlightenment, without the need for painful or extreme practices.
