## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on verses 18 and 19 from the second chapter of the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_. It invites us to use the power of our imagination to offer boundless, jewel-filled palaces and beautiful golden-handled umbrellas (parasols) to the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas. This practice teaches us how to take an ordinary object from daily life—like an umbrella or shade—and transform it into a powerful practice of generosity and mental offering.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

These verses conclude the teachings on the twelve types of mental offering by focusing on celestial palaces and precious umbrellas. Master Shantideva imagines presenting immeasurable, jewel-built palaces where delightful songs of praise and music echo through the halls. These palaces are adorned with glowing pearl pendants and shining gemstones, filling the entire sky like a magnificent ornament. He joyfully offers all of this to the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas, who are the very embodiment of great compassion.

Next, recalling how the ancient gods Brahma and Indra once offered umbrellas to the Buddha, Shantideva mentally presents magnificent precious parasols with solid gold handles. These umbrellas are beautifully shaped and decorated around the edges with strings of pearls and jewels. He vows to continually offer these protective canopies to the awakened beings.

Spiritual teachers explain that offering these umbrellas carries a deep spiritual wish. By mentally offering parasols that protect from the burning sun, we plant the seeds of positive energy (merit) so that all living beings may be protected from the burning heat of worldly suffering and pain. We pray that through this gift, everyone may find safe, cool refuge under the protective shade of the Three Jewels (Buddha, Dharma, and Sangha).

## 3) Today's Practice

Actual Practice: Today, when I see an umbrella or shade, I will offer it in my mind to the Three Jewels as a special umbrella protecting everyone from pain.

Explanation: (Generosity Practice) Usually, when we use an umbrella or look for shade from the sun or rain, we only think about keeping ourselves comfortable. Today, following the teachings, when I see an umbrella or cool shade, I will picture it as a beautiful "special umbrella" with a gold handle and covered in jewels. I will happily offer it in my mind to the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas. As I make this offering, I will strongly wish that its cool shade protects me and all living beings from the hot fire of anger and pain, bringing everyone into the safe and peaceful care of the Three Jewels.
