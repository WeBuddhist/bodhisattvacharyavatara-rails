# Day 16 — Offering riches no one could ever own

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Offer something you could never own
> **Detail:** The offering continues. Today, picture something far beyond your reach and offer it in your mind.

## Opening

Yesterday's offering held the beauty of this world. Today it reaches things no one could ever own: wish-granting trees, forests of jewels, lakes of lotuses and singing swans from the god realms. Shantideva gathers them all in his mind and offers them.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

You can only give what you can get hold of. Or so it seems.

These verses offer wish-granting trees and forests of jewels, things found only in the god realms, far beyond anyone's reach. Ngulchu Thokme, a fourteenth-century teacher, explains how this works. The offering is made in the mind, so whatever you can picture, you can offer. Nothing here is yours, and nothing is out of reach. **In the mind, the rarest things in the world are yours to give.**

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Today, bring to mind something wonderful you will never own, a far-off mountain, a place you have only seen in pictures, a night sky full of stars. Picture it clearly, then offer it in your mind to the Buddha, the Dharma, and the Sangha.
