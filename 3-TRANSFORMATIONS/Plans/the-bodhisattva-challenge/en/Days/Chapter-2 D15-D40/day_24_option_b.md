# Day 24 — Offer what your hands could never hold

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Offer something too big to give — the whole sky
> **Detail:** Today follows Manjushri's offering. Give in your mind what you could never give in your hands.

## Opening

Today's offering looks to a model. Shantideva names Manjushri, a great bodhisattva, and others like him, who fill all of space with gifts for the buddhas. He cannot do what they do. So he offers in the one way open to him: he aspires to give exactly as they give, to the buddhas and their bodhisattva children.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

We picture a gift far past anything we own: the whole sky filled with offerings, the way the great bodhisattvas fill it. Then the honest thought comes. I cannot actually do that. Ngulchu Thokme, a fourteenth-century teacher, says this is the heart of the verse. The word he uses is **aspiration**. You cannot produce the offerings, so you picture them and you form the wish to give just as Manjushri gives. That wish is the offering. Its worth does not depend on what your hands can carry. The virtue gathers in the mind that means it, and it gathers for you.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Once today, pick something you could never actually hand over: the open sky, the sea, a mountain you love. Offer it in your mind to the buddhas, the dharma, and the sangha, and add the simple wish to give the way the great bodhisattvas give. You hold nothing back and you lose nothing.
