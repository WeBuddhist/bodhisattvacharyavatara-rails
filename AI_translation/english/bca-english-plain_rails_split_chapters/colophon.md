## Colophon ^a-0

The Engaging in Bodhisattva Conduct, composed by the master Shantideva, is complete. ^a-1

## Colophon ^b-0

From the finalization of the translation by the Indian master Sarvajñādeva and the chief revisor and translator, the monk Bande Peltsék, based on a Kashmiri manuscript: ^b-1

Later, the Indian master Dharmashrībhadra, and the chief revisor and translator, the monk Rinchen Zangpo, and Shakya Lodrö, corrected and revised it in accordance with manuscripts and commentaries from central India, and then finalized it. ^b-2

Again, at a later time, the Indian master Sumatikīrti and the chief revisor and translator, the monk Lodën Sherab, corrected and revised it, and perfectly established it. ^b-3
