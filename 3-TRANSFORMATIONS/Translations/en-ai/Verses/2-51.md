## Combined Commentary Summaries

All three commentators read this stanza as a continuation of the desperate refuge-taking begun in verse 50: after appealing to Samantabhadra, Mañjughoṣa, and Avalokiteśvara, the speaker now cries out to the other great bodhisattva protectors — Ākāśagarbha (Essence of Space), Kṣitigarbha (Essence of the Earth), Maitreya, and Sarvanivaraṇaviṣkambhin (Remover of All Obstacles) — out of terror at the sufferings of the lower realms.

Gyaltsab Je and Sazang Mati Panchen both emphasize that this refuge is not a formal, distant supplication but an anguished cry "from the depths of the heart," calling out the bodhisattvas' names one by one. Ngulchu Thokme treats this verse as part of a single extended appeal begun with Samantabhadra and Mañjughoṣa in the preceding verse, underscoring that the entire passage (vv. 50–52) forms one continuous outpouring of fear-driven refuge in the great bodhisattvas before turning, in the next verse, to Vajrapāṇi.
