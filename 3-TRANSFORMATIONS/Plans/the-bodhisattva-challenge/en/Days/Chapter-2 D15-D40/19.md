# Day 19 — The ones who dress up for our sake

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Make your manner easy for someone today, for their sake
> **Detail:** The great bodhisattvas take an approachable form to reach people. Today, make yourself easy to come to.

## Opening

Shantideva has bathed the buddhas and offered them robes. Now he adorns the great noble ones, Samantabhadra, Manjushri, Avalokiteshvara, and the rest, with soft cloth and hundreds of fine ornaments. These are beings who have already seen the truth. They have no need of jewels. Yet they appear in bright, ordinary, princely form anyway, and the verse meets them as they are.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

We often think the spiritual goal is to need nothing and to show it.

The verse adorns the great bodhisattvas with soft cloth and fine ornaments. Ngulchu Thokme, a fourteenth-century teacher, points out something easy to miss. These beings have directly seen the truth; they need no ornament at all. They wear the bright, approachable form of a layperson on purpose, so that ordinary people will feel able to come near and be taught. The jewels are not for them. They are a kindness aimed at the people they want to reach. So when you adorn them in your mind, you are honouring a beauty that was only ever turned outward, toward us.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

With one person today, make yourself easy to come to, for their sake and not your own. A warmer tone, an unhurried face, putting your phone down so they have your attention. Let how you show up be a small kindness to them.
