# Day 28 — We don't have to wait to see it pass

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Recall someone already gone, and loosen one tie
> **Detail:** Much of what we cling to is already passing. Today, let that ease one grip.

## Opening

It is easy to hear "everything passes" as something far off. Today's three verses bring it closer. Death keeps no schedule and waits for no one. We will leave it all behind and go on alone, and the people we favoured and the people we fought, like us, all come to an end.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

The verse can sound like a warning about the far future: someday everyone dies. **Sazang Mati Panchen**, a fourteenth-century teacher, says we do not have to wait that long to test it. Look back over this one life. Many people we loved, and many we could not stand, have already passed out of it, and what we shared with them is now only a memory, like waking from a dream. The proof is behind us, not only ahead. So the favours and the grudges we pile up, and the wrongs we do for them, are aimed at ties that are already dissolving. He asks us to let that settle before we act on the next one.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Bring to mind one person who has already left your life, through distance, a falling-out, or death. Notice how solid that bond once felt, and how it is now memory. Let that soften your grip on one thing you are clinging to, or fighting over, today.
