## Today's Verse

> To the lowly, do not teach the profound and vast teachings,
> nor to women without men present.
> One should always act with equal respect
> towards the teachings of the lesser and the supreme. ^5-89

> One should not apply the vast teachings,
> which are a vessel for the Dharma, to lesser teachings.
> One should not completely abandon conduct.
> One should not be misled by sutras and mantras. ^5-90

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the eighty-ninth and ninetieth verses of the fifth chapter. We do not hope only for a quick shortcut. We train our own mind to become a good vessel for the Dharma.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable quotation, which is easy to understand and encouraging. The _Heap of Jewels Sutra_ says as follows. It is a mistake of a bodhisattva to teach the vast Dharma of the Buddha to beings who have not become vessels. It is also a mistake of a bodhisattva to teach the lesser vehicle to beings who have deep confidence in the vast Dharma. Therefore, we must teach each person the Dharma that suits them. We do not teach the vast Dharma to those who are not vessels. We do not teach the lesser vehicle to those who have deep confidence in the vast Dharma.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, whether a Dharma teaching is lesser or higher, I will listen with great respect, making no difference between them.

**Explanation:** _(Taming the mind)_ If we show equal respect to all the Buddha's Dharma, our pride becomes calm and our good actions increase. By making no difference between lesser and higher Dharma, we tame our own mind, as the _Bodhicaryāvatāra_ intends.
