# Day 10 — Bodhisattvacharyavatara Practice Plan

## Chapter 1: Benefits of Bodhicitta — Verses 1-28 to 1-30

---

### 1. Refuge & Bodhicitta (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ་སེམས་བསྐྱེད།)

#### **1. Refuge (སྐྱབས་འགྲོ།)**

> *Until I reach the heart of awakening,*
> *I take refuge in the Buddhas.*
> *Likewise, I take refuge in the Dharma*
> *And in the assembly of Bodhisattvas.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྙིང་པོར་མཆིས་ཀྱི་བར། །
> སངས་རྒྱས་རྣམས་ལ་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །
> ཆོས་དང་བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་དཔའི་ཡི། །
> ཚོགས་ལའང་དེ་བཞིན་སྐྱབས་སུ་མཆི། །

---

### 2. Benefit (ཕན་ཡོན།)

Based on the traditional commentaries of Gyaltsab Darma Rinchen, Sazang Mati Panchen, and Ngulchu Thokme Zangpo, practicing and reflecting on today's verses yields the following profound benefits:

*   **Seeing Our Mistakes Clearly (Self-Awareness):** People often want to be happy, but they do things that bring more pain. Studying these verses helps you see this mistake in yourself. This kind of honest awareness is the first step to real change.

*   **Finding True Help (Trustworthy Guidance):** The wish to help all beings — with love, compassion, and wisdom — is the best quality a person can develop. It acts like a wise and caring friend. It shows you what to do and what to avoid in life.

*   **Building Powerful Good Qualities (Merit):** The wish to benefit all beings is greater than any other good action. This is not just a nice idea. It brings real inner strength and positive change to your daily life.

---

### 3. Today's Root Verses & Commentaries (དེ་རིང་གི་སྤྱོད་འཇུག་རྩ་ཚིག་དངོས།)

#### **Verse 1-28**

> སྡུག་བསྔལ་འདོར་འདོད་སེམས་ཡོད་ཀྱང་། །
> སྡུག་བསྔལ་ཉིད་ལ་མངོན་པར་རྒྱུག །
> བདེ་བ་འདོད་ཀྱང་གཏི་མུག་པས། །
> རང་གི་བདེ་བ་དགྲ་ལྟར་འཇོམས། ། ^1-28
>
> *Although beings wish to abandon suffering, they run directly into suffering itself. Although they desire happiness, out of ignorance they destroy their own happiness like an enemy.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-28**

This verse describes a painful truth about ordinary life: people want to be happy and free from pain, but they keep doing things that bring more suffering. This happens because they do not know which actions lead to happiness and which lead to harm. Like a moth flying into a flame, they seek relief but cause themselves more damage. The wish to help all beings is the only force that can fix this problem, because it brings love, compassion, and the wisdom to show people the right path.

---

#### **Verse 1-29**

> གང་ཞིག་བདེ་བས་ཕོངས་པ་དང་། །
> སྡུག་བསྔལ་མང་ལྡན་དེ་དག་ལ། །
> བདེ་བ་ཀུན་གྱིས་ཚིམ་པ་དང་། །
> སྡུག་བསྔལ་ཐམས་ཅད་གཅོད་བྱེད་ཅིང་། ། ^1-29
>
> *This mind satisfies those deprived of happiness with every joy. It also cuts away all their suffering.*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-29**

This verse explains what the wish to help all beings can actually do for those who are suffering. People who lack happiness and are full of pain can be given true joy through great love. All of their suffering can be removed through great compassion. This is not a small or ordinary kind of help — it addresses every kind of pain and brings every kind of happiness. No other good action in the world has this kind of power to benefit others so completely.

---

#### **Verse 1-30**

> གཏི་མུག་ཀྱང་ནི་སེལ་བྱེད་པ། །
> དེ་དང་དགེ་མཚུངས་ག་ལ་ཡོད། །
> དེ་འདྲའི་བཤེས་ཀྱང་ག་ལ་ཡོད། །
> བསོད་ནམས་དེ་འདྲའང་ག་ལ་ཡོད། ། ^1-30
>
> *It also dispels their ignorance. Where is there a virtue equal to this? Where is there such a spiritual friend, and where is there such merit?*

##### **Combined Commentary Summary for Verse 1-30**

This verse asks three simple but powerful questions: Where is there a better good action? Where is there a better friend? Where is there better merit? The answer to all three is: nowhere. The wish to help all beings removes ignorance by showing people what to do and what to avoid. It is the best kind of friendship, because it protects others from harm and guides them to happiness. The positive energy this wish creates is greater than that of any other action.

### 4. Daily Life Application (ཉིན་རེའི་འཚོ་བའི་ནང་ཉམས་སུ་ལེན་ཚུལ།)

*   **1. Noticing My Own Confusion:** Today, when I feel frustrated or unhappy, I will stop and ask myself: "Am I doing something that actually makes things worse?" I will try to see honestly when my own actions are causing my pain.

*   **2. Wishing Others to Be Happy:** When I meet someone who is struggling, I will take a moment to think: "I wish this person had happiness and was free from pain." I will let this feeling of care grow, even for people I find difficult.

*   **3. Remembering the Value of This Wish:** Each time I study or reflect today, I will remind myself that the wish to benefit all beings is more valuable than any other good action. I will try to keep this wish alive in my heart throughout the day.

### 5. Dedication & Aspiration (བསྔོ་བ་དང་སྨོན་ལམ།)

#### **1. Dedication (བསྔོ་བ།)**

> *By the virtue accumulated*
> *Through composing this entry into the Bodhisattva conduct,*
> *May all wandering beings without exception*
> *Engage in the Bodhisattva conduct.*

> བདག་གིས་བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་པ་ལ། །
> འཇུག་པ་རྣམར་བར་བརྩམས་པ་ཡི། །
> དགེ་བ་གང་དེས་འགྲོ་བ་ཀུན། །
> བྱང་ཆུབ་སྤྱོད་ལ་འཇུག་པར་ཤོག །

#### **2. Aspiration (སྨོན་ལམ།)**

> *May the precious and supreme mind of awakening,*
> *Where it has not arisen, arise;*
> *Where it has arisen, may it not decline,*
> *But increase further and further.*

> བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་མཆོག་རིན་པོ་ཆེ། །
> མ་སྐྱེས་པ་རྣམས་སྐྱེ་གྱུར་ཅིག །
> སྐྱེས་པ་ཉམས་པ་མེད་པ་དང་། །
> གོང་ནས་གོང་དུ་འཕེལ་བར་ཤོག །

### 6. Key Terms Glossary (གནད་ཚིག་ཁག་གི་འགྲེལ་བཤད།)

*   **Ignorance (གཏི་མུག):** This word means not knowing the difference between what helps and what harms. In these verses, it describes why people keep hurting themselves even when they want to be happy. It is like walking in the dark without a light.

*   **The Wish to Help All Beings (བྱང་ཆུབ་ཀྱི་སེམས།):** This is the wish to lead all living beings to complete happiness and freedom from suffering. The great teacher Śāntideva shows in these verses that this wish is the most powerful good quality a person can develop.

*   **Great Love (བྱམས་པ་ཆེན་པོ།):** This is the wish for all beings to have happiness and its causes. In Verse 1-29, it is described as the force that can truly satisfy those who are deprived and suffering. It goes beyond ordinary kindness.

*   **Great Compassion (སྙིང་རྗེ་ཆེན་པོ།):** This is the wish for all beings to be free from suffering and its causes. The commentaries explain that this powerful compassion can cut through every kind of pain that living beings experience.

*   **Spiritual Friend (བཤེས་གཉེན།):** In Verse 1-30, the great teacher Śāntideva asks where such a friend can be found. A spiritual friend is someone who truly guides you toward what is good and protects you from harm. The wish to help all beings is the highest form of this friendship.
