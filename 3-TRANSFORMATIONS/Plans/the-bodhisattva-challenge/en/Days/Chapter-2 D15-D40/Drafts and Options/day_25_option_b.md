# Day 25 — Refuge is more than a rescue

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Say the refuge prayer slowly today, and mean it
> **Detail:** Today's verses turn to refuge: not a quick rescue, but entrusting yourself for the long road.

## Opening

Today's verses are about who you turn to, and why. Shantideva first bows to the teachers and sacred things that carry the teaching. Then he takes refuge in the buddhas, the dharma, and the sangha, all the way until awakening. Then he joins his palms before the buddhas and bodhisattvas of every direction and begins to ask. The middle act, refuge, is the one the commentary opens up most.

## Renewing the Bodhisattva Vow

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

The bow comes first, to the images and scriptures that carry the teaching, the stupas, and the teachers who passed it on. Then refuge, and here Ngulchu Thokme, a fourteenth-century teacher, draws a clear line. Many people reach for a higher power only when they are frightened and want a quick way out. Refuge in the Three Jewels is not that. It starts from compassion, from wanting to become someone who can actually help every being, and it lasts until awakening, not just until the fear has passed. He adds that refuge is something you then live: you keep faith with the Three Jewels, you try to harm no one, and you keep good company. Last, Shantideva joins his palms before the buddhas and bodhisattvas who hold great compassion. Ngulchu reads this as the opening of confession. You turn to those who will not look away, and that is what makes it safe to admit what you have done and let it be cleaned.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Once today, say the refuge lines slowly, and mean them as you go: until reaching the heart of buddhahood, I take refuge in the Buddha, the dharma, and the sangha. Not words to get through, but a real handing over: I rely on you, please hold me. Notice what it is like to lean your weight on something for a moment.
