# Day 13 — A moment of admiration matters more than you think

## Opening

Think of someone you deeply respect. A teacher, maybe. Or someone whose kindness you still remember.

For a moment, you feel it. **Real respect.**

Today's verses show **two feelings** we can have toward such a person. And what each one does to us.

## From the Tradition

Today's verses compare two thoughts you can have toward a bodhisattva:

- **an angry one**
- **a warm, trusting one**

You might think they are equal. They are not. **The warm one matters far more.**

**Ngulchu Thokme** gives an example. Imagine someone makes every blind person in the world see again. A vast kindness. Now imagine someone else, who does nothing so great. They simply look at a **bodhisattva** with real trust and respect. That quiet look, Thokme says, brings **far more merit** than making all those people see.

Why does this matter so much? Because a bodhisattva works to free every being. So a single moment of faith in them brings enormous merit.

Remember this today: **a warm, trusting thought matters more than you think.**

## Today's Practice Challenge

**Your task today:**

Think of one person whose goodness you respect. A teacher, a friend, anyone kind.

For a few seconds, **let yourself truly admire them.**

Feel that respect, and do not hurry away from it.
