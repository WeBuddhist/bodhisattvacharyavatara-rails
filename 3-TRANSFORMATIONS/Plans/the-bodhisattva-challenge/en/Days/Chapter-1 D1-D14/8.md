# Day 8 — Even wishing to cure one headache counts

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Wish one good thing for someone struggling
> **Detail:** Even the smallest wish to help carries great merit. Today, make one clear wish for someone.

## Opening

Today's verses start with a worry many people feel: the path looks impossibly large, so why even begin? Shantideva answers with a small example. Even wishing to cure one person's headache is worth more than you would think. And if a wish that small matters, then the wish to free every being from all pain is beyond measuring.

## From the Tradition

**Sazang Mati Panchen**, a fourteenth-century teacher, tells the story behind this verse. A woman named Maitrakanya had badly hurt her mother, and after death she found herself in a place of torment, a burning wheel turning on her head. In the middle of that pain, one thought rose in her: may everyone who would suffer like this have it come to me instead.

The moment she thought it, the wheel lifted and she was free. Sazang's point is plain. If a single kind thought can do that in the worst place imaginable, there is no limit to what it can do here.

> May all living beings come to engage

## Today's Practice

Think of one person who is going through something hard right now. Once, as clearly as you can, wish for them: may you be free of this. However it comes out is fine. The wish is what counts.
