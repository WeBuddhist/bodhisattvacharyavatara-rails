# Day 27 — What you did the harm for will be gone

> **Notification**
> **Title:** Name one wrong you did for someone's sake
> **Detail:** Today's verses confess the harm we have done, and the commentary shows what is left of it after everyone is gone.

## Opening

Much of the harm we do, we do for someone, to help a person we love or to get back at one we dislike. Today Shantideva confesses the harm done to the Three Jewels, his parents and his teachers, lays the whole mass of it before the buddhas and bodhisattvas, and asks to be rescued before death.

> **Mind Training** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> May all beings enjoy happiness and the causes of happiness.
> May all beings be free from suffering and the causes of suffering.
> May all beings never be separated from supreme happiness, free from suffering.
> May all beings rest in great equanimity, free from attachment and aversion to those near and far.
>
> **Refuge** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Until reaching the heart of buddhahood,
> I take refuge in the Buddha,
> I take refuge in the dharma,
> And in the sangha of bodhisattvas.
>
> **Taking the Bodhisattva Vow** *(recite 3 times)*
>
> Just as the buddhas of the past gave rise to bodhicitta
> And engaged step by step in the training of the bodhisattvas,
> So I too, for the benefit of all beings, will give rise to bodhicitta
> And engage step by step in that same training.

## From the Tradition

Much of the harm we cause, we cause for someone: to protect a friend, to get back at a person we dislike. Ngulchu Thokme, a fourteenth-century teacher, follows that harm all the way to its end. The friend you protected will be gone. The person you struck at will be gone. You yourself will be gone too. What does not disappear is the mark the misdeed leaves on your mind, and that mark waits in front of you like something still owed. He compares the good things you gained to a dream: real while you sleep, nowhere to be found once you wake. So the harm was done for people who cannot take any of its result from you. You alone carry it, and that is why all of it goes into the confession.

## Aspiration and Dedication

> May the precious bodhicitta
> Arise in those who don't have it yet;
> And in those who already have it,
> May it not decline, but grow ever further.
>
> By the merit of practicing
> The Bodhicharyavatara today
> May all living beings come to engage
> In the conduct of the bodhisattvas.

## Today's Practice

Bring to mind one wrong you did for someone else's sake. The person it was for cannot take any of it from you now. Confess that one thing to the buddhas and bodhisattvas, plainly, without dressing it up.
